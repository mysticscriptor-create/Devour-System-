## ⬜ Dormant — 3×–5×

A person with a Core that hasn't awakened. Their body passively absorbs the Core's baseline output, making them stronger than any trained human without knowing why.

**Physical**
- Lifting: 240–400 kg. Feel naturally strong without trying.
- Speed: 30–45 km/h sustained sprint. Don't get winded like others.
- Durability: Bruise more slowly. Recover faster. Bones harder than average.
- Vision: Slightly sharper. Nothing dramatic.
- Lifespan: 100–120 years.

**What they don't know:** Their body is already different. They just think they're naturally gifted.

**Social role**
Dormants are discovered through ICA arc-scale screening, which is mandatory in most countries for children aged 8–14. Those identified are offered cultivation pathways, military recruitment, or labor contracts. Many Dormants who never awaken still earn more than baseline humans — construction, law enforcement, manual labor industries specifically recruit them. A Dormant can out-lift any crane operator, work sixteen hours without meaningful fatigue, and take a beating that would hospitalize a normal person.

They are paid better than normal workers. They are treated as less than Corebearers. This is the source of significant cultural friction.

## The Five Visible Stats

Every ICA arc-scale scan produces a standardized stat sheet showing rank-tiers, not numbers. This is deliberate — numbers invite arguments. Rank-tiers invite policy.

```
─────────────────────────────

```

---

### Power
Raw force output. Covers strength, striking force, shockwave generation, energy projection damage. **Cannot exceed Vitality by more than one tier** without causing structural damage to the body.

### Speed
Kinetic capability. Covers movement speed, reaction time, combat reflexes, evasion. **Effectiveness is capped by Perception** — you can't move faster than you can process information.

### Vitality
Structural durability. Covers physical and energy resistance, recovery rate, stamina, regeneration. The "body integrity" stat. **Power and Recovery cannot outscale Vitality.**

### Perception
Sensory and cognitive superiority. Covers awareness, instinct, combat intuition, sensory range, threat detection. Secretly governs effective reaction time — high Speed with low Perception means fast movement that can't react accurately.

### Energy
Internal core output. Covers total energy pool, aura strength, ability activation, sustained technique duration, burst potential. The fuel tank. **Cannot exceed Core Stability.**

### Core Stability (Hidden)
The ICA sees this. The Corebearer doesn't. Covers overload resistance, breakthrough success probability, backlash resistance, energy circulation efficiency. A Corebearer with low Core Stability and high Energy is dangerous to themselves. The ICA uses this number to quietly decide who gets supervised breakthrough facilities and who gets placed on a watchlist.

# Section 4 — The Essence Cycle
*Why killing dungeon creatures works — and why nothing else does*

---

## 4.1 — What Happens When You Kill a Dungeon Creature

When a Corebearer kills a dungeon creature, something happens that no ICA instrument has fully measured.

The creature dies. Its body disperses. But in the seconds between death and dispersal, its stored essence — the dimensional energy that powered it — does not scatter. It is drawn toward the nearest active Core. Like water finding the lowest point.

The ICA calls this **Essence Absorption.** They have documented it for eight hundred years. They have not once produced a complete explanation.

The reason: the mechanism isn't biological. It isn't chemical. It operates at the dimensional level — the dungeon's Core architecture is actively directing absorbed essence into the Corebearer, because the dungeon's reward structure is deliberately engineered. *(See: Cosmic Map — Part 12)*

**What Corebearers feel:** A faint warmth spreading from the chest immediately after a kill. A subtle clarity. A sense of being slightly more *present* than a moment before. Experienced runners call it the Rush. New Corebearers think they're imagining it.

They're not imagining it. Their Core just absorbed raw soul-adjacent energy from a being that came from across the cosmos.

---

## 4.2 — Why Non-Dungeon Creatures Give Nothing

Outside the dungeon, creatures don't carry the same dimensional essence architecture. Wild animals, surface predators, even dungeon creatures that escape — killing them produces nothing. Their deaths don't generate the absorption signal because they are not connected to the dungeon Core's reward distribution system.

Some Corebearers have killed exceptional surface wildlife and felt nothing. Some believed they were doing it wrong. None figured out why.

**The reason:** the essence in dungeon creatures is not natural. It was put there. Each creature in a dungeon is a being from across the cosmos, carrying dimensional energy from wherever it came from. The dungeon Core manages the distribution. Outside the Core's range, there is nothing to distribute.

**One exception — eating dungeon meat:** Consuming dungeon creature flesh provides a very small fraction of the essence that killing it would. The energy is bound in the tissue rather than the dimensional architecture. The body extracts it through digestion.

| Rank | Value of eating dungeon meat |
|------|---------------------------|
| F–E | Meaningful supplement — worth doing |
| D | Noticeable but not a replacement for killing |
| C | Minor boost — roughly equal to killing one F-rank creature |
| B+ | Negligible — like trying to fill a barrel with thimblefuls |

---

## 4.3 — The Refinement Process (What Sleep Is Actually Doing)

After a dungeon run, every Corebearer is exhausted — not just physically. Energetically. The muscles ache, yes. But the deeper exhaustion is the Core running at capacity trying to process what it absorbed.

When the Corebearer rests deeply, the Core enters **Passive Refinement Mode:**

1. The outer "noise" of the creature's origin — its alien nature, its corruption, its history — is stripped away
2. The underlying energy is converted into pure Soul Essence — the form that Corebearer development runs on
3. This refined essence distributes through the Core's channels into the body, contributing to breakthrough progress

The process takes **4–8 hours** of genuine rest. The Corebearer wakes up slightly stronger than they went to sleep. Not noticeably — the increments are tiny. But real, and they compound.

> **What the Corebearer doesn't know:** the Devour System reads the "noise" the Core strips away — every creature's origin data, where it came from, what it was before corruption — and records it before the refinement discards it. This is what makes Marc qualitatively different from every Corebearer who has run dungeons before him.

### The Essence Split Problem

When a group kills a higher-rank creature, essence doesn't divide equally. It flows primarily to whoever dealt the killing blow, with diminishing shares to meaningful contributors. This creates dungeon team politics — who gets the kill strike matters enormously.

Some teams rotate kill priority deliberately. Others use contracts to specify distribution. The least experienced teams dissolve over it.

## 5.4 — What "Near S-Rank" Means in Practice

An S-rank creature appearing in a Tier-3 dungeon is not a dungeon-clearing target. It is an **emergency event.**

The nation housing the dungeon notifies its allies. The ICA escalates to multi-national response. The dungeon is quarantined. A response team of 30–80 A-rank Corebearers is assembled, briefed, and deployed.

Not all of them come back.

This is not fiction. This is the documented operational history of every S-rank creature encounter in Earth's Corebearer record.

# Section 6 — The Food Transition
*What Corebearers eat — and how it changes*

---

| Rank | Dietary Reality |
|------|----------------|
| Dormant | Normal human diet. Slightly elevated appetite. High protein cravings but no special requirements. |
| F-rank | 3–5× normal calories. Standard food works but not efficiently. Begin noticing dungeon-sourced meat tastes richer, more satisfying. Minor essence boost from dungeon meat (~5% of killing the creature). |
| E-rank | 8–12× calories. Regular food feels like eating without getting full. Dungeon creature meat provides noticeable satisfaction and minor sustained essence boost. Dungeon-grown plants provide nutrition that normal plants cannot match. |
| D-rank | Normal food barely satisfies — tastes bland. Body now requires dungeon-grade nutrition: creature meat, essence-infused supplements, or dungeon-grown produce. Without dungeon food: functional but perpetually hungry, refinement rate drops 20–30%. Small creature cores can be mixed into food for mild boost. |
| C-rank | Normal food provides essentially no value. Tastes like ash. Texturally detectable but the body registers zero nutritional signal. Requires high-grade dungeon creature meat and Tier-2+ dungeon flora exclusively. This is where the food economy becomes expensive — C-rank Corebearers without institutional support spend significant income on food. |
| B-rank | Only high-grade dungeon resources satisfy. Core fragments from C-rank creatures are now food — directly consumable, more essence than meat from the same source. Lower-rank cores provide negligible value at this point. |
| A-rank | Only elite dungeon creatures provide meaningful nutrition. Beginning to passively draw ambient essence from the environment during rest. |
| S-rank | Must consume high-rank creatures regularly. Energy starvation is real — deprivation causes the body to pull ambient essence from surroundings destructively. A hungry S-rank near civilians is dangerous without active suppression. |
| SS+ | Food is psychological, not biological. Body sustains itself on ambient essence absorption and Core output recycling. Eating is optional and nostalgic, not necessary. |

### The Dungeon Food Industry

By D-rank, Corebearers' dietary requirements have created an entire economic sector. ICA-licensed dungeon farming operations run specific Tier-1 and Tier-2 dungeons for food yield rather than essence yield.

The market tiers:
- **Mass market:** Processed dungeon meat supplements for F and E-ranks
- **Mid-tier:** Fresh dungeon creature meat for D and C-ranks
- **Premium:** Living or freshly killed high-rank creatures, private distribution, B and above
- **Black market:** Illegally obtained A-rank+ creature cores — primary reason many D-rank Corebearers accept mid-realm binding contracts

> A D-rank Corebearer's food budget can exceed their housing budget. This is one of the main reasons many accept contracts from sources they don't fully understand.

## Reproduction and Bloodlines

The Core affects hormonal output, vitality drive, and biological fertility in measurable ways.

- **F-rank:** Moderate elevation over baseline. Still essentially human.
- **D-rank:** Biological drives significantly stronger but so is control. The instinct is louder; so is the volume control.
- **B-rank and above:** Reproduction with baseline humans carries risk to the baseline partner.
- **A-rank and above:** Reproduction with someone more than one full rank below becomes biologically complicated.

Children of two high-rank Corebearers have significantly elevated Core Potential at awakening. This is the primary driver of bloodline management among elite Corebearer families — and the primary reason certain ICA-adjacent factions quietly track Corebearer relationship histories.

# Section 8 — What Makes Marc Different
*The Devour System vs standard progression*

---

## What the System Adds

Every Corebearer progresses the same way: kill dungeon creatures, absorb essence, sleep to refine, accumulate toward breakthrough. The Core handles everything automatically. The Corebearer doesn't understand the mechanism.

Marc's Core handles everything automatically too. The difference is the System.

| Feature | Standard Corebearer | Marc (Devour System) |
|---------|--------------------|--------------------|
| Essence absorption | Passive. Core pulls automatically at death. | Active. System assists Core during the kill. Conversion begins earlier. |
| Origin data | Discarded during refinement. | Read by the System before discard. Stored in archive. |
| Trait integration | Cannot acquire creature traits directly. | Can be offered direct trait integration — bypasses years of organic development. |
| Compatibility | Conflicting traits destroy each other. | World Core Shard makes incompatible combinations viable. |
| Essence efficiency | Baseline. | ~10–15% more refinable essence per kill at early rank. Compounds dramatically over time. |

## What the System Does NOT Add

- Extra daily kill capacity (refinement limit is the same)
- Immunity to exhaustion (Marc still needs rest)
- Instant rank progression (essence requirements are the same, just more efficiently acquired)
- Safety from higher-rank creatures (Marc can be killed like any other F-rank)
