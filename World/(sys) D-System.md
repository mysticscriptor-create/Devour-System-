## THE TWO FORMS
### *The Core of Everything*

Marc awakens in his **Monster Form.** That is the first thing the system registers. That is the first thing it shows.

This is the only system in existence that opens with the user's secondary state as the primary one.

```

```

Not Human. Not [Sealed]. Not [Secondary].

*Active.*

The monster form is not a transformation Marc chose. It is what the devouring of the heart produced — the Devourer's soul imprint meeting Marc's body and expressing itself immediately at full physicality. Seven feet. Dense black hair. Obsidian claws. Jaw structure that no human face should have. A body that feels like it belongs to something else.

He will eventually discover the human form is still there, still accessible, still *him.* But accessing it is not explained. The system does not offer a button. He has to figure it out — and figuring it out is tied to something deeper than mechanics.

---

### Human Form

Marc's natural state. The body he grew up in, now fundamentally altered by the awakening but still recognizably his.

In human form, Marc can integrate:

- Mental traits
- Sensory traits
- Energy-type traits
- Bloodline traits
- Awakened abilities
- Core traits

Physical trait integrations still happen beneath the surface in human form — denser bones, altered nerve conduction, restructured musculature — but nothing visually dramatic. He looks human. Mostly. At high enough resonance counts on physical traits, the alterations start becoming harder to fully hide.

---

### Monster Form

The full expression. Every physical trait Marc has absorbed manifests here without restriction. This is where scales grow. Where wings unfold. Where claws reach their full length and true structure. Where the accumulated creature biology he has been collecting expresses itself without apology.

Monster form also carries a passive that human form doesn't:

**Origin Pressure** — ambient creatures within a radius determined by Marc's rank register him as a predator above their tier. Not through any active ability. Simply through what he *is* in this form. Lower creatures avoid him. Prey-category creatures freeze. Predator-category creatures either challenge him or flee depending on the gap between their strength and his.

This is not a system ability. It is a consequence of what he contains.

---

### Switching Between Forms

The system never explains how. Marc discovers it during a moment of genuine desperation — trying to pass through an area where his monster form will get him killed by guards. He wants to be human badly enough, under enough pressure, and something *shifts.*

Later he understands: the switch is an act of will anchored to identity. Not the identity of "I am a monster" or "I am human." The identity of **who he is regardless of what he looks like.** When he knows that with enough clarity and conviction, he can wear either form like a coat.

The moment he first achieves human form is one of the most important character moments in the story — not because of the mechanics, but because of what it means about how he sees himself.

Beneath that single line, three processes run in sequence — invisible, instantaneous, permanent:

**Step 1 — CLAIM ORIGIN**
The system locks onto the creature's origin signature — not its species template, but its specific individual origin. The fundamental pattern that made it *that exact creature.* This is why the remains disintegrate: the claim strips authorship from the matter. Matter without origin cannot maintain structural coherence. It simply stops being.

**Step 2 — DISASSEMBLE STRUCTURE**
The claimed origin is broken into its components: physical architecture, energy patterns, instinct framework, bloodline threads, ability structure. Each is catalogued, separated, assessed.

**Step 3 — FILTER AND INTEGRATE**
Here is where the World Core Shard earns its presence. It resolves every compatibility conflict in real time. Cold-blooded scale biology integrating into warm-blooded tissue. Predator neural patterns layering onto a human nervous system. Things that should cause catastrophic rejection are restructured at the origin level to function within Marc's specific framework.

No sickness. No corruption. No instability.

This is the single most valuable function in the entire system. And Marc takes it completely for granted because he has never experienced anything else.

He will eventually understand — when he watches other cultivators attempt to integrate incompatible traits and fail horrifically — exactly what the World Core Shard is doing for him.

### Trait Types

The system can detect and present any of the following as a trait. This is not a complete list — it is a framework the system works within, and the system finds trait *categories* Marc hasn't encountered yet as he progresses.

**Physical Traits** — Structural body components. Scales, claws, bone density, organ modifications, musculature. Always absorbed in Monster Form. Always alter that part of Marc's body when integrated.

**Sensory Traits** — Preternatural perception. Heat-vision, tremor-sense, echolocation, electromagnetic field detection, aura reading. Absorbed in either form. Don't alter appearance.

**Instinct Traits** — Behavioral architecture. Predator awareness, pack coordination instinct, territorial memory, migration sense. Abstract and difficult to consciously control early on. They express as impulses, not commands.

**Energy Traits** — How the creature generates, stores, and expresses its core energy. Flame breath mechanics, lightning generation, void-bleed absorption. These interact with Marc's own Energy stat.

**Bloodline Traits** — Ancestral power passed through lineage. Rarer. Higher rank. Carry more information in their origin signature — echoes of the entire lineage, not just the individual.

**Awakened Abilities** — Another Corebearer's personal ability. Only available from fallen Corebearers. Extremely rare. The ethical weight of devouring a person's awakened ability is something the story does not ignore.

**Core Traits** — The inner core of a dungeon creature. Absorbing these does something Marc doesn't immediately understand: it contributes partially to the repair of his own damaged core. The system never explains this directly. He notices his Vitality improving faster after absorbing cores. The connection takes time to make.

### Trait Integration — The Mind Load

There is no hard limit on how many traits Marc can hold simultaneously.

There is a soft limit that is far more interesting: **his mind.**

Each integrated trait carries its origin's instinctive framework. A predator's territorial aggression. A herd creature's panic-response architecture. A creature with echolocation's constant awareness of reflected sound that doesn't exist for humans. A venomous creature's chemical-triggered feeding impulse.

None of these are controllable through the system. They are the passenger cost of integration. Marc's consciousness has to *contain* all of them simultaneously while remaining Marc.

Early on, two or three integrated traits is manageable. The instincts are faint, like background music. They don't interfere significantly with normal thought.

At ten integrated traits, the background gets louder. Overlapping impulses from different predator types occasionally conflict. Marc finds himself responding to stimuli that his human reasoning doesn't register as significant — because three of his integrated predators absolutely do.

At twenty, thirty, more — this becomes the central challenge of his existence that no rank or authority can fully solve. He is accumulating a crowd of instinctual personalities inside his own mind, and the only thing keeping him as Marc rather than the crowd is the clarity and strength of who he is.

This is not a mechanic the system manages. This is a *character* challenge. The system never acknowledges it. It is simply the price of what he does, and how he handles it defines what kind of person he remains.

## THE HIDDEN AUTHORITIES
### *Four Entries With No Names*

They appear in the interface as this:

```

  ◈ 【 ??? 】
  ◈ 【 ??? 】
  ◈ 【 ??? 】
```

No descriptions. No conditions listed. Not *[Locked]* — just *[???]* meaning the system either cannot or will not display them yet.

Marc notices them from the very beginning. He will not learn their names for a long time.

For story planning purposes only — these are not visible to Marc or the reader until their individual reveal moments:

---

**【 GENESIS 】**

Creates entirely new traits from nothing. Not Merged from existing ones — *authored.* Marc specifies a function. The World Core Shard builds the origin framework from raw primordial material. A trait that has never existed anywhere in the Origin Index, built from his specification.

He stops claiming what exists. He begins making what didn't.

Unlocks when the Core reaches full integrity and Marc has demonstrated genuine understanding of what origin functions *are* at their root level — not what they do, but why they are.

---

**【 INDEX — FULL 】**

Complete, unrestricted access to the Origin Index. Every cross-reference flag. Every anomaly report. Every restricted entry. Every classified file from the Zenith Empire's Sovereign Management Archive.

This is the moment Marc finds out what the system actually is.

Every secret the Zenith Empire buried. Every historical record. Every research finding. Every sealed technique. The vaults he has been unknowingly building toward — now visible, now accessible, gated by clearance tiers he now has to earn through challenges the system will finally explain.

**【 UNMAKE 】**

Does not devour. Does not claim. Does not convert.

Removes the Origin Identity from a living target entirely.

The target continues to exist. Their body remains intact. Their consciousness remains intact. But everything that made them *specifically themselves* — bloodline, cultivated power, abilities, the core of what they became — is gone. Stripped back to the raw biological baseline they started from.

They become blank. Whatever they built, taken. Whatever they were given by birth, taken.

The target is not dead. They might prefer death.

This is the authority that changes how cosmic powers perceive Marc when they eventually learn about it. It is not a weapon. It is an argument about what existence means. About whether what you are built from is *yours.*

Unlock conditions involve a moment of moral clarity the story will construct naturally. It cannot be described here without spoiling it.

---

**【 AUTHOR 】**

The final authority. The one the system has been building toward since the collision in the Zenith Empire's treasury.

When this authority activates, the system interface changes. The header that has read **THE DEVOUR SYSTEM** since Marc's awakening — since the first moment the screen appeared before his eyes in a cave full of bones — updates.

The new name is not listed here.

Some things should only exist in the story, at the moment they are supposed to arrive.

What can be said: the message that accompanies the activation of **【 AUTHOR 】** is the first time the system speaks to Marc in something other than interface language. Not a notification. Not a status update.

A sentence. In plain words.

And what it says answers the question Marc has been carrying since the day he woke up with claws and a dead heart dissolving into his blood:

*Why him.*

The things Marc can devour follow a scale that mirrors the cosmic structure he is slowly learning exists. Each category carries, in its origin signature, information about how it was made and what made it.

```
Early Arc     Dungeon creatures
              [Individual origins — building blocks]

Mid Arc       Apex dungeon creatures
              [Lineage origins — evolutionary peaks]

Expansion     Void-touched creatures
              [Corrupted origins — what the Inner Void
               does to living things]

Late Arc      Other Corebearers
              [Refined soul-origins — the weight of this
               cannot be overstated]

Deeper        Ancient bloodline bearers
              [Legacy origins — things that were old
               before the world remembered them]

Transition    Dungeon Cores
              [Constructed origins — something built
               this, deliberately, for a purpose]

Rising        Rift Cores
              [Spatial origins — what stabilizes the
               dimensional tears in Marc's world]

Major         World Cores
              [World-scale origins — the heartbeat of
               entire planets]

Critical      Universe Cores
              [Bubble-scale origins — what a universe
               is made of at its foundation]

Endgame       Primordial Fragment Cores
              [The shards of what everything came from]
```

By the endgame, Marc's Origin Index contains enough data to reconstruct — in theory — the complete taxonomy of how the Primordial World's fragmentation distributed origin identities across the cosmos.

He becomes, without intending to, the most comprehensive cosmological researcher who has ever lived.

The difference between Marc and the Zenith Empire's researchers — the ones whose questions started the chain of events that produced his system — is one thing:

They were looking for answers to protect themselves.

Marc's answers are looking for something to say back to whatever is waiting on the other side of the Eternal Aegis.
