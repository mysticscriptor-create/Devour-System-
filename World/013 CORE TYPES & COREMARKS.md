# CORE TYPES & COREMARKS
## Classification Reference — World of the Fractured Age

---

> When a human Awakens, two things happen simultaneously: a Core forms near the left ventricle, and a Coremark burns itself into the skin. The mark is permanent. It is unique. No two Corebearers in recorded history have ever shared an identical Coremark — even within the same Core Type.
>
> The Type tells you what category of power the Core draws from.
> The Mark tells you that this specific person carries it.

---

## ON COREMARK APPEARANCE

A Coremark appears at the moment of first Awakening — during or immediately after the First Spin breakthrough. The location on the body varies by individual and has no known pattern. The mark itself is not tattooed or burned onto the skin in a conventional sense: it *emerges*, as though it was always beneath the surface and the breakthrough simply made it visible.

Coremarks are not flat. Under light, they have a faint dimensional quality — a subtle depth, as if the mark exists slightly inside the skin rather than on it. At low rank, the mark is dim, the lines thin. As the Corebearer advances in rank, the mark intensifies: lines thicken, secondary details sharpen, and at B-rank and above, most Coremarks develop a faint passive luminescence visible in darkness.

Each Core Type has a **mark language** — a visual vocabulary that defines its category. Within that vocabulary, every individual mark is distinct: the specific arrangement, the proportions, the direction of the lines, the density of the pattern. Two Elemental-type Corebearers will both have geometric marks, but the specific geometry will never match.

## THE EIGHT CORE TYPES

---

### TYPE 1 — ELEMENTAL

**What it covers:**
All ability sets rooted in the manipulation of natural forces and physical phenomena. This is the most common Core Type globally.

Includes: fire, water, ice, lightning, wind, earth, stone, metal, magma, sand, wood, nature, plant-life, gravity, magnetism, light, darkness, sound, lightning, radiation, plasma, vacuum, pressure, temperature extremes, weather, tide.

**The logic:** Elemental Cores interface with the physical world's existing energy systems. They do not create — they redirect, amplify, and shape forces that already exist in the environment. A fire Elemental doesn't generate fire from nothing; they access the latent thermal potential in the air and accelerate it. This is why Elemental Corebearers are environment-sensitive: a water-type weakens in desert conditions, a lightning-type strengthens during storms.

**Coremark — Geometric Patterns:**
Elemental Coremarks are composed entirely of geometric shapes — triangles, hexagons, intersecting lines, radial symmetry, lattice structures. No curves. No organic forms. The geometry is precise, almost mechanical. The pattern often reflects the specific element: fire-type marks tend toward sharp, upward-pointing forms; water-type marks toward interlocking rings or wave-interrupted grids; earth-type toward dense, heavy angular tessellations.

The mark always looks like it could be a technical diagram for something. Something about its precision suggests it was *calculated* rather than grown.

---

### TYPE 2 — PHYSICAL

**What it covers:**
All ability sets rooted in enhancement and transformation of the body itself. The second most common Core Type.

Includes: superhuman strength, speed, endurance, reflexes, regeneration, durability, bone density, muscle fiber rewriting, sensory amplification (sight, hearing, smell beyond human range), body-part specialization (hardened fists, blade-edged limbs, enhanced lungs), adrenaline control, pain suppression, metabolic acceleration, biological adaptation to environments, partial or full transformation, beast-form abilities, bloodline physical enhancements.

**The logic:** Physical Cores route energy inward rather than outward. Instead of shaping the external world, they continuously reshape the Corebearer's own biology. Advanced Physical-type Corebearers are often indistinguishable from Elemental-type in raw destructive output — the difference is that every bit of that output is channeled through the body first. This makes Physical-type Corebearers extraordinarily durable but also more vulnerable to abilities that bypass physical defenses entirely.

**Coremark — Anatomical Lines:**
Physical Coremarks follow the body's own structure. They look like stylized anatomical diagrams — lines that suggest musculature, bone, tendons, or vascular pathways, abstracted into something that is clearly not a medical illustration but clearly references one. The mark often sits in a location that relates to the dominant ability: a strength-type mark over the shoulder or forearm, a speed-type along the calf or spine, a sensory-type near the temple or behind the ear.

The lines have weight to them — thick at load-bearing points, thinner at joints. They look like the map of something functional.

---

### TYPE 3 — SPATIAL

**What it covers:**
All ability sets rooted in the manipulation of space, distance, position, and dimensional structure.

Includes: teleportation, short-range blink-steps, portal creation, spatial compression, spatial expansion, distance warping (making far things near or near things far), pocket dimensions, dimensional storage, spatial cutting (cleaving through space itself), area denial, forced displacement, anchoring (locking a person or object in place in space), spatial perception (sensing everything within a radius regardless of line of sight), void manipulation, phase-shifting through solid matter.

**The logic:** Spatial Cores do not move through space — they rewrite the relationship between positions in space. A teleporting Corebearer isn't traveling the distance; they are making two points in space equivalent for a moment. This is why Spatial-type abilities are disproportionately powerful at high ranks — the gap between a C-rank and an A-rank Spatial-type is not more distance or faster speed, it is a qualitative difference in how much of the spatial fabric they can affect simultaneously.

**Coremark — Layered Overlapping Shapes:**
Spatial Coremarks are composed of shapes that overlap and intersect each other at impossible angles — as if the mark exists in more than two dimensions and the skin is only showing part of it. Circles that pass through each other, squares rotated through a third axis, lines that appear to go behind and in front of other lines without a clear logic to which is which. The mark seems to shift slightly when viewed from different angles, though whether this is real or optical is debated among ICA researchers.

Looking at a Spatial Coremark too long produces a faint sense of depth vertigo. Not everyone notices it.

---

### TYPE 4 — MENTAL

**What it covers:**
All ability sets rooted in the manipulation of mind, perception, information, and consciousness.

Includes: telepathy, mind reading, surface thought scanning, deep memory access, emotion sensing, emotion manipulation, illusion (visual, auditory, tactile, full sensory), fear induction, memory alteration, mental compulsion, suggestion, psychic shielding, mental fortress construction, shared consciousness, dream manipulation, perception filtering (making the Corebearer invisible to minds rather than eyes), tactical precognition (reading intent rather than future), information absorption (instantly processing observed data), mental link networks, psychic projection.

**The logic:** Mental Cores operate through a separate medium from physical energy — they interface directly with the consciousness field that all living beings generate. This is why Mental-type abilities work on dungeon creatures despite their non-human cognition (all complex nervous systems generate a consciousness signature), but fail entirely on constructs, undead, or spatial phenomena that have no mind to interface with. Mental Corebearers are the most feared in covert operations and the most politically scrutinized by the ICA.

**Coremark — Flowing Script:**
Mental Coremarks look like writing — but writing in no human or known foreign language. The marks are composed of fluid, cursive-like strokes that form characters or symbols that feel as though they should be readable, as though meaning is right at the edge of comprehension. The script flows in one or more lines across the skin. It does not repeat. No linguist has ever decoded it, though several have gone to unusual lengths trying.

The mark gives most non-Corebearers a mild, indefinable unease when looked at directly. Something about the way it almost means something.

---

### TYPE 5 — TEMPORAL

**What it covers:**
All ability sets rooted in the manipulation of time, causality, and sequence. The rarest Core Type. ICA estimates fewer than one in ten thousand Awakenings produce a Temporal Core.

Includes: personal time acceleration (subjective speed increase while the world slows), localized time dilation, time reversal on objects or wounds (returning something to an earlier state), predictive precognition (seeing short windows of future probability), post-cognition (reading the past of a location or object), temporal anchoring (preventing something from being affected by time-altering effects), age manipulation of non-living matter, moment freezing, selective memory of alternate timelines, causal threading (identifying the chain of causes behind a current state).

**The logic:** Temporal Cores are not manipulating a fourth dimension in the scientific sense — they are manipulating the Corebearer's relationship to the *sequence of dimensional energy flow*. Everything in the world exists in a state of continuous dimensional energy movement; Temporal Cores can adjust how a person or area interacts with that flow. True reversal of time does not exist within this system — what appears to be reversal is actually a forced return of dimensional energy to a prior configuration, which has limits and costs that scale sharply with complexity.

**Coremark — Spirals and Recursive Forms:**
Temporal Coremarks are built from spirals — single spirals, double spirals, logarithmic spirals, spirals that fold back into themselves. The mark may also feature recursive elements: patterns that repeat at smaller and smaller scales, or outer rings that contain smaller versions of the full design. The overall effect is a mark that appears to be in motion even when it isn't — the eye follows the spiral inward (or outward) and does not find a resting point.

At B-rank and above, some Temporal Coremarks appear to pulse very slightly at irregular intervals. Instruments confirm the mark does not physically move. The pulse, whatever it is, occurs between observations.

---

### TYPE 6 — FORCE

**What it covers:**
All ability sets rooted in the generation and manipulation of abstract energy, raw kinetic force, and fundamental physical laws beyond standard elemental classification.

Includes: telekinesis, kinetic energy manipulation, momentum transfer, force fields, energy absorption and redirection, impact amplification, shockwave generation, vibration manipulation, inertia control, pressure wave attacks, hardlight constructs, energy blasts of non-elemental origin, aura projection (force aura rather than elemental), barrier generation, recoil nullification, mass manipulation, explosive force generation, gravitational lensing (distinct from gravity as an element — this is the manipulation of force geometry rather than the gravitational phenomenon itself).

**The logic:** Force Cores operate in the layer of physics between the elemental and the spatial. They do not change what matter is (Elemental), they do not change where matter is (Spatial) — they change how matter *moves* and how energy *transfers*. A Force-type Corebearer at high rank can stop a mountain-destroying attack not by enduring it but by perfectly redirecting every joule of force in the impact. The discipline of Force-type development is almost entirely about precision and efficiency rather than raw output.

**Coremark — Radial Burst Patterns:**
Force Coremarks radiate outward from a central point — lines, arcs, or layered concentric shapes expanding from a core origin. The marks look like the visual representation of an impact or an explosion frozen in the moment of expansion. Some Force marks have a clear direction of flow; others appear perfectly symmetric in all directions. The central point is always empty — a void or a single dot from which everything else originates.

The mark has a sense of energy to it even when the Corebearer is at rest. Standing close to a high-rank Force-type mark, some sensitive individuals report feeling a very faint pressure — as if the air near it is very slightly more compressed.

---

### TYPE 7 — SOUL

**What it covers:**
All ability sets rooted in the manipulation of life force, spiritual energy, death, summoning, bonds, and the non-physical components of living beings. Historically called "Cursed-type" or "Heretic-type" in older ICA classifications; these terms were officially retired 200 years ago.

Includes: life force drain, vitality transfer, healing (Soul Cores heal by restoring the life-force pattern beneath physical damage, not by directly repairing tissue), curse application, blessing application, death affinity (reading, sensing, and communicating with death-state energy), spirit summoning, contract binding (binding another being to an agreement at the soul level), soul sight (perceiving the spiritual/energetic signature of any being), aura reading, undead creation and command, familiar bonding, sacrificial power (converting life force — self or others — into energy), death reversal (temporary, costly, and regulated heavily by the ICA), link abilities (creating permanent or semi-permanent connections between consciousnesses or life forces).

**The logic:** Soul Cores interface with the dimension that underlies biology — the energy pattern that defines a living being as distinct from inert matter. The ICA's most complex regulatory framework surrounds Soul-type Corebearers, because the ability to affect life force directly intersects with every major ethical and legal category the Association maintains. Most of these regulations were written after incidents. Soul Cores are not inherently dangerous. Their users are held to a higher verification and monitoring standard than any other type.

**Coremark — Organic and Living Forms:**
Soul Coremarks look alive. They are composed of forms that suggest organic life — branching shapes that resemble roots, veins, neurons, mycelial networks, or coral growth. They are the only Core Type whose marks appear to grow over time: a newly Awakened Soul-type will have a simple mark, but as the Corebearer advances in rank, the mark extends and complexifies — new branches appear, existing structures densify. This growth is slow and continuous, not sudden.

The mark feels slightly warmer than the surrounding skin to the touch. This has been measured. No one has a satisfying explanation for it.

---

### TYPE 8 — VOID

**What it covers:**
All ability sets rooted in negation, erasure, nullification, and the manipulation of absence itself. The second rarest Core Type after Temporal.

Includes: ability nullification (suppressing or canceling another Corebearer's power within range), energy erasure (consuming and eliminating energy rather than redirecting it), silence fields (zones where no ability functions), existence suppression (making the Corebearer — or a target — undetectable to any sense or instrument), negation aura (passively weakening abilities used against the Corebearer), anti-materialization (preventing energy from taking physical form), conceptual erasure (at very high ranks: removing a concept — such as *hardness* or *heat* — from a localized area), counter-force generation, immunity stacking, resistance fields, disruption of dimensional energy flow, signal cancellation.

**The logic:** Void Cores are not the absence of power — they are the active manipulation of absence. The distinction matters because true nullification requires more precise control than almost any other type: to erase something completely, the Corebearer must perfectly understand what it is they are erasing. Void-type Corebearers are regarded as among the most strategically dangerous opponents in the world, not because of destructive output but because their existence invalidates the combat assumptions of every other Core Type. An A-rank Void-type can functionally neutralize an S-rank of any other type in the right conditions.

**Coremark — Negative Space:**
Void Coremarks are the inverse of every other type. The mark is defined not by what is drawn but by what is absent — the mark appears as a region of skin that looks subtly *wrong*, where the light behaves slightly differently, where shadow falls in ways that don't quite match the ambient lighting. The "design" is the shape of the void: a precisely bounded area of apparent emptiness, often edged by a single clean line that defines the boundary of the absence.

Looking at a Void Coremark produces a sensation that is difficult to describe — not vertigo, not unease, but a sense of something *missing* in the field of vision. Some people report that they cannot look directly at a high-rank Void mark for more than a few seconds. This is not a compulsion effect. The mark simply resists sustained attention.

## MARK INTENSITY BY RANK

| Rank | Mark Appearance |
|---|---|
| **F** | Thin, dim. The lines are present but faint, as if the mark hasn't fully committed to being there yet. |
| **E** | Slightly thicker. Noticeably more defined under direct light. The mark looks settled now — it belongs. |
| **D** | Full-weight lines. The mark is clearly visible in normal light. Secondary details that were unclear at lower ranks become legible. |
| **C** | The mark deepens. At this rank, the first signs of passive luminescence appear in darkness for most types — a faint glow, barely perceptible. |
| **B** | Luminescence is clear in low light. The mark has reached its full design complexity. Everything that will ever appear in the mark is now present. What remains is intensity. |
| **A** | The mark pulses faintly in sync with the Corebearer's energy output — visible to the naked eye if the observer is close and paying attention. |
| **S** | The mark is always slightly luminescent, even in daylight. Instruments register a faint energy emission from the mark itself. |
| **SS** | The mark has become a secondary energy organ. The luminescence is clearly visible in normal light. The mark produces measurable output regardless of the Corebearer's activity state. |
| **SSS** | The mark appears to exist slightly outside the skin. The visual depth that was subtle at low rank is now unmistakable — the mark looks like a window into something. |

## MIXED-TYPE AWARENESS

True dual-type Cores — a single Core that covers two full type categories — do not exist in documented ICA records. A Corebearer has one Core Type. Their abilities fall within that type.

What does occur, rarely, is **edge ability expression**: a Corebearer whose abilities sit at the boundary between two types. A high-rank Physical-type who develops regeneration so advanced it begins affecting nearby allies (approaching Soul-type territory). A Force-type whose barrier abilities begin to have spatial qualities at A-rank and above. These cases are flagged and monitored by the ICA's Classification Review Board, but the Core Type itself does not change.

**Mixed Coremarks** — marks that appear to contain visual elements from two different type languages — are extremely rare and always significant. In ICA records, fewer than thirty cases are documented across 650 years. Every one of those Corebearers was exceptional in ways that defied standard rank assessments. The ICA does not currently have a satisfying theoretical explanation for why mixed marks occur or what they mean for the Corebearer's development ceiling.

This information is not publicly available. It is in a restricted classification file.

## COREMARK SOCIAL FUNCTION

In the world as it currently exists, Coremarks are simultaneously the most personal thing a Corebearer carries and the most public. The mark is visible. It cannot be removed. At high ranks it cannot even be fully concealed.

Among Corebearers, reading someone's mark — identifying the type, assessing the rank by intensity, noticing anything unusual about the specific design — is instinctive. It happens in the first few seconds of meeting. It is considered polite to not comment on what you observe unless invited to.

Dormants cannot read Coremarks in any meaningful way. They can see the mark, identify that it is a Coremark, and at B-rank and above, observe the luminescence. But the type, the rank, the significance of the specific design — all of that requires a Corebearer's perceptual sensitivity to read.

The single exception to this social norm is the ICA's official inspection protocol, in which a licensed Examiner has the right to request a full mark reading at close range. Refusing an official mark reading is a significant legal event. Corebearers who refuse are flagged, and the flag does not expire.
