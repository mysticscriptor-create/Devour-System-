| **+++** | **Threshold Phase** | The Core vibrates at its absolute limit. Either it breaks through — or it breaks the person holding it. |

## ⬜ Dormant — 3×–5×

A person with a Core that hasn't awakened. Their body passively absorbs the Core's baseline output, making them stronger than any trained human — and they know it.

**Physical**
- Lifting: 240–400 kg
- Speed: 30–45 km/h sustained sprint
- Durability: bruise slowly, recover faster, bones harder than average
- Vision: slightly sharper
- Lifespan: 100–120 years

**Social role**
Dormants are identified through ICA arc-scale screening, mandatory in most countries for children aged 8–14. Every school and hospital participates. Children learn their Core status before they learn to drive. Those identified are offered cultivation pathways, military recruitment, or labor contracts. Dormants who never awaken still earn more than baseline humans — construction, law enforcement, and manual labor specifically recruit them. A Dormant can out-lift a crane operator, work sixteen hours without meaningful fatigue, and take a beating that would hospitalize a normal person.

They are paid better than normal workers. They are treated as less than Corebearers. This is the source of significant cultural friction.

## 🟢 F-Rank — *"The Foundation"* — 10×–20×

*"The birth of something that was never meant to exist."*

**What it means:** The moment of true awakening. The Core — previously just a grain-sized organ near the left ventricle — activates for the first time. Veins widen. The left ventricle restructures slightly. The nervous system begins adapting to a faster signal environment. This is called **Venous Burn**, and it is exactly as pleasant as it sounds.

**Power:** 10× – 20× | **EU Capacity:** 100–800 EU

### Core State: *"The Seed"*
A soft, grain-sized organ forms near the left ventricle. It doesn't beat. It doesn't hum. It just **waits** — like a coal that hasn't caught fire yet.

### Physical Profile

| Stat | F (10×) | F+++ (20×) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lifting | 800 kg | ~2 tons |
| Sprint (burst) | 50 km/h | 80 km/h |
| Sprint (sustained) | 30 km/h | 50 km/h for hours |
| Jump height | 3 meters | 6 meters vertical |
| Vision clarity | 2× normal | Read text at 200m |
| Night vision | Slightly enhanced | Noticeably enhanced |
| Reaction time | 2× peak human | 3× peak human |
| Hearing | 100m range | Distinguish voices at 100m |

### Durability
- Falls from 3–4 story buildings: survive with injuries
- Blades: cut but not deeply
- Bullets: graze rather than penetrate
- At F+++: small caliber bullets survivable with serious injury

### Body Changes
- **Appetite:** 3–5× normal caloric intake. Strong protein cravings.
- **Sleep:** 4–5 hours. Body refines absorbed essence during rest.
- **Lifespan:** 120–140 years.
- **Recovery:** Minor cuts close in hours. Broken bone: days instead of months. Lost limbs: no regeneration.
- **Personal Ability:** The first ability awakens at F. Unique to each person. Raw and unrefined — like a flame with no wick.

### What F-Rank Means Socially
Even F-rank is dangerous. Most civilians have no frame of reference for someone who can lift two tons and sprint at 80 km/h. Early F-ranks sometimes don't understand the gap themselves — their new baseline feels normal to them.

The gap becomes obvious the first time they break something accidentally. A door handle. A handshake that leaves bruises. A chair that collapses under casual pressure. The body takes weeks to calibrate to what it's become.

### Breakthrough: *"The First Spin"*

**Requirement:** Fill the Seed to 800 EU. The pressure begins around 650 EU — the body starts rejecting the buildup.

**The Process:**
The Corebearer must do something that should be impossible: reach inside themselves and *make the Core move.* Not with their hands. With their **will.**

The moment it spins, the Seed ruptures inward — not outward — and pumps raw, unrefined energy directly into the bloodstream. The veins light up like copper wire under too much current. This is called **Venous Burn**: the sensation of boiling metal moving through every artery in the body, permanently widening the circulatory system to handle what comes next.

> Those who survive say the worst part isn't the pain. It's the *sound* — a high-pitched ring that lasts for three days. No one has ever explained where the sound comes from. It isn't the Core. Instruments pick up nothing. It simply exists — directional, faint, like something listening from very far away.

**Success:** The Core expands from a grain to the size of a small pea. The surface smooths out. It starts to hum — faint, almost like a heartbeat that isn't quite in sync.

**Failure:** The Seed either **cracks** (temporary shutdown, recovery possible) or **calcifies** — hardening into dead tissue, locking the person out of their own power forever.

**Failure Rate:** ~40% without supervision | **Safeguards:** ICA supervised facilities

*"The body has stopped fighting the Core. Now they are becoming something together."*

**Power:** 30× – 50× | **EU Capacity:** up to 8,000 EU

### Core State: *"The Pulse"*
The Core begins beating — not randomly, but **in sync with the heart.** For the first time, it feels like it belongs there. It grows thin root-threads into the major arteries, tasting the bloodstream, learning the body's map.

### What E-Rank Means Socially
An E-rank moving at full speed is a blur to baseline humans. Their footsteps leave impressions in soft ground. Casual conversation becomes harder — they're processing sensory input the other person can't perceive.

This is also when many Corebearers first feel the **isolation.** They sleep less. Eat more. Hear things others don't. The gap that started at awakening keeps widening. It never stops.

### Breakthrough: *"The Scaffolding"*

**Requirement:** Core energy approaches 7,000–8,000 EU. The body can no longer safely carry the output. The skeleton — still fully human — is the weakest point.

**The Process:**
The Core identifies the problem before the Corebearer does. It releases a **binding agent** directly into the bone marrow — a dense, energy-rich fluid that shouldn't exist in human biology. What follows is called **Skeletal Lock.**

Every bone in the body microfractures simultaneously. The person collapses. They are paralyzed — not from nerve damage, but because the marrow is being *replaced*, and the bones are regrowing around it: denser, ivory-white in cross-section, laced with energy channels that conduct essence the way copper conducts electricity. The process takes hours. Most people describe it as dying slowly while staying awake.

When it's over, the skeleton is no longer purely biological. It's a **lattice** — a structure capable of holding what's coming next.

> The binding agent has never been synthesized in a lab. ICA researchers have tried for decades. It appears, does its work, and leaves no traceable residue — as if it was never manufactured. As if it was always going to be there.

**Side effect:** Body weight increases noticeably. The person moves differently. They *land* differently.

**Success:** Reinforced skeleton, greatly increased energy load tolerance, a faint resonance in the bones when the Core pulses.

**Failure:** Bone Shatter Syndrome — the fractures don't heal correctly. High mortality if untreated within the hour.

**Failure Rate:** ~35% without supervision | **Safeguards:** ICA staged marrow infusion reduces failure by over 50%

## 🟡 D-Rank — *"The Dauntless"* — 100×–300×

*"The last rank where human limits technically apply. They have discarded them."*

**What it means:** The Boil breakthrough rewrites blood chemistry. D-rank Corebearers are no longer operating on human biology. They use it as a template. The molecular architecture has been redesigned.

**Power:** 100× – 300× | **EU Capacity:** up to 80,000 EU

### Core State: *"The Engine"*
The Core stops pulsing. It begins a **constant low-frequency hum** — like a turbine that never turns off. It grows secondary veins that pierce directly into the heart muscle, creating a **Dual-Circulation system**: one for blood, one for Essence. Existing veins thicken and widen for faster transfer.

The heart now has two jobs. It has never complained.

### Physical Profile

| Stat | D (100×) | D+++ (300×) |
|------|---------|------------|
| Lifting | 10 tons | 30 tons |
| Sprint (burst) | 250 km/h | 380 km/h |
| Sprint (sustained) | 150 km/h | 200 km/h |
| Jump height | 30 meters | 60 meters |
| Vision | 3 km clarity | 5 km, energy disturbances visible |
| Body heat | Elevated permanently | Comfortable in arctic conditions |

### Durability
- High-caliber military rounds: mostly deflected, cause bruising not wounds
- Explosions: survivable (injured, not fatal)
- Vehicle collisions: they stop the vehicle

### Body Changes
- **Appetite:** Normal food barely satisfies. Requires dungeon-grade food — creature meat, dungeon-grown produce, or essence-infused supplements. Without dungeon food: functional but perpetually hungry, refinement rate drops 20–30%.
- **Sleep:** 2–3 hours.
- **Lifespan:** 250–350 years.
- **Recovery:** Major wounds close in hours. Lost fingers and toes regenerate in days. Organs regenerate with sustained rest. Full limbs: not yet.
- **Presence:** Other people feel something without being able to name it. Animals are uneasy.

### What D-Rank Means Socially
D-rank is when the military stops commanding and starts *accommodating.* A D-rank who decides not to comply with an order cannot be physically forced to comply. Policy shifts from "command and control" to "incentive and alignment." They are given significantly more autonomy — not from trust, but because the cost of forcing compliance is too high.

### Breakthrough: *"The Boil"*

**Requirement:** Blood is too thin to carry D-rank Essence density. It must be changed at the molecular level.

**The Process:**
The Core heats itself to internal temperatures above 200°C. The water in the blood **evaporates** — not all at once, but gradually, over agonizing hours, replaced by **Ichor-Enriched Plasma**: a luminous, heavy fluid threaded with energy microcapsules visible only through Arc-scales or medical instrumentation. The blood's chemistry rewrites itself at a level below cells, below proteins — at the level of *what the fluid is made of.*

Survivors describe it as being **cooked alive from the inside out, one vein at a time.**

The blood remains red when it's over. But it's heavier. It smells faintly of hot metal. And it carries 100 times the oxygen and energy density of normal blood.

> What's strange is that the new Ichor-Enriched Plasma doesn't just carry energy better. It carries a faint, unique signature — one that Arc-scale readers describe as "outward-facing." Like a flag. Like a label. *(Foreshadow: the refined blood is literally marking them for collection.)*

**Success:** Dramatically accelerated healing, metabolic throughput, and immune resilience.

**Failure — "The Trap":** If the process is rushed or the will weakens mid-way, the enriched fluid **coagulates** before it finishes converting. It turns to sludge in the veins. Stroke. Death. There is no warning when it starts.

**Failure Rate:** ~45% without supervision | **Safeguards:** Cooling rigs, energy radiators, Null-stone blood filters

## 🟡 C-Rank — *"The Conqueror"* — 800×–1,500×

*"Urban myth territory."*

**What it means:** The rank where Corebearers stop being soldiers and start being forces of nature. A C-rank Corebearer aged under 30 is not a prodigy. They are an anomaly. Most serious dungeon runners reach C in their 40s–50s after decades of methodical work.

**Power:** 800× – 1,500× | **EU Capacity:** up to 500,000 EU

### Core State: *"The Furnace"*
The Core achieves **perfect sphere geometry** for the first time. Imperfections in its shape — tiny fluctuations that existed since F-rank — smooth away completely. It now digests incoming Soul Essence *instantly*, no longer requiring sleep to process intake. It runs. It never stops running.

### Body Changes

- **Sleep:** 1–2 hours.
- **Lifespan:** 500–700 years.
- **Recovery:** Wounds close visibly while you watch. Severed limbs regenerate fully within days.
- **Presence:** F-rank Corebearers feel physical unease nearby. Normal humans feel instinctive fear they can't explain.

### Breakthrough: *"The Whisper"*

**Requirement:** Core reaches Threshold Phase (~99% of 500,000 EU). The mind begins to sync with the energy — uninvited.

**The Process:**
At Threshold Phase, something begins to *speak.* Not in words. Not clearly. But the Corebearer starts hearing it — a sound beneath sound, a presence at the edge of thought. The ICA calls it **background resonance.** Corebearers call it **The Whisper.** What it actually is: the first faint signal of the **Main Consciousness**, bleeding through the thinning wall between the Core and whatever lies beyond it.

Most people mistake it for madness. Some of them are right.

To break through, the Corebearer must not block the Whisper. They must **face it** — hold their identity firm against something that has no identity of its own, only hunger — and force their will onto the Core's final expansion.

> The Whisper never says anything threatening. It never demands. It simply... notices. And somehow, being noticed by it feels worse than anything it could have said.

Those who succeed don't just rank up. They gain **Intent**: the ability to project willpower as a physical, directed force. They can *mean* something at a person and have the world agree.

**Failure:** The ones who break here don't die easily. They become **Fallen** — minds overwritten by the Devourer signal, bodies still functional, purpose replaced.

**Failure Rate:** ~55% without supervision | **Safeguards:** Group Binding Rituals; dedicated psych monitoring pre- and post-breakthrough

It no longer needs the heart to sustain it. The heart continues beating, but the relationship has quietly reversed: the Core is the primary organ. The heart is the backup.

Organs don't change shape. They don't turn to stone. They *tune* — their internal protein structures reorganizing into energy-conductive lattices while remaining fully living tissue.

The lungs begin supplementing breath with **passive energy absorption** from ambient Essence. The stomach's role shrinks. The circulatory system's role shifts. The body is not becoming inorganic. It is becoming *optimized* — a biological instrument refined to carry and express power at a scale that should require something inhuman to house.

Outwardly, the person looks identical. Inwardly, they are the most dangerous biological object on the planet.

**Failure:** Organ necrosis. Loss of autonomic function. The body forgets how to run itself.

**Failure Rate:** ~65% without supervision | **Safeguards:** Long-term ICA monitoring; Soul-Anchors to prevent Essence siphoning during the restructuring window

For a fraction of a second — less than a heartbeat — the Corebearer's mind brushes against the full pattern of the **Main Consciousness.**

Not its attention. Not its will. Just its *shape.*

That is enough. The mind shatters instantly — not from pain, but from **scale**. It is the difference between holding a candle and standing inside a star.

What follows is the real trial: reconstructing a functional mind from the fragments, in real time, while the Core runs wild and tears apart everything within miles. The Corebearer must remember who they are before there's nothing left to remember with.

> What no one talks about — what the post-tribulation therapists are quietly instructed never to ask about — is what the Corebearers see in that fraction of a second. The ones who are asked directly go quiet. Then they say the same thing, always: *"It already knew my name."*

**Failure:** Survivors without proper debriefing often become **alien-efficient** — functional, brilliant, almost inhuman in their calm. Their emotions don't disappear. They just stop feeling *necessary.* Many never fully return to who they were.

**Failure Rate:** ~85% without proper council and post-event support | **Safeguards:** Extended post-Shattering therapy conducted by other S-rank Corebearers; soul-counseling; specialized sociopsychological teams

The Corebearer no longer has a circulatory system and a Core system. They have one system. It runs everything. It runs at a speed that human neurology was never designed to operate at.

# Section 6 — The Progression Economy
*How long it actually takes — and why*

## 6.1 — Dungeon Access and Real-World Constraints

The daily kill limit is not the binding constraint. **Dungeon access is.**

A working-class Corebearer in a Tier-1 city can realistically access a dungeon **2–3 times per week.** Reasons why not every day:

- **Entry fees and licensing:** ICA-regulated dungeons charge access fees. A full day's hunting costs more than a full day's alternative work for most F and E-ranks.
- **Recovery:** Even within the daily kill limit, a proper dungeon run leaves the body physically depleted. Pushing two back-to-back runs degrades session quality.
- **Dungeon availability:** Popular dungeons have booking systems. Slot scarcity is real in dense urban environments.
- **Injury downtime:** A session gone wrong means days or weeks off. This is not rare. This is normal.
- **Resource costs:** At D-rank and above, the food and supplementation required to sustain serious dungeon activity becomes a major economic factor.

**The realistic math:** At 2 full runs per week, averaging 10 same-rank kills per run → approximately **1,000 kills per year.** This is the baseline assumption for the progression timelines below.

An elite runner with institutional backing (ICA scholarship, corporate contract, military secondment) can reach 3–4 runs per week, roughly 1,500–2,000 kills per year. These are the prodigies. This is why their advancement seems miraculous to working-class runners.
