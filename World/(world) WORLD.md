# PART ONE — TIMELINE & HISTORY

## What the Public Knows

The world has been broken for 812 years. That is the number in every textbook, carved into monuments, taught to children before they can read well. The official story goes like this:

A dimensional disaster split reality open beneath the Pacific Ocean. Humanity almost didn't survive. Then, 650 years ago, dungeons appeared — strange dangerous pocket worlds anchored to Earth — and some humans began awakening cores inside them. These first Corebearers gave humanity a way to fight back. Six hundred years ago the ICA was formed. The world was slowly rebuilt. There have been four major Realm-Fractures since the first. The last one, 19 years ago, was sealed. The world is stable now.

That story is not a lie. It is just incomplete in ways that matter enormously.

---

## The Real Timeline

### The Dark Century — 812 to 650 Years Ago

The First Realm-Fracture did not come from nowhere. A pre-ICA coalition of nations was running energy experiments deep beneath the Pacific — they believed they had found a way to tap unlimited dimensional energy. They were right that the energy was there. They were wrong about what would happen when they pierced through to it.

The fracture opened beneath their drill site. Everything in a two-hundred kilometer radius sank into the ocean within four hours. The energy that poured through rewrote the physical laws of every coastal region on Earth within weeks. Creatures came through that made modern weapons useless. Entire island nations vanished in a single night.

For over a century, humanity did not fight. It hid, starved, and died.

What the ICA does not teach: early proto-Corebearers existed before dungeons appeared. Exposure to the dimensional energy flooding through the fracture mutated a small number of survivors. These people were unstable — their cores formed without structure, without the dungeon system to calibrate them — and many of them were genuinely monstrous. Surviving governments executed most of them out of fear. A few survived long enough to teach others. The legends of Saints and Demons from this period describe the same people, remembered differently depending on who the storyteller's community feared more.

---

### The Great Emergence — 650 Years Ago

Across six continents, over approximately three months, hundreds of dungeons appeared simultaneously. No geological logic. No warning. Some formed inside cities. Some at the bottom of the ocean. Some inside structures that did not survive the formation.

The ICA teaches this as humanity's gift. A blessing from tragedy. The truth is more complicated: dungeons are containment systems built into the dimensional fabric of lower-realm worlds. Their appearance was a structural response to the fracture's destabilizing energy — they siphon excess dimensional pressure to keep the planet from fracturing further. Without dungeons, Realm-Fractures would multiply uncontrollably. The planet would eventually tear apart.

This also means: destroying too many dungeons could end the world. The ICA knows this. They have known it for approximately three hundred years. They have never announced it publicly.

The dungeons' second function — giving humanity access to dimensional energy that awakened cores — was incidental. Real, useful, world-changing. But incidental.

---

### The Age of Reclamation — 650 to 500 Years Ago

Humanity learned to use dungeons. The first organized dungeon-clearing teams formed. Monster materials proved valuable for weapons, medicine, and construction. Coreborn children — humans born with cores already forming, as dimensional energy saturated the world — began appearing in small numbers.

The world started rebuilding.

Dimensional Leaks and Micro-Fractures began leaving their mark across the world. Areas where these events had happened repeatedly became the first Wild-Lands — open-air zones of altered terrain, foreign creatures, and unpredictable energy. Humanity adapted: underground tunnel networks began forming between settlements, first as simple shelters, later as the foundation of what would become the global transit system.

---

### The First Consolidation — 600 Years Ago

The ICA was not formed peacefully.

Three rival Corebearer coalitions were competing for control of the dungeon network and the growing Corebearer population. The ICA — then called the Continental Accord — was one of them. Over approximately twenty years, the Accord systematically crushed the other two coalitions. Black-ops elimination of key leaders. Memory-altering relics used on surviving witnesses. Dungeon-based disappearances for anyone who tried to speak publicly about what had happened.

By the time the ICA was formally announced to the world as a neutral organization formed to protect humanity, there were no surviving organized alternatives. Neutrality had been manufactured.

The registration system came next. Dungeons placed under state and corporate control. Ranking systems standardized. The ICA positioned itself as the necessary center of everything.

---

### The Second Realm-Fracture — 500 Years Ago

The Mediterranean region. The fracture opened and did not close — self-sustaining, far larger than the first. This became the first confirmed Foreign Infested Zone. The ruling species were the Abyssal Nagarae. What the ICA did not publicize: the Nagarae had been present in the Pacific since the first fracture. They had, in fact, stabilized it from the inside. The Pressure Wall — the dimensional barrier surrounding the First Fracture — was their construction, not a natural phenomenon. Humanity had been protected by a species it was taught to view as invaders.

The secret treaty signed at this time still holds today: the Nagarae protect Earth's oceans from dimensional collapse. In exchange, humanity supplies them with dimensional crystals they use to maintain communication with their origin realm.

---

### The Age of Expansion — 500 to 300 Years Ago

Dungeons reclassified as resource mines, training grounds, and controlled farms. Corebearer academies established. Humanity transitioned from survival to dominance in most reclaimed territories.

The ICA ran its first black project during this period: Project Cradle. Hundreds of infants were exposed to controlled dungeon pressure in an attempt to manufacture Coreborn children on demand. Almost all died. The survivors — roughly eleven confirmed, possibly more — do not appear in any ICA registry. They were placed in separate facilities. Their current whereabouts and status are classified above SSS-guardian clearance level.

---

### The Third Realm-Fracture — 300 Years Ago

The Scorched Belt — between the first and second fractures, roughly equatorial. The Iron-Sands Sultanate formed from this fracture. The Rakshasa clans arrived alongside several other species. First confirmed contact with non-aquatic sovereign species.

The ICA made a decision that shaped the next three hundred years: supply the Iron-Sands Sultanate with Void-Metal — a processed dimensional alloy that is effectively a controlled weapon. Keep them fighting other zones instead of humans. The logic was sound in the short term. Three hundred years later, the Sultanate is the largest non-human territory on Earth, better-armed than any single human region, and the source of constant border conflict with the Mordant Dominion.

---

### The Age of Tension — 300 to 100 Years Ago

Rising conflict between human powers over dungeon ownership. ICA authority challenged repeatedly, never broken. Proxy wars fought through dungeon control and Corebearer contracts. The eight current great regions began consolidating from the dozens of smaller political entities that existed before.

The Censored Event: 40 years ago, a Realm-Fracture briefly opened in what is now the Orenthellion's eastern edge. It was sealed within hours. The surrounding city — population 340,000 — vanished entirely. The ICA's official record lists it as a large-scale dimensional disaster caused by dungeon instability. The actual record, classification Absolute Seal, describes an emergency Null-Core collapse performed by the regional SSS-guardian to close the fracture. The guardian did not survive. Their name was removed from all public records and replaced in official history with the cover story. There is no dungeon in that region today. There never will be. The dead zone is listed as a geological hazard zone.

---

### The Fourth Realm-Fracture — 19 Years Ago

An Arctic fracture. Catastrophic. The ICA's official account describes a heroic united effort — thousands of elite Corebearers sacrificing themselves to seal the breach.

The truth has three layers:

**Layer One (the version the ICA's upper leadership knows):** The effort did involve mass Corebearer sacrifice, but it was organized by the ICA as a deliberate overload operation. Entire combat units had their cores pushed past breaking point in a controlled sequence designed to create a dimensional implosion. Survivors were memory-wiped and retired with honors. Several dozen who could not be safely memory-wiped were quietly removed from the record.

**Layer Two (the version only three people on Earth know):** The fracture was not sealed by the Corebearer overload operation. The overload slowed its expansion and bought time. The actual sealing was performed by a single individual — an old practitioner named Ryath, carrying an infant through a dimensional storm — who used a technique that even SSS-rank guardians cannot perform. He sealed the fracture using a mechanism tied to the same cosmic architecture the Zenith Empire once worked with. He nearly died doing it. He is still recovering in a pocket space adjacent to Earth's bubble universe.

**Layer Three (what the non-human regions know):** Sealing a Realm-Fracture is categorically impossible by human means. The ICA's overload operation would have slowed the breach but never closed it. Something else happened. Something they cannot explain. The highest leadership of the Iron-Sands Sultanate, the Abyssal Nagarae, the Jade Hegemony, and the GLACIAL HIVE all know this. None of them have shared this information with their lower ranks. None of them have moved against humanity since. They are waiting to understand what sealed that fracture before they commit to any large-scale action.

Humanity — including the ICA's own highest leadership — believes their operation worked. They do not know about Ryath. They do not know about the infant he was carrying. They have spent 19 years celebrating a victory they did not actually win.

---

### The Current Era

Eight great human regions. Four foreign infested zones. A cold war built on a misunderstanding. The public believes the world is stable. The upper ranks of the ICA know it is managed. The highest powers in the non-human regions know something sealed that fracture that they haven't found yet.

The fractures still bleed. Slowly.

# PART TWO — THE ICA

## International Corebearer Association

**Full Name:** International Corebearer Association
**Headquarters:** Sovereign-Grand, Orenthellion
**Stated Purpose:** Regulate Corebearers, manage dungeons, protect humanity, maintain diplomatic contact with Foreign Infested Zones
**Actual Function:** The closest thing Earth has to a world government, operating through controlled information, economic dependency, and strategic force

---

## Structure

### The Ten Pillars

The ICA's ruling council. Officially: ten seats held by elected representatives from major regions plus the SSS-rank Guardians. In practice, the Guardians hold effective veto power over all major decisions, and three of the ten seats have been held by the same ancient families for over two hundred years through various legal mechanisms.

Current membership is classified. Public-facing names are given for five seats. The other five are officially anonymous for "security purposes."

---

### The Departments

**Dungeon Operations Command** — Manages all ICA-registered dungeon access, tier classification, reclassification proceedings, emergency response operations. The largest department by headcount.

**Hunter Corps** — The ICA's armed response force. Handles Dimensional Leaks, Micro-Fractures, wild monster incursions, and border enforcement. Organized into regional divisions.

**Intelligence Division (ICAID)** — The part that doesn't appear in most public descriptions. Monitors Corebearer power levels across all regions, tracks unregistered individuals, maintains the memory-alteration relic program (classified), and runs the ICA's network of informants inside every major academy and corporation.

**Diplomatic Council** — Manages ongoing contact with the four Foreign Infested Zones. Officially the most transparent department. Actually one of the most information-restricted, because everything discussed with foreign sovereigns is classified by default.

**Research & Classification** — Studies dungeon phenomena, monster biology, Core development, and dimensional events. Has a classified sublevel that researches things they don't publish anywhere.

**Academy Oversight Bureau** — Accredits academies, sets curriculum standards, manages the Academy Grant system. Has significant power over which academies survive financially.

---

## Registration System — Why Everyone Registers

The ICA registration system works primarily through economic dependency, not direct force. Most Corebearers register because the alternative is worse, not because they are forced to.

**What registered Corebearers get:**
- Access to ICA-licensed markets (the only legal source for high-grade Core Supplements, advanced dungeon equipment, dimensional alloys, and processed monster materials)
- Legal permission to enter ICA-registered dungeons
- The rank certification that most employers, academies, and contracts require
- ICA insurance — which covers medical costs from dungeon injuries at licensed facilities
- Legal protection under Corebearer law (unregistered individuals have no standing in ICA arbitration)

**What unregistered Corebearers cannot access:**
- Any ICA-licensed market (which covers roughly 80% of the high-value goods economy)
- Any ICA-registered dungeon (which covers all Tier-2 and above)
- ICA medical coverage
- Most corporate contracts (which require ICA certification as a baseline)
- Legal recourse if harmed by a registered Corebearer — ICA arbitration does not handle cases involving unregistered parties

**The gap in the system:**
Ancient families with privately-owned dungeons and megacorporations with corporate-controlled dungeon access can operate in a semi-registered gray area. They train their people in private facilities, use their economic power to access markets through intermediaries, and maintain enough registered members to keep a legal face. The ICA knows this. The political cost of actually confronting a megacorporation with a seat on the Benefactor Council is higher than the benefit of closing the gap. So the gap continues.

---

## The ICA's Hidden Tools

**Memory Alteration Relics** — Recovered from a dungeon approximately 400 years ago. The ICA has never published their existence. They are dimensional artifacts that can overwrite short-term memory — up to approximately 48 hours — with constructed false memories. Used sparingly, on specific individuals whose testimony would be inconvenient. The individuals chosen for alteration are always selected carefully: people with no powerful protectors, no institutional backing, and no reason anyone would investigate their claims if they contradicted the official record.

**The Disappearance Protocol** — Dungeon-based removal. Specifically: using a Wild or Sovereign-Controlled dungeon in a remote location to "lose" someone whose removal is required but whose death cannot be officially documented. The person is listed as missing following a dungeon incident. The ICA's own emergency response team is the one that "searched and found nothing." This has been used approximately 340 times in the ICA's history. Each instance is classified above SSS clearance. The list exists in one location: a physical ledger in the Director-General's office in Sovereign-Grand, handwritten, never digitized.

**The Threat Matrix** — A classification system for Corebearers who represent potential threats to ICA authority. Not dangerous in a monster sense — dangerous in a political sense. Individuals with unusual power trajectories, those asking the wrong questions, those with independent social influence that isn't channeled through ICA-approved institutions. The Matrix has five levels. Being added to the Matrix does not mean immediate action. It means monitoring. Level four means soft containment — steering the individual toward controllable positions. Level five is the Disappearance Protocol.

PART THREE — WILD-LANDS & DIMENSIONAL EVENTS

> *The space between safe zones is not empty. It never was.*

---

## What Wild-Lands Are

Wild-Lands — sometimes called Danger Zones, Out-Zones, or just "the Out" — are areas permanently altered by dimensional events. They exist in every human region. Some are old enough to have been on maps for 600 years. Some are new enough that the ICA's ink hasn't dried on the reclassification paperwork.

They come from three sources: **Dimensional Leaks**, **Micro-Fractures**, and **Dungeon Bursts**. Each leaves a different kind of mark on the land.

---

## Dimensional Events

### Dimensional Leak
A temporary rift — it opens, pours foreign energy and creatures into the surrounding area for minutes to hours, then closes on its own. The most common dimensional event. Hunter Corps maintains rapid response protocols specifically for these. Death toll depends almost entirely on how fast response arrives and how populated the area is.

The area around a closed Leak is temporarily elevated in dimensional energy. Creatures that came through may linger. Flora may accelerate growth or mutate. Within weeks or months, the area usually stabilizes back to baseline. Usually.

### Micro-Fracture
Not a full Realm-Fracture, but more than a Leak. A Micro-Fracture stays open for days to weeks, continuously pouring foreign matter — energies, terrain-altering minerals, biological organisms, and creatures — into the surrounding area. The terrain changes. Permanently. Rock formations that shouldn't exist grow overnight. Plants from other worlds take root. The soil chemistry shifts.

A Micro-Fracture site does not go back to normal when it closes. It becomes something else. If no one clears it, it becomes a Wild-Land.

### Dungeon Burst
When a dungeon's internal pressure exceeds its Core's containment capacity and ruptures outward. The dungeon doesn't disappear — it tears. The creatures inside pour out. The local energy spikes violently. The surrounding area takes on characteristics of the dungeon's interior environment. A forest dungeon that bursts leaves behind a forest that follows dungeon rules: the creatures respawn, the energy stays elevated, the terrain doesn't quite behave like normal land anymore.

Bursts are the most immediately dangerous event. Leaks and Micro-Fractures give some warning through DFU escalation. Bursts often don't.

---

## Wild-Land Classification

The ICA classifies Wild-Lands using a color-coded danger system, displayed on all official maps and the public ICA information network.

| Color | Name | Threat Level | Who Can Enter |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Green** | Controlled Zone | Minimal or suppressed | Civilians permitted with advisory |
| **Yellow** | Caution Zone | Low-moderate creature activity | Registered Corebearers recommended |
| **Orange** | Restricted Zone | Active creature presence, D–C rank | Licensed Corebearers only |
| **Red** | Exclusion Zone | High-density B–A rank activity | Hunter Corps operations only |
| **Black** | Forbidden Zone | Extreme, SS-possible, physics unreliable | ICA special operations, no civilian access |
| **Grey** | Dead Zone | Complete dimensional collapse or permanent fracture remnant | No sanctioned access |

Green Zones are common near cities — managed, periodically cleared, sometimes commercially used. Black and Grey Zones are rare and tend to stay on maps for a very long time.

---

## Wild-Land Creatures

Not everything that comes through is monstrous. This is one of the most important facts about Wild-Lands that the ICA underemphasizes in public communication — because the nuance complicates the "stay out, it's dangerous" message they prefer.

**Predatory creatures** — what most people picture. Foreign apex species that kill on sight, carry dimensional energies that are harmful to baseline humans, and form territorial ranges in Wild-Lands. These are what the Hunter Corps primarily handles.

**Neutral creatures** — large populations of creatures that are not inherently aggressive to humans. Many have meat that is, depending on the species, edible, delicious, or genuinely medicinal. Some carry materials in their bodies — secretions, horn compounds, bone structure — that have beneficial properties not found in Earth biology. A significant portion of the specialty ingredients market depends on Wild-Land creature harvesting.

**Exotic fauna** — creatures valued not for meat or materials but for what they are. Some Wild-Land species are extraordinarily beautiful. Some have abilities that make them desirable as companions or mounts. A market in Wild-Land exotic fauna has existed for centuries. The ICA regulates it imperfectly. It continues regardless.

---

## Creature Taming

Taming Wild-Land and dungeon creatures is a recognized profession. Not common — the skills required are specific, the danger is significant, and a significant percentage of taming attempts end badly for the human involved. But enough practitioners exist to have shaped institutions around it.

**Who tames:**
- Specialist Corebearers who develop abilities suited to creature management and communication
- Wealthy individuals who want powerful creatures as mounts or status symbols — some high-rank Corebearers use tamed creatures to travel, and a tamed A-rank creature as a mount is a statement that communicates your power level before you say a word
- Research-oriented Corebearers studying foreign creature biology
- Military applications — some Hunter Corps units work with tamed creatures for specific operational needs

**Taming Academies:**
The Wild-Heart Taming Institute in Mardu Frontier is the most respected formal institution. Several smaller academies exist in Aethelgard Reach and Vexton Nexus. There are also informal masters — experienced tamers who take apprentices rather than run institutions. The informal lineage often produces better field results than the formal programs, which the formal programs resent.

**The Taming Market:**
Exotic creatures change hands at the higher end of the black market more reliably than almost anything else. A correctly tamed, stable, high-rank creature is worth more than most high-grade dungeon material hauls. The Commission's open platform has made legal creature trading more accessible — it is now one of the most active categories on the platform.

---

## Why Normal Humans Travel Underground

The wild lands between cities make overland travel genuinely dangerous for anyone without Core enhancement. Baseline humans caught in a Yellow Zone without Corebearer escort have poor survival odds. Orange Zones or above are effectively impassable without licensed backup.

The solution built over 600 years is the **Null-Stone Tunnel Network** — underground transit corridors reinforced with Null-Stone, a material that absorbs Core-energy signature and suppresses dimensional interference. The tunnels are large enough for trains. The trains are powered by dimensional energy crystals harvested from dungeons. The network connects every major city and most secondary cities across the human regions.

**Practical details:**
- Travel between major cities takes hours rather than days
- The tunnels are brightly lit, maintained by NullPath Transit under ICA contract
- Civilian use requires no special certification — any person can buy a ticket
- Cargo runs on separate lines from passenger trains
- Emergency stations are spaced at regular intervals for situations where a train stops unexpectedly
- The network does not extend into Black or Grey Zones, obviously
- Some remote areas and smaller towns are not connected — residents of disconnected settlements live in a fundamentally different relationship with the Wild-Lands than city populations do

**The class dimension:** Tunnel travel is affordable for most city populations. It is effectively inaccessible to people in poor or remote communities who live too far from a station. These populations rely on Corebearers for escort when they need to move between settlements. The ICA's strategic neglect of underserved regions means some communities exist in near-complete isolation, surrounded by Wild-Lands with no reliable connection to the tunnel network.

## AETHELGARD REACH

**Character:** Lush mountains, dense forests, jungles in the south, rolling plains near the coast. Green, living, layered. The kind of place where something old is always watching from between the trees.

**Climate:** Temperate to subtropical. Humid in the forested interior. Cooler in the mountain ranges.

**Historical Significance:** This is where humanity first organized a proper counterattack against dungeon creatures during the Age of Reclamation. The term "first pushed back" refers to a specific battle — the Battle of Aethel Ridge — where a coalition of early Corebearers held a Tier-2 dungeon breach and stopped it from expanding into a populated valley. The site is now a national monument. Every Aethelgard child knows the name of the battle before they know the capitals of other regions.

**SSS-Rank Guardian:** Lady Elena Thorne, called "The Iron Matriarch." Publicly known as the guardian. Her actual history is not publicly known. She is approximately 340 years old — which is possible for high-rank Corebearers whose Core development slows aging — but her official biography begins only 200 years ago. The first 140 years are listed as "private records, sealed by personal request." She is the only SSS guardian who has ever refused an ICA directive, on two separate occasions. Both times the ICA backed down. Neither incident is in public records.

**Countries:**
- **Kest** — Capital: Oakhaven. Home to the [[Lord Markham Corebearer Academy]]. Famous for massive ancient oaks, some of which are so large that the first dungeon teams carved shelters inside them. The Kest government is old money — several of the founding families still hold significant political power. Oakhaven is the largest city in the Reach.
- **Varen** — Capital: Aethel-Core. Built atop a Tier-3 Domesticated Dungeon — one of the few Tier-3 dungeons fully under state control in any human region. Aethel-Core is effectively built around the dungeon. The city's lower districts are carved into the dungeon's outer boundary zone. Residents of those districts are given small monthly stipends and slightly lower rent in exchange for accepting the elevated dimensional energy in their neighborhood.

**Major Cities:**
- **Oakhaven** — Administrative capital of Kest. Population ~1.2 million. Seat of the Markham Academy. Has the feel of old stone architecture modernized through additions rather than replacement — very few buildings were torn down when the city grew, they were just built around.
- **Aethel-Core** — The dungeon city. Population ~800,000. Glows faintly at night from the Tier-3 dungeon's energy bleed. Tourists visit specifically to see this. Residents are used to it.
- **Kestrel** — Port city on the eastern coast. Population ~600,000. Major trade hub for sea routes between Aethelgard and Argent-Palar.
- **Northbridge** — Mountain city, sits at the junction of three major mountain passes. Population ~400,000. Heavily fortified — this is where most of the supply lines for the northern border with the Glacial Hive run through.
- **Low-Hale** — Southern city near the Esten Province border. Population ~350,000. Known for its large Corebearer market — the largest licensed market in the Reach.
- **Drenfort** — Jungle-edge city in the south. Population ~200,000. High dungeon density in the surrounding region — used as a training ground for D and C rank teams.
- **Valis** — A smaller city near the border of Fenner Basin. Known for producing a disproportionate number of B-rank Corebearers — something about the local dungeon cluster seems to accelerate development.

**Major Academies:**
- [[Lord Markham Corebearer Academy]] — Oakhaven, Kest. One of the oldest, most prestigious, most ethically complex academies in the human regions. Full detail in Part Seven.
- **Aethel Military Institute (AMI)** — Aethel-Core, Varen. Built specifically to train Corebearers who will serve in the regional Hunter Corps and border defense. Less prestigious than Markham academically, but produces more frontline fighters. Graduates are highly sought by the Hunter Corps and by mercenary firms operating near the Glacial Hive border.
- **Greenspire Academy** — Drenfort. Specializes in dungeon ecology and monster-material harvesting. Not known for combat training. Graduates fill research, assessment, and resource extraction roles. Funded primarily by the Varen Dungeon Authority and two major corporations.

**Major Guilds:**
- **The Oakroot Guild** — The largest registered hunter guild in the Reach. Based in Oakhaven. Around 4,000 active members. Known for reliable mid-tier operations — Tier-2 and Tier-3 clearances. Not flashy. The guild that gets the job done without drama.
- **Thornspear Company** — A smaller elite guild, around 400 members, all B-rank or above. Based in Northbridge. Takes border contracts — clearing dungeon overflow near the Glacial Hive boundary. Paid significantly above guild average. Mortality rate is significantly above guild average.
- **The Verdant Circle** — A niche guild focused specifically on dungeon ecology, not combat. About 600 members, mostly D and C rank with specialized knowledge. They provide assessment services for dungeon reclassification proceedings. The ICA relies on them for second-opinion classification reports. Several members are ex-ICAID who quietly left the organization.

**Major Corporations:**
- **Aether Resources Ltd.** — The largest dungeon-materials processing company in the Reach. Controls seven Tier-1 and two Tier-2 dungeons through long-term ICA concession agreements. Their headquarters is in Kestrel. Their chief operations officer has been the same person for 60 years — which is possible at high rank but still noticed.
- **Markwood Industries** — Wood, construction, and bio-material development. Uses dungeon-grown timber from Tier-1 forest dungeons extensively. Products are used across three regions. Based in Low-Hale. Has endowment agreements with Markham Academy.
- **Ironvale Corp** — Equipment manufacturing. Produces Corebearer armor and tools for the Reach's Hunter Corps. Mid-size company, but one of the only producers licensed to use Aethel-Core's dimensional alloy — a material that comes from the Tier-3 dungeon's energy output and cannot be replicated anywhere else.

**Ancient Bloodlines:** *(full detail in Part Five)*
- **House Thornwell** — Based in Oakhaven. The Thornwell family produced one of the first Corebearers after the Great Emergence. Their current matriarch is Mirela Thornwell — peak SS-rank, approaching SSS. The public knows the Thornwells as a powerful old family with significant political connections. They do not know Mirela is likely over 300 years old, or that the Thornwell line has maintained continuous contact with middle-realm entities for approximately 150 years.
- **The Valen Compact** — Not a single family but a network of six families that descended from a group of early Corebearers who survived the Dark Century together. ## MARDU FRONTIER

Located northwest of the central human world, bounded by Fenner Basin to the northeast, Aethelgard to the southeast, the Jade Hegemony to the far west, and the Abyssal Thronedom's ocean to the west.

**The Wall:** The most defining physical feature of Mardu Frontier. ## FENNER BASIN

**Character:** The world eats because of Fenner Basin. Most of humanity's food supply — both standard agriculture and dungeon-grown produce — originates here. The landscape is vast, mostly flat or gently rolling, separated from the Mardu Frontier by a sea gap with chains of islands between them. The sea gap and those islands make Fenner feel genuinely separate from the rest of the world — a place that the larger conflicts of the human regions mostly reach as news rather than direct experience. It is the most peaceful of the eight great regions by a significant margin, and the people here know it, and are quietly grateful for it in the way people are grateful for things they know others don't have.

**Volcanos and Rocky Mountains:** The interior of Fenner has a mountain range with active volcanic activity — milder than Mordant's but present. The dungeon formation rate in Fenner is high because the volcanic dimensional energy concentrations create favorable conditions. These are managed as agricultural dungeons — Tier-1 and Tier-2 facilities that produce dungeon-grown food crops rather than combat materials.

**Climate:** Temperate with significant regional variation. Coastal zones are mild and wet. Interior is warmer and drier. The volcanic interior creates pockets of geothermal warmth that enable crop growth in areas that would otherwise be too cold.

**SSS-Rank Guardian:** Bram the Golem. The most unusual of the SSS guardians. The "Golem" is not a nickname — it refers to the fact that Bram's physical form is not fully human. At some point in his development — the public record is deliberately unclear — his Core and body underwent a fundamental transformation. He is human in origin and in mind. His body is partially formed from dungeon-material alloys that have integrated with his biological structure. He is enormous, slower than other SSS guardians, and reportedly completely unmovable from any location he chooses to hold. He is also, by most accounts, the most genuinely good-natured of the SSS guardians. He has never been involved in any political controversy. He grows vegetables in a garden adjacent to his residence. This is considered a charming detail by the public and a deeply reassuring fact by anyone who knows what he is capable of.

**Countries:**
- **Mallow** — Capital: Sun-Field. The main agricultural zone. Surrounded by what Fenner calls "Golden Dungeons" — Tier-1 Domesticated facilities that produce enhanced crops, medicinal plants, and food materials that grow at accelerated rates under dungeon energy influence.
- **Bennic** — Capital: River-Gate. Trade and logistics. The river systems of Bennic connect to the port infrastructure that moves Fenner's agricultural output to the rest of the world.

**Major Cities:**
- **Sun-Field** — The agricultural capital. Population ~1.1 million. The architecture here is older and less modified than in other regions — Fenner has less reason to constantly rebuild, so buildings last longer. Has a very high percentage of Dormant citizens relative to Corebearers, which is unusual for a major city. The agricultural industry doesn't require awakened individuals for most of its operations.
- **River-Gate** — Trade hub. Population ~900,000. Built around a confluence of three rivers that connect to the coast. The port infrastructure is significant — most of Fenner's agricultural output leaves through River-Gate.
- **Willow** — Inland agricultural city. Population ~400,000. Named for the willow trees that line the river running through it — these are dungeon-grown willows from a Tier-1 forest dungeon, which means they are larger, longer-lived, and have minor dimensional properties that make the wood unusually resilient for certain applications.
- **Hearth** — Southern city near the Wall. Population ~350,000. Has permanent Hunter Corps presence for Wall maintenance support. The city grew around the Wall garrison.
- **Stone-Brook** — City near the volcanic interior. Population ~250,000. The geothermal energy here is used for both heat and as an alternative to dungeon energy in some industrial applications. Has a small but significant dungeon research community studying volcanic dungeon formation.

**Sea Islands:** The island chain between Fenner Basin and Mardu Frontier are mostly uninhabited except for small fishing communities and two ICA monitoring stations. Some of the islands have dimensional phenomena from Micro-Fracture activity in the sea gap. Two islands are classified as DL-3 zones and have been evacuated.

**Major Guilds:**
- **The Harvest Guild** — 4,000 members. Primarily operates agricultural dungeons — clearing, managing, harvesting. Not a combat-focused guild. The most economically important guild in the world by the measure of what their operations feed.
- **The Sea Watch** — 1,800 members. Hunter guild focused on monitoring the island chain between Fenner and Mardu for dimensional events — Micro-Fractures, Dimensional Leaks, sea creature migrations from the gap. Lower mortality than Wall operations. Considered stable work rather than glorious work. The guild that keeps things quiet so Fenner can keep farming.

**Major Corporations:**
- **Fenner Agricultural Consortium** — The dominant agricultural entity. Controls approximately 40% of Fenner's dungeon-farm output. Has contracts with food distribution networks across five regions. Politically powerful in ways that are disproportionate to its geographic location — food supply leverage is significant.
- **River Basin Trading** — The dominant logistics and trade company for agricultural output. Controls most of the shipping infrastructure between Fenner and the other regions. Unglamorous, essential, quietly profitable.

**Ancient Bloodlines:**
- **The Bram Line** — Bram's actual family. Unlike most guardians, Bram's family situation is complicated by the nature of his transformation — he has outlived every relative from his original family multiple times. He maintains connections with the descendants of his original bloodline, none of whom have his specific capability. They manage a large farming estate adjacent to his residence.
- **The Old Families of Mallow** — Several farming families that have held land in Mallow since the region was first settled after the Age of Reclamation. They predated the current dungeon-farming system and adapted to it. Three of them now own significant Dungeon Farm licenses. They are wealthy, conservative, and almost entirely uninterested in anything beyond Fenner's borders.

## SOLACE MERIDIAN

**Character:** Maritime. Southern. A continent surrounded by the most dangerous ocean in the human world — the Abyssal Ocean, where the Abyssal Thronedom's influence extends to the water itself. The Solace Meridian exists in a permanent state of managed tension with the ocean around it. Everything in the region's culture reflects this — the architecture faces the sea without hiding from it, the Corebearers train in and around water, and the culture has a specific relationship with danger that is less about hardness and more about acceptance.

Located south of the Jade Hegemony and Vexton Nexus, with sea borders to the Abyssal Thronedom.

**Climate:** Warm temperate to subtropical. Coastal humidity. Significant rainfall.

**SSS-Rank Guardian:** Soren Kai, called "The Tide-Caller." The only guardian who regularly operates in the ocean. Soren Kai is at home in water in a way that suggests the Core development pathway that produced their abilities is specifically sea-adapted. The ICA has studied this and has not reached public conclusions. Soren Kai does not discuss it.

**Countries:**

It is the most unusual major city in the human world from an architectural standpoint.

**Major Cities:**
- **Azure-Port** — The floating capital. Population ~1.3 million. Has a higher percentage of Corebearers per capita than almost any other major city, because ordinary civilians without Core enhancement find the maritime environment genuinely dangerous. - **White-Cliff** — Research and naval command. Population ~800,000. The most advanced research facilities for Null-Stone application and dimensional technology outside of Vexton Nexus.

**Major Academies:**
- **Azure Maritime Academy** — Azure-Port. Specializes in maritime Corebearer training, underwater combat, and the specific skills of operating near the Abyssal Thronedom's border. Produces graduates who are exceptional in water-adjacent operations and less focused on conventional dungeon work.
- **White-Cliff Research Institute** — White-Cliff. Not a traditional combat academy. Focused on Null-Stone research, dimensional technology, and the applied sciences of making Corebearers harder to detect. **Ancient Bloodlines:**
- **The Kai Line** — Soren Kai's family. Established maritime history. The water-adapted Core development that Soren Kai embodies appears in lesser degrees across the family — not SSS-level, but noticeably above average for water-adjacent abilities. The family does not publicize this.
- **House Meridian** — The oldest family in Lannis. Present before Azure-Port was built. Holds the original land rights to the portion of seafloor that Azure-Port's foundation platforms are anchored to. This makes them technically the landlord of the city. # PART FIVE — FOREIGN INFESTED ZONES

## THE ABYSSAL CONVERGENCE
### *First Fracture — Hadal Origin World*

**Location:** Far west — the open Pacific. A vast whirlpool-ringed ocean zone.
**Nature:** A compressed ocean-realm folded into Earth's reality. The water here runs deep in ways that defy geography.
**Border:** The Pressure Wall — a dimensional barrier of layered gravitational-water pressure their own construction, not a natural phenomenon. Ships that approach without specific clearance implode. Even Corebearers describe crossing near the Pressure Wall as "heavy" in a way that isn't purely physical

**The Truth About the First Fracture:** The Thronedom  entities were not the first to arrive after the First Fracture opened. The Nagarae found the fracture and stabilized it from the inside before humans understood what had happened. The Pressure Wall is their creation. Humanity has been protected by the Nagarae for 800 years while calling them an infested zone.

**The Secret Treaty:** The Nagarae protect Earth's oceans from dimensional collapse. In exchange, humanity supplies dimensional crystals — materials the Nagarae use to maintain communication with what remains of their origin realm. The treaty is 500 years old. Four people on Earth currently know its full terms. The Nagarae have honored it without deviation. Humanity's side has been honored imperfectly twice, both times resolved through private diplomatic negotiation that did not appear in any public record.

---

### Tri-Throne — The Pressure Compact

Power in the Thronedom is held by three ruling entities under an agreement enforced by the ocean itself. Breaking the Compact triggers depth inversion — a localized reversal of pressure that crushes the violator from within. This has happened three times in the Convergence's history. Nobody breaks the Compact twice.

**The Abyssal Nagarae**
Golden-eyed, multi-limbed serpentine royals. They govern law, diplomacy, and long-term planning. Cannot survive prolonged air exposure — the few who have appeared on the surface did so through significant dimensional aid. They communicate through water resonance rather than sound. Arrogant, cultured, and genuinely ancient. The eldest of the ruling Nagarae are older than the fracture itself — they came from the origin realm carrying personal histories that predate anything in Earth's written records.

**The Leviathan Ancients**
Colossal, semi-sentient beings that are older than the fracture. Each Leviathan is a walking treaty — their presence stabilizes regions of the Thronedom that would otherwise be environmentally chaotic. If a Leviathan awakens fully — moves from its semi-dormant state into full activation — the fracture destabilizes. Everyone dies if they fight. This knowledge keeps the peace between entities that would otherwise consider each other rivals.

**The Thalassar Deep Elves**
Bioluminescent elves adapted to total darkness. Rune-etched, focused on abyssal sorcery and ocean-current manipulation. They function as administrators and scholars — the intellectual infrastructure of the Convergence. They have a specific hatred for surface elves (the High Sylvans of the Jade Hegemony) rooted in a conflict from their origin realm that predates the fracture. They respect strength entirely and sentiment not at all.

**Sub-species:**
- **Bathyshell Colossi** — City-sized crustacean titans. Their shells form the physical infrastructure of Thronedom settlements. Neutral by nature, programmable loyalty, serve as construction and defense.
- **Siren Clades** — Biological sonic weapons. They can shatter organs, Cores, and constructs with focused vocal output. Restricted by treaty from mass singing. Used as intelligence operatives and interrogators.

**External Contact:** All ruling races possess Abyssal Relay Organs — a biological mechanism that allows communication with their origin realm. Using them to summon higher beings collapses the fracture. They maintain communication. They do not summon.

**Why They Haven't Invaded:** Their world is gone. The ocean they are in is the most stable environment they have access to. Destroying Earth's stability destroys the only place they can exist. Even the most aggressive Nagarae factions understand this. It does not make them friendly. It makes them calculated.

## THE Jade Hegemony
### *Second Fracture — World-Tree Continuum*

**Location:** South-center-left — a vast continent formed from the fracture's geological impact, larger than any single human region. The megaflora here is alien and enormous.
**Nature:** A world-tree ecology folded into Earth. The biology here is not Earth biology wearing a different suit. It is a completely different evolutionary lineage that happens to use similar materials.
**Border:** The Living Ring — a continuously migrating belt of megaflora that rearranges the geography at the border daily. No permanent entry point exists. The Ring moves. Attempting to map it produces maps that are already outdated.

**The Secret About the Jade Hegemony:** The Jade Hegemony is not the aggressive power the ICA presents it as. The High Sylvans are territorial — deeply, fundamentally territorial — but the Mandala's collective leadership has maintained its internal ecological peace specifically because full-scale war would trigger the Mandala's own self-correction mechanism. The faction that wants expansion — specifically, one High Sylvan noble house — is real, active, and dangerous. The majority of the Mandala's ruling circle is against it. The agents causing trouble in Vexton Nexus and Aethelgard Reach are operating from that one noble house's resources without full Mandala authorization. The ruling circle knows about it. They have not stopped it because stopping it would require internal conflict, which the Mandala is structured to avoid at all costs.

---

### Fivefold Accord — Ruling Circle

Power distributed across ecological dominions. No single ruler. The ecology itself enforces balance — large-scale conflict triggers a cascade that kills aggressors first.

**The High Sylvans**
Tall, silver-haired, wheat-skin "garden gods." They view planets as crops — not for eating, but for cultivation. They are not hostile to Earth out of malice. They are frustrated by what they perceive as a poorly-managed world being hoarded by a species that doesn't understand its potential. This perspective does not make them benign. Their arrogance is complete and their patience is extremely long.

**The Worldroot Dwarves**
Ancient subterranean race fused with planetary root systems. They forge bio-metal and living fortresses. Cannot survive without stone contact — their physiology requires geological grounding. They control underground logistics and trade. Genuinely ancient intelligence in an unassuming form.

**The Verdant Titans**
Living mountains grown from world-tree seeds. Rarely move. Each one stabilizes a region of the Mandala. If one is killed, the biome it anchors collapses — which means every faction in the Mandala has a survival interest in protecting them, even factions that disagree on everything else.

**The Mycelial Synod**
A distributed intelligence spanning the Mandala's entire territory. Controls decay, rebirth, information flow, and healing. Functions as neutral arbiters for internal disputes. Cannot be fully destroyed — the network exists across too many nodes. Their information about what happens inside the Mandala is comprehensive. Their information about what happens outside is limited. They are the entity most likely to have accurate intelligence about the current situation, and their assessment of the fracture-sealing 19 years ago is that they have no adequate explanation for it.

**The Skybound Aviora**
Four-winged, rune-feathered aerial aristocracy. Control weather and airspace across the Mandala. Mercenary culture — loyal to contracts, not ideology. Have sold information to humans on three occasions through intermediaries. The ICA knows this. They have used it.

**External Contact:** Each ruling race maintains Seed-Anchors — biological links to their origin realm. Communication is possible. Summoning is not. The Seed-Anchors carry a specific limitation: they function in one direction. Information can flow from origin realm to fracture. But the beings in the origin realm cannot use the Anchors to come through. The fracture's energy density won't support it.

## THE IRON-SAND SULTANATE
### *Third Fracture — Ash-Forged War Realm*

**Location:** The Equatorial Scorchbelt — between the first and second fractures
**Nature:** An ash-and-fire ecology. The sky is permanently particulate — light-eating ash that creates permanent twilight conditions beneath the Ash Firmament.
**Border:** The Cinder Cloud / Ash Firmament — a layered atmospheric barrier of intelligent particulate matter. Not inert. It responds to pressure. Aircraft cannot fly through it. Corebearers who have entered describe the air itself resisting them.

**The Truth About the Sultanate:** The Rakshasa clans are warmongering in the honest sense — they love war as an experience, find a worthy opponent to be a genuine gift, and measure personal value through combat achievement. What they are not is mindless. The Rakshasa leadership understands that full-scale war against humanity would eventually destroy the Sultanate. They don't want that. They want the fight. The distinction is meaningful.

The ICA has been supplying the Sultanate with Void-Metal for 150 years to keep them fighting other zones instead of humans. The Sultanate knows this is what the metal is for. They have accepted it because the other zones are also good opponents. The arrangement is, in their view, a fair trade. In the ICA's view, it is a successful deterrence strategy. In the Jade Hegemony's view, it is humans arming their enemies and hoping the target stays pointed elsewhere. The Mandala has not shared this view with humans.

The annual sparring arrangement between Varek the Unbroken and Magnus Kruger is known to approximately 40 people across the human and Sultanate power structures. It represents the most functional diplomatic relationship between any human and non-human sovereign entity, running entirely outside official channels, maintained by mutual respect between two individuals who would kill each other if their peoples went to war.

### Warlord Balance — Ruling Axis

Power maintained through mutual deterrence, not peace.

**The Rakshasa Clans**
Horned, rune-scarred warlords with crimson skin. They gain power by consuming Cores — which is the source of the "Core-Eater" designation humans use for them. This capability is the reason they are feared and the reason other factions within the Sultanate give them room. They rule martial law and respect strength only. Negotiations with Rakshasa clans work through one mechanism: demonstrating you are worth talking to. Magnus Kruger is, in their assessment, worth talking to.

**The Ashen Efreet**
Semi-incorporeal fire entities bound to obsidian cores. They control energy flow and the industrial furnace systems that process the Scorchbelt's resources. Cannot be killed conventionally — their consciousness is distributed between their physical manifestation and their obsidian core. Destroying the manifestation does not kill them. Destroying the core does. Very few humans know where any Efreet's core is located. Manipulative, ancient, and with a patience that makes even Nagarae diplomatic timelines look short.

**The Umbral Sun-Elves**
Fire-scarred elves descended from a Sylvan lineage that was exposed to the fracture event and transformed by it. They worship scorched light — the specific quality of light that comes through the Ash Firmament. Deep hatred for the Jade Hegemony's High Sylvans, which is mutual and which predates the fractures. Elite tacticians. The ICA Intelligence Division considers them the most dangerous military planners in any foreign zone.

**Supporting Species:**
- **Behemoth Carriers** — Mountain-scale creatures with cities built on their backs. Neutral transport and siege platforms. Cannot refuse contracts — their psychology is bound to the contract system. Memory-bound loyalty: once contracted, the loyalty is part of their memory structure and cannot be broken without memory disruption.
- **Scorpion Laminar** — Venom-tail Lamia aristocrats. Run intelligence networks and maintain poison alchemy. The Sultanate's primary intelligence gathering capability against human territories is the Lamiar network.

**External Contact:** Core Shrines — concentrated dimensional anchors linked to origin realms. Functional for communication. Cannot be used for transit. The Rakshasa have tested this limit multiple times. The testing has never been mentioned in any communication to humans.

## THE GLACIAL HIVE
### *Fourth Fracture — Frozen Time World*

**Location:** Far north/northeast — beyond the Glacial Blockade above Argent-Palar. Arctic territory.
**Status:** Officially "Closed" — the fracture was sealed 19 years ago. The entities that came through before it sealed are still there. The GLACIAL HIVE exists as a bounded territory: they cannot expand further, they cannot leave through the now-sealed fracture, and their origin realm communication is functional but limited by the seal's interference.
**Border:** The Stillness Line — the boundary where ambient temperature drops to a level where dimensional energy begins to crystallize. Motion slows measurably. Time appears to behave differently very close to the Line — not in a physically measurable sense that ICA instruments can quantify, but in the consistent reported experience of every human who has approached it.

**The Truth About the GLACIAL HIVE:** The entities here are the most contained and the most frustrated of the four foreign zones. They cannot expand. Their origin realm is inaccessible. They know something sealed their fracture, and they know it was not the human operation — their temporal awareness gives them a different perspective on events than human or other foreign zone species. The Chrono-Glacials, specifically, experienced the sealing from a time-adjacent position and have information about what actually happened that they have not shared with anyone. What they know is that the sealing came from something that carries an extremely old resonance — older than any Corebearer development timeline. They have been trying to understand this for 19 years.

**The Glacial Blockade** visible on the map is the ICA's Heat-Core barrier system — a line of dimensional energy generators maintaining elevated temperatures that prevent the Stillness Line from passively expanding southward toward inhabited territory.

### Cold Accord — Ruling Covenant

**The Frost-Lords**
Living ice sovereigns who are embodiments of frozen law. Absolute control over temperature. Slow but inevitable — a Frost-Lord in motion is not fast, but stopping one requires force that most humans at current development levels cannot generate. Their speech freezes moisture in the air. Entire cities in the GLACIAL HIVE form as a consequence of where a Frost-Lord has spoken at length.

**The Chrono-Glacials**
Beings partially frozen in different temporal states. They experience time non-linearly — some are partially in the past, some partially in a future that may or may not arrive. Appear fractured and distorted in visual perception. Cannot be safely approached by Corebearers below S-rank — the temporal distortion they create is passive and involuntary and will damage anything below the threshold. They carry information that nobody else in any fracture zone has: they experienced the sealing of their fracture from a time-adjacent angle and have partial awareness of what caused it.

**The Ymirkin**
Massive intelligent beings descended from the builder caste of their origin realm. They construct the GLACIAL HIVE's physical infrastructure. Immense endurance. Loyal to the Covenant with a sincerity that makes them distinct from the political calculations of every other ruling species in any fracture zone.

**The Rime Arachnarchs**
Crystal spiders with territorial domain webs — they claim territory through silk that functions as dimensional sensor networks. Cannot be safely entered without Rime Arachnarch permission. Used for assassination operations within the GLACIAL HIVE. Have expressed interest in expanding their web territory beyond the Stillness Line — an interest the Cold Accord has not formally prohibited and not formally permitted.

**External Contact:** Cryo-Anchors — dimensional cold-memory storage that maintains a link to the origin realm. The seal on the Fourth Fracture has degraded their Cryo-Anchor function by approximately 40%, which means their communication with the origin realm is partial and slow. This is deeply inconvenient for them. They have not told anyone how inconvenient.

# PART SIX — ANCIENT BLOODLINES
### *The Progenitors — What They Are and Why Nobody Knows*

The public term "old families" describes wealthy families with long histories. The actual entity being described here is something different: the descendants of the first Corebearers — the individuals who awakened during or shortly after the Great Emergence — who survived long enough for Core development to fundamentally alter their relationship with time.

The official term used within these families, in private communications, is **The Progenitor Lines**.

---

## What Progenitors Are

The oldest Progenitors are not quite human in the baseline biological sense. Centuries of high-level Core development have altered their physiology at a fundamental level. Their aging has slowed to the point where they effectively do not age at functional human rates. Their Cores have integrated with their bodies differently than the Core integration of younger Corebearers — the boundary between their Core and their physical structure is less distinct. Some of the most senior Progenitors have physical characteristics that are not quite human, which is why they are so careful about public exposure.

The public does not know these individuals still exist. The ICA does know — or rather, the ICA's classified records contain evidence of several Progenitors still being alive. The formal ICA position is to not address this evidence and to manage the specific individuals through relationship rather than confrontation. Confronting a 600-year-old Corebearer with unknown capability ceiling is not something the ICA's Ten Pillars have ever formally recommended doing.

---

## Known Progenitor Lines

**House Thornwell** (Aethelgard Reach)
The Thornwell family produced one of the first Corebearers after the Great Emergence. The current matriarch, Mirela Thornwell, is approximately 300 years old. Her publicly-presented age suggests she is in her late 60s. The discrepancy is managed through careful use of a legal identity succession system the ICA designed — technically for high-rank individuals whose lifespans exceed normal expectations, practically for Progenitors who need to maintain a presentable public face.

Mirela holds peak SS-rank approaching SSS. She has been approaching SSS for approximately 60 years. The ceiling appears to be genuine — not performance, not suppression. Something about the Thornwell Core development pathway produces extraordinary ceiling approach without final breakthrough. No one has explained this satisfactorily.

Mirela is the person who performed the binding ritual on Marc. She did not believe it was wrong at the time. She believes, with the specific quality of belief that comes from decades of processing a decision rather than simply defending it, that she made the correct choice given her information. She does not believe the same decision would be correct with full information. The distinction matters to her. It will not matter to Marc.

**The Reiss Line** (Orenthellion)
High Chancellor Reiss has held his position for approximately 180 years under different formal identities. He is the most politically integrated Progenitor — his entire existence is structured around maintaining institutional power rather than stepping back from it. His legal framework — designed originally for other Progenitors, ironically now mostly used by him — is the primary mechanism by which several ancient families maintain continuity across multiple generations.

Reiss is not a villain in the classical sense. He is a person who has spent 180 years managing a world that would be significantly worse without management, who has made accommodations and compromises that compound across decades in ways that have become impossible to fully track, and who has been in power long enough that he genuinely cannot remember what his original goals were clearly enough to evaluate whether he has achieved them.

**The Valen Compact** (Aethelgard Reach)
Six families that survived the Dark Century together. Three of the original survivors are still alive — the oldest approximately 600 years old. They operate with a patience that makes other power structures seem impulsive. The Compact does not seek dramatic action. It seeks continuity. Its dungeon access, resource holdings, and political connections are substantial by any measure.

**The Forge Founders** (Vexton Nexus)
Three families present at the founding of Star-Forge. Two original members still alive. Their involvement in the manufacturing sector is not driven by profit — at their level of resource accumulation, profit is not the motivator. It is driven by the same thing most long-lived Progenitors eventually fall back on: the specific comfort of controlling something familiar.

**House Meridian** (Solace Meridian)
The oldest family in Lannis. The "landlord of the city" arrangement is not merely symbolic — the original member who holds the land rights is still alive, which is the reason the lease is renegotiated every 50 years rather than resolved. The current "representative" of House Meridian at negotiations is, in fact, the original. They present differently each time. The same ICA diplomat has handled four consecutive negotiations over 200 years and has not officially noted the physical similarities because doing so would require explaining things he does not want to explain.

**The Deep Root Families** (Mardu Frontier)
Three families. One original Progenitor still alive — the oldest of any confirmed Progenitor at approximately 620 years. She does not leave her family's estate anymore. The dungeon on the property that the ICA has been diplomatically not-confronting for 400 years has been managed by her personally since it formed. The ICA's reluctance to formally address the situation is partly strategic and partly something more honest: the ICA's senior leadership knows she could have submitted to registration at any point in the last 600 years and chose not to, and the calculation of what confronting her would cost has never come out favorably enough to attempt it.

# PART SEVEN — MAJOR GUILDS & CORPORATIONS

## Cross-Regional Guilds

**The Apex Circle**
The most exclusive hunter guild in the world. 50 members total. All A-rank or above. No open recruitment — invitation only, and invitations require unanimous existing member approval. Takes contracts that no other guild has the rank threshold to fulfill. Based formally in Sovereign-Grand but operates everywhere. **Irongate Alliance**
A mid-tier guild cooperative — not a single guild, but an alliance of twelve medium-sized guilds that pool resources for large-scale operations. 18,000 total members across the alliance. The alliance structure allows them to take contracts that require large numbers without having a single-organization chain of command. Complex internal politics. Effective operations.

**The Silent Company**
A guild that does not advertise. Has no public recruitment. Corebearers join through referral only, and referrals are not discussed. Specializes in operations that require discretion — escort, extraction, asset recovery, intelligence support. Has a working relationship with the ICA Intelligence Division that neither party confirms. Estimated membership: 300-400. Actual membership: unknown. Operations: unlogged by design.

## Cross-Regional Corporations

**The Benefactor Council**
Not a corporation — an association of the twelve largest megacorporations that fund ICA Academy grants. Membership is by invitation, requires a minimum annual grant commitment, and conveys specific privileges: seats at academy sponsor meetings, preferential access to top-graduate recruitment, and ICA regulatory consultation rights. The Benefactor Council has significant informal influence over ICA policy in areas that affect corporate dungeon operations.

**Continental Materials Exchange (CME)**
The primary licensed market for dungeon materials across the human regions. The ICA's registration requirement channels most dungeon material sales through the CME or its regional affiliates. The exchange takes a processing fee on every transaction. This fee, across the entire market, generates revenue that rivals national budgets. The CME is technically a private entity. Its board composition requires ICA approval.

**NullPath Transit**
The private company that maintains the Null-Stone tunnel network infrastructure under ICA contract. Technically subordinate to ICA operational authority. Practically, NullPath's engineers are the only people with full technical knowledge of how the network operates. The ICA could nationalize it. They have chosen not to because the engineering knowledge would not transfer automatically and network failures during a transition period would be catastrophic.

# PART NINE — UNDERGROUND & SHADOW GROUPS

> *These organizations exist in the space between what the ICA permits and what it can reach.*

---

## The Hollowed

**What They Are:** The most dangerous and least understood underground organization operating on Earth. The Hollowed are former Corebearers — or beings that were once Corebearers — whose Cores have been transformed through a process that human science cannot fully explain. They are still capable. They are no longer recognizable as human by their energy signatures.

**What They Do:** The Hollowed extract Cores from living Corebearers. The technique is unknown to mainstream human science — how they know it is a question for later arcs, related to middle-realm contact. Extracted Cores are sold through a layered distribution network to buyers whose identities are protected through cut-outs and intermediaries.

**Who Joins:** Primarily survivors of communities destroyed by Dimensional Leaks or Micro-Fractures that the Hunter Corps did not reach in time. Some joined voluntarily — people who lost everything to a dungeon event and found the ICA's response inadequate, or people who were made promises the ICA broke. Others were coerced — offered protection or resources in communities where the alternative was death, with the cost of that protection being participation.

The moral spectrum of the membership is wide. Some members believe what they're doing is justified revenge against an ICA that abandoned their people. Some are simply criminals who found an employer. Some were victims who gradually became perpetrators without a clear moment where they noticed the transition.

**Operations:** Small, mobile, anonymous. The Hollowed operate in teams of three to five. They identify targets — mid-rank Corebearers who won't be immediately missed, or lower-rank Corebearers in underserved areas — conduct surveillance, execute extraction operations, and move. No fixed bases. Safe houses only. Communication through a relay system that uses dungeon creature biological components as encryption medium.

**The Buyers:** Classified. Some buyers are collectors — individuals with enough wealth and enough detachment from consequences to treat Cores as luxury objects. Some are researchers attempting to understand Core structure outside ICA oversight. Some are connected to middle-realm entities who want Earth Cores for purposes that Earth's leadership would find alarming if they knew.

**ICA Awareness:** The ICA knows the Hollowed exist. They have been unable to trace the full network. They have apprehended individual teams — typically when a team makes an error in target selection and takes someone whose disappearance is noticed and investigated. The ICA's public position is that Core extraction is a rare criminal practice performed by isolated actors. This position is deliberate. Acknowledging the true scale would require acknowledging how much of the human world the ICA doesn't actually control.

---

## The Fracture Witnesses

**What They Are:** A philosophical-terrorist organization. The Fracture Witnesses believe that the current order — the ICA's managed stability, the dungeon industry, the political stalemate — is a deception that masks an inevitable planetary collapse. They believe humanity needs to be forced to confront this reality before it's too late to do anything meaningful.

**The Compelling Part of Their Argument:** They are partially right. The world is not as stable as the ICA presents. The dungeon system is a stopgap against dimensional pressure that will eventually fail without more fundamental intervention. The ICA does suppress this information. The Witnesses have access to some genuine suppressed data, which makes their messaging to potential recruits more effective than it would be if they were simply wrong.

**The Terrible Part of Their Methods:** They believe that controlled crises — dungeon overflow events deliberately triggered, Dimensional Leaks allowed to expand, public sites targeted — will force the ICA to disclose the truth rather than maintain the stability fiction. The logic is that a population that knows the truth will demand real solutions rather than managed decline. The practice is that people die in these events who did not choose to participate in the Witnesses' revelation strategy.

**Operations:** Cell-based structure. Individual cells do not know other cells exist. Communication flows upward through single contact points. The leadership is three people, all former ICA employees, who have never been identified by name in any ICA investigation. Their motivation was not cynical from the beginning. It has become harder to characterize over time.

**ICA Response:** Classified active investigation at highest priority. The challenge is the cell structure — every captured member knows only their own cell's operations and one contact point. The contact points have been found to be dead ends. The leadership has maintained anonymity for 23 years.

---

## The Unregistered

Not an organization. A category. The Unregistered are individuals who have awakened Cores outside the ICA registration system and have chosen, or been forced, to remain outside it. The population is estimated at between 8,000 and 40,000 — the range is wide because the ICA's methodology for estimating the unregistered is limited by the definition of the problem.

Most Unregistered are not political. They are people in underserved regions where registration infrastructure doesn't exist or doesn't function adequately, people who awakened before they had access to registration facilities, people fleeing specific circumstances who cannot register without revealing their location, or people who had bad experiences with ICA authority and made a practical choice.

A small fraction are deliberate. Former registrants who removed themselves from the system, or individuals with specific reasons to want ICA visibility suppressed. Ancient family members who maintain registration through intermediaries while operating personally without direct ICA tracking.

The Unregistered community, where it exists as a community, operates through informal networks — specific markets in specific cities that don't participate in the CME, dungeon facilities operated by ancient families or independent actors without ICA licensing, medical practitioners who treat Core injuries off-record.

---

## The Gray Market Brokers

Not an organization in the formal sense. A loose professional network of individuals who facilitate transactions between the official ICA-licensed economy and the spaces around it. Gray Market Brokers are not primarily criminals — many of what they facilitate is technically legal or in a regulatory gray area. They are the people who know how to get high-grade materials to an unregistered Corebearer, how to move dungeon resources between regions without triggering the CME's tax system, how to access a privately-held dungeon without leaving an ICA record.

They operate openly enough that most mid-tier Corebearers know how to reach one if they need to. The ICA is aware of their existence and operations. The ICA's enforcement against Gray Market Brokers focuses on the edges — the transactions that are clearly illegal — and leaves the middle ground largely alone, because the middle ground includes transactions that serve populations the ICA relies on.

# THE COMMISSION & THE SILENT ACCORD

> *Two organizations. One face, one shadow. The most interesting power structure outside the ICA — and the one the ICA is most uncertain how to handle.*

## THE COMMISSION
### *The Open Platform*

**Full Name:** The Commission for Open Contracts
**Common Names:** "The Commission," "The Board," "The Open Market"
**Classification:** ICA-labeled: Unregulated but monitored. Legal status: Private enterprise, legitimately registered in Orenthellion.
**Headquarters:** Centralis, Orenthellion (main), with regional offices in every major city

**What It Is:**
The Commission is an open contract platform — a legitimate, functioning business that allows anyone to post jobs with attached rewards, and allows any registered Commission member to accept and complete those jobs. It operates like a cross between a bounty board and a private contracting marketplace. Members are ranked by job completion history, not by Corebearer rank. The platform works both digitally — through a Commission network terminal accessible in any regional office or online — and physically through regional offices where members can meet employers in person.

**Core Rules:**
- Anyone can post a job. Anyone can pay the registration fee and become a member.
- Members do not have to reveal their identity, face, or abilities. Anonymity is protected.
- Rewards are set by the employer. The Commission takes a 12% processing fee on all completed contracts.
- If an employer does not pay the stated reward, their membership is permanently revoked and they are permanently banned from the platform. No exceptions. No appeals. This rule has been applied 847 times in Commission history. It has never been bent.
- Corebearers can be officially registered with the ICA and Commission members simultaneously — many are.

**What Jobs Look Like:**
The Commission's job board is extraordinarily varied. A sample of current-day listings:

- Collect 40kg of Ashroot Moss from a Red Zone in the Mordant Dominion (reward: 8,000 credits + rare equipment)
- Tame and deliver one living Emberscale Hawk from Mardu Frontier (reward: 60,000 credits)
- Clear a nest of Pale Hollows from a farmer's outlying land in Fenner Basin before harvest season (reward: 2,000 credits + livestock)
- Escort a Prism Research transport from Gear-Town to Drift-Lab without official ICA logging (reward: 140,000 credits — no questions asked)
- Retrieve a specific artifact from the Yellow Forest dungeon's second layer and deliver sealed (reward: classified, inquire directly)
- Find and photograph a Glacial Mimicbird in its natural habitat — a collector's request (reward: 12,000 credits)

The variety is the point. The Commission serves as the functional marketplace for anything the ICA's official system handles too slowly, too expensively, or not at all.

**Membership Ranks:**
Rank is determined purely by completed job count and employer satisfaction ratings. No combat test, no ability assessment.

| Rank | Name | Approximate Members |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open | ~200,000 |
| 2 | Listed | ~60,000 |
| 3 | Verified | ~20,000 |
| 4 | Recognized | ~5,000 |
| 5 | Notable | ~1,000 |
| 6 | Distinguished | ~200 |
| 7 | Renowned | ~40 |
| 8 | Sovereign | ~8 (current holders) |

Higher-rank listings are only visible to members of equal or higher rank. The most valuable jobs never appear on the open board at all.

**Why the ICA Hasn't Shut It Down:**
The Commission is too large, too embedded in too many economies, and too useful to too many people — including people the ICA cannot afford to alienate. The Benefactor Council has significant interest in the Commission as a procurement tool. Several Progenitor Lines use it for sensitive operations. High-rank Corebearers who find ICA official channels too slow or too monitored use it regularly. Shutting it down would create a political crisis that the ICA's current leadership has consistently decided is not worth starting.

The ICA watches it. They have placed informants inside the Commission's regional offices. The Commission knows they have placed informants. The Commission's internal security quietly flags the obvious ones. Both parties continue.

## THE SILENT ACCORD
### *The Shadow Behind the Commission*

The Commission is real. It is also a front.

**What the Silent Accord Is:**
A hidden organization that predates the Commission by approximately 200 years. The Accord is the true controlling body behind the Commission — the founders, the actual rule-setters, the invisible infrastructure that makes the platform's ironclad rules actually function across hundreds of thousands of transactions without corruption.

The Accord's membership is unknown to the public and to the ICA. It is not a criminal organization in the conventional sense. It is something older and more specific: a coalition of entities who know what the ICA actually is, and who have been building a parallel power structure across centuries as insurance against the day the ICA's management becomes something that cannot be tolerated.

**Who They Are:**
- Several Progenitor Lines who survived the First Consolidation's purge of rival coalitions — specifically, descendants of the Northern Accord and the Free Cities Alliance, the two coalitions the ICA destroyed. They remember what was done. They have been remembering for 600 years.
- Three megacorporations whose founding families trace their wealth to the pre-ICA era and whose institutional memory includes what the ICA actually did to consolidate power.
- Several powerful individual Corebearers at S-rank or above whose specific circumstances — being too powerful to disappear, too independent to co-opt, too informed to dismiss — have made them natural nodes in the network.
- A small number of extremely old Progenitors whose motivations are not easily summarized. They have watched the ICA for centuries. They have seen what it does with power over time. They are not interested in destroying it. They are interested in ensuring that when the ICA eventually compromises itself fatally, there is something else already in place.

**What the Accord Actually Wants:**
Not to overthrow the ICA. Not to seize global power. The Accord's goal is structural: to maintain the capacity to operate independently of the ICA's control, to preserve knowledge the ICA has suppressed, to protect individuals the ICA would otherwise eliminate, and to ensure that the "managed stability" the ICA maintains never becomes so complete that no alternative exists.

The Commission is not primarily a profit enterprise for the Accord. It is an infrastructure — a global network of independent operators with established trust in the system, who can be activated for specific purposes by someone with the right standing. It is the Accord's field capability, maintained openly, in front of the ICA, for the day it becomes necessary.

**What Marc Represents to the Accord:**
The Accord doesn't know Marc exists yet. When they find out — and they will — the response will be complicated. Someone carrying Zenith Empire system signatures and demonstrating capabilities outside all known human development pathways is exactly the kind of entity the Accord has been positioned to protect for 600 years. Whether they would protect him or attempt to use him is genuinely unclear, because the Accord's membership does not have uniform goals beyond the structural ones.

**Suggested Names (for writer reference):**
- The Commission is simply "The Commission" in the world.
- The silent organization behind it: *The Accord*, *The Founders' Accord*, or *The Old Board* (used by those who know, never publicly). The name is flexible until you decide.

# PART TEN — THE BLACK MARKET

## Structure and Operation

The black market in the Fractured Age is not a single place or organization. It is an ecosystem of separate networks, some connected and some not, all operating in the space the ICA's reach doesn't fully cover.

**Layer One — Street Level**
Low-rank dungeon materials sold without ICA logging. Minor equipment modified beyond ICA certification standards. Information brokering. Core Supplement analogs produced outside licensed facilities — cheaper, less effective, occasionally dangerous. The people operating at this level are visible if you know where to look. They operate in the underserved districts of major cities, in transit areas, near dungeon clusters where monitoring is less consistent.

**Layer Two — Mid Market**
Higher-grade dungeon materials from unlicensed sources. Unregistered Corebearer services — operations, escorts, intelligence. Suppressed rank certifications (documents that show a lower rank than actual for Corebearers who want to avoid ICA monitoring). Equipment rated for higher tiers than ICA certification allows for civilian use. This layer requires connections — you can't walk into it without knowing someone who knows someone.

**Layer Three — High End**
This is where the real money moves. S-rank materials from private ancient family dungeons. Middle-realm items accessed through channels the ICA doesn't know about. Extracted Cores — the Hollowed's product reaches buyers through this layer. Information classified above standard ICA clearance. This layer has no street presence. Access is by referral, relationships, and the kind of reputation that takes years to build in communities most people don't know exist.

---

## Key Black Market Commodities

**Extracted Cores** — The most dangerous and most valuable product in the entire ecosystem. A living Core extracted from a Corebearer. The process kills the original owner. Buyers use them for research, for consumption (Rakshasa-style Core absorption has human analogs — dangerous and unstable, but practiced), or for sale further up a chain that ultimately reaches non-human buyers. The ICA considers extracted Core trade an existential threat if it scales further than it has.

**Unlogged Dungeon Access** — Access to privately-held dungeons without ICA monitoring. Ancient families and a small number of corporate operators provide this for a price. The advantage: no ICA record of what you find inside, no tax on materials, no risk assessment required. The disadvantage: no ICA insurance, no emergency response if something goes wrong.

**Rank Suppression Documents** — Falsified or modified ICA records showing a lower rank than actual. Used by Corebearers who want to operate without attracting the attention their actual rank would generate. A B-rank Corebearer with suppressed documentation shows as D-rank in standard scans. This requires either corrupted ICA administrative staff or access to a specific modification technique that the ICA knows exists and has been trying to eliminate for 100 years.

**Origin Crystals — Off-Market** — The Devour System's Convert output would be, if it ever became known, the most valuable item in this market. Currently not applicable. But Origin Crystals produced through other means — from dungeon-creature processing techniques that the ICA hasn't licensed — trade here. Lower quality than system-produced, but available without paperwork.

**Exotic Creature Trade** — Living Wild-Land creatures, especially tamed or tameable specimens. One of the most active categories in both the Commission's open market and the black market's high end.

**Classified ICA Information** — Reports, assessments, threat matrices, investigation files. Usually stolen by ICA staff with access or purchased from staff who need money. The value varies enormously based on content and who's buying.

---

## Key Black Market Locations

**The Salvage Halls** — Districts in major cities where off-market trading happens openly enough to be known but not openly enough to be directly prosecuted. Typically adjacent to large dungeon complexes where the volume of legitimate material movement masks the illegitimate. Oakhaven has one. Neon-Vexton has three. Sovereign-Grand technically doesn't have one, but the area adjacent to Prime-Bridge does.

**Dead Zone Markets** — The permanent dead zone in Orenthellion's eastern edge (the censored Fifth Fracture site) has no official presence. It does have a small community of individuals who find the ICA's total absence there more useful than its presence anywhere else. The population is maybe 400 people. The trade that happens there is not visible to anyone maintaining formal records.

**The Anchor Port Shadow** — At the regulated exchange point between humans and the Nagarae in Cresthaven, a parallel exchange operates at the edges. The Nagarae's Siren Clades are the primary intelligence operatives and also the primary non-human participants in the black market's highest-value tier. Information flows through the Anchor Port Shadow that officially doesn't exist.

# PART ELEVEN — ICA DARK HISTORY

*The ICA is not evil. It is an institution that has been managing an impossible problem for 600 years under conditions that reward compromise and punish transparency. What it has become is a product of those pressures, not a choice made by a single malicious actor.*

This does not make its history clean.

---

## The Founding Purge (600 Years Ago)

The ICA's formation required eliminating organized alternatives. Three rival Corebearer coalitions — the Northern Accord, the Free Cities Alliance, and the Meridian Compact — were systematically dissolved. Methods: black-ops elimination of leadership, memory alteration of key witnesses, dungeon-based disappearances of individuals who couldn't be managed otherwise. The process took approximately twenty years. By the time the ICA was formally announced, there were no surviving organizations capable of challenging its authority.

This is not officially documented. It exists in the physical ledger in the Director-General's office and in the memories of the Progenitors who were present.

---

## Project Cradle (400-350 Years Ago)

An attempt to manufacture Coreborn children through controlled dungeon exposure. Hundreds of infants. Almost all died. Eleven survivors, possibly more. The survivors were placed in separate classified facilities. Their current status is above SSS-guardian clearance. Two of the Progenitors alive today were involved in Project Cradle — one as an administrator who eventually ended the project, one as a researcher who proposed it.

---

## The Memory Program (Ongoing, 600 Years)

The systematic use of memory-alteration relics to manage specific witnesses and information. Current estimated total uses: 340 documented in the ledger, additional unlogged uses suspected. The relics were recovered from a dungeon and have never been replicated — the ICA has three functional relics and has not been able to produce more. Use is strictly controlled because supply is limited, not because of moral constraint.

---

## The Fifth Fracture Cover-Up (40 Years Ago)

A fracture opened in Orenthellion's eastern edge. 340,000 people vanished when the SSS-guardian performed the emergency Null-Core collapse that sealed it. The ICA classified this as a natural dimensional disaster. The guardian who performed the sealing — whose name was removed from all public records — is memorialized in the physical ledger as "Guardian Aris." Every senior ICA official serving today was briefed on the real events upon taking their position. The briefing includes a specific protocol for what to say if anyone asks questions. It has been used seventeen times in forty years.

---

## Current Dirty-Handed Tactics

**Threat Matrix Management** — The active monitoring and soft-containment of Corebearers the ICA considers potential threats to institutional authority. Steering individuals toward controllable positions, limiting their access to specific resources, using academic and corporate relationships to shape their options without direct confrontation.

**Strategic Neglect** — Regions, communities, and populations that are politically inconvenient to serve well are served poorly. The Hunter Corps response times for underserved areas are measurably longer than for high-population, politically-connected areas. The ICA's own internal quality assessments document this. The documents are classified.

**Information Asymmetry as Control** — The most powerful tool the ICA has is not force. It is the difference between what the ICA knows and what everyone else knows. This asymmetry is maintained through classification systems that go far beyond genuine security need, through the suppression of research that would give independent actors information the ICA prefers to hold exclusively, and through the systematic construction of a public narrative that makes the ICA's management appear more successful and more necessary than it actually is.

# PART TWELVE — WHAT NOBODY KNOWS

*The deepest layer. The things that are true that no one on Earth has figured out yet.*

---

**The Dungeons Are Prisons**
Every dungeon on Earth is a containment system. The creatures inside are not native — they are captured beings placed there by the Hollowed Ones after the Devourer's death destabilized the dimensional framework. The Dungeon Core is a lock. The respawn cycle is not there to make dungeons useful — it's there because a contained entity that dies and doesn't respawn is a containment failure. Eight hundred years of dungeon-running has been the operation of the cosmos's prison system as a resource extraction industry.

**The Non-Human Regions' Worlds Are Dying**
Every foreign zone entity came from a world that is collapsing. The fractures opened because those worlds needed somewhere to go. Earth is one of the few stable anchors left. The non-human powers haven't told humanity this, partly because they don't fully understand Earth's stability and partly because admitting they're refugees rather than conquerors changes a negotiating position they depend on.

**The SSS Ceiling Is Designed**
The maximum rank achievable on Earth is not natural. It was designed into the cosmic architecture. Beyond SSS, the connection to the holding universe network — the prison system's management infrastructure — becomes a problem for that network. The ceiling protects the prison system's operational integrity. Nobody on Earth, including the SSS guardians, knows this.

**The Fourth Fracture Was Sealed By One Person**
Ryath sealed the Fourth Fracture. Not the ICA's operation — the operation slowed expansion but would never have sealed it. Ryath used a technique connected to Zenith Empire architecture that no current human development pathway can produce. Three people on Earth know: Elaine, Cassian Thornwell, and Mirela Thornwell. No non-human power has identified the source. The Chrono-Glacials have partial awareness from a time-adjacent experience. They haven't been able to interpret what they sensed.

**Marc's System Can Read The Inmates**
The Devour System reads the origin data of creatures inside dungeons — the cosmic history of where they came from before they were imprisoned. Marc has been building the most comprehensive involuntary cosmological research document ever assembled without knowing he's doing it. The day he can read the Archive fully changes the question from "what are these creatures" to "who put them here, and why."

**The First Fracture Was Caused By Humans**
The pre-ICA energy experiment beneath the Pacific pierced through to dimensional space and caused the fracture. Humanity is responsible for the event that nearly destroyed it. The ancient seabed ruins still inside the Abyssal Thronedom are not Nagarae construction — they are the remains of the experiment that started everything.

**The Progenitors Are More Than Old**
The oldest Progenitors — 600 years of Core development — are no longer fully human in any biological sense. Some of them carry resonances that the Zenith Empire's assessment systems would have recognized. The Valen Compact's oldest member has been developing in a direction that no current human assessment framework can accurately measure. She is not, in any meaningful sense, a human Corebearer anymore. She has not examined this closely enough to articulate it herself.

**The Silent Accord's Oldest Members Know More Than They Let On**
The Progenitor Lines who founded the Silent Accord have been watching the ICA for 600 years, collecting information the ICA thought it had suppressed. Several Accord members have pieced together portions of the truth about dungeons, about the cosmic architecture, and about why the world has the structure it does. None of them have the full picture. All of them have enough pieces that when Marc's story begins to unfold, some of them will recognize what they're seeing
