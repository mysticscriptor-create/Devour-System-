### I. STUDENT STRATIFICATION SYSTEM (VERY IMPORTANT)

Before departments, understand how students are classified.

#### 1. **Elite Track**

Who:
- Awakened students with high growth potential
- Rare abilities
- Political or financial backing


Privileges:
- Private or semi-private housing
- Priority dungeon access
- Personalized instructors
- Early exposure to higher-rank dungeons


Expectation:
- They will become commanders, heroes, weapons, or symbols.
- This is where Max and Ethan belongs.

#### 2. **Standard Track**

Who:
- Awakened but average growth
- Useful combat abilities
- Inconsistent performance

Privileges:
- Shared housing
- Limited dungeon access
- Group-based instruction

Expectation:
They become soldiers, specialists, or support for elites.

#### 3. **Auxiliary Track**

Who:
- Awakened with weak or narrow abilities
- Low combat scalability

Privileges:
- Minimal dungeon access
- Reassignment-focused training

Expectation:
- They fill infrastructure roles, not frontline combat.

#### **4. Dormant Program**

(Officially “Alternative Contribution Path”)

Who:
- Unawakened Coreborn

- Politically inconvenient cases

Expectation:
