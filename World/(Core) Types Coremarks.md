# CORE TYPES & COREMARKS
## Complete Guide
### *The Fractured Age — World of the Devour System*

---

> When a human Awakens, two things happen simultaneously: a Core forms near the left ventricle, and a Coremark burns itself into the skin. The mark is permanent. It is unique. No two Corebearers in recorded history have ever shared an identical Coremark — even within the same Core Type.
>
> The Type tells you what category of power the Core draws from.
> The Mark tells you that this specific person carries it.

---

## ON COREMARK APPEARANCE

A Coremark appears at the moment of first Awakening — during or immediately after the First Spin breakthrough. The location on the body varies by individual and has no known pattern. The mark itself is not tattooed or burned onto the skin in a conventional sense: it *emerges*, as though it was always beneath the surface and the breakthrough simply made it visible.

---

> **The question that generates the categories:**
>
> A Core is a fragment of Primordial energy — a shard of a consciousness that once ran an entire world — fused into a human body through dimensional exposure. When it awakens, it seeks an interface. Something to connect to. Something to draw power through.
>
> The type of Core is not random. It is determined by which layer of existence the shard has the deepest resonance with. Which frequency it naturally vibrates at. Which part of reality it can *speak to.*
>
> In-universe, this is what a researcher who figured out the categories would say:
> *"The Core doesn't choose what it can do. The Core chooses what it is. And what it is determines everything else."*
>
> The categories below are not organized by what the ability does. They are organized by **what the Core is reaching into.** The difference is everything.

---

## THE FRAMEWORK: SIX REACHES

> *"Power divides along the lines of what exists. Matter. Force. Space. Mind. Soul. Time. Everything that a Core can touch is one of these six things. There is no seventh. There has never been a seventh. If you find one, you haven't found a new type — you've found someone whose Core reaches further than you thought was possible."*
> — Attributed to the first ICA Classification theorist, 600 years ago

The six categories are called **Reaches** in formal ICA documentation and **Types** in common usage. Both terms are correct. The distinction is philosophical: *Reach* acknowledges that the Core is extending toward something external. *Type* treats it as an internal property. Most working Corebearers use *Type.* Most researchers use *Reach.*

Each Reach corresponds to a layer of existence that a Core can interface with. The six layers are not metaphorical — they are physically real in this world's cosmology. They are the six things that make up a bubble universe. Remove any one of them and reality becomes something else entirely.

---

# REACH 1 — SUBSTANCE

> *"The world is made of something. Substance Cores speak to what it's made of."*

**What it is:** The Core interfaces with physical matter and the forces immediately embedded in it — the molecular, chemical, biological, and elemental composition of the world. Substance Reach is the most common type globally. It is also the most ancient — the first Corebearers to develop consistent ability expression were almost all Substance-type.

**The logic that makes it inevitable:** Before anything else exists — before space, before time, before mind — there has to be *something.* The first question existence answers is "what is here." Substance Cores answer that same question. They are the most fundamental expression of power because they engage with the most fundamental layer of reality.

---

## What Substance Covers

**Elemental Expression (most common):**
Fire, water, ice, lightning, wind, earth, stone, metal, magma, lava, sand, crystal, smoke, ash, steam, mist, mud, oil, acid, poison, ice, snow, hail, tide. These are Substance Cores reading the elemental state of physical matter and reshaping it.

**Chemical & Molecular Expression:**
Rust induction (accelerating oxidation), crystallization control, liquefaction of solids, gas manipulation, density alteration of physical matter, explosive chemical reaction triggering, molecular bonding manipulation, corrosion, purification, toxin generation, venom production.

**Biological Expression:**
Plant and nature control, growth acceleration, organic matter manipulation, biological toxin synthesis, pheromone generation, spore release, mycelial network connection. When a Corebearer makes vines grow from stone floors or summons a forest mid-battle — that is a Substance Core reading the biological potential embedded in matter.

**Thermal Expression:**
Heat and cold as independent forces — not as elemental fire or ice but as temperature itself. Thermal Substance Cores can superheat air without producing flame, freeze objects at the molecular level without ice formation, create heat differentials that function as pressure weapons.

**Radiation & Energy-Matter Expression:**
Plasma manipulation, radiation emission and absorption, electromagnetic field generation, light as a material phenomenon (hardlight constructs, laser-class output, prismatic refraction). These are at the high end of Substance expression — matter in its most energized states.

---

## What Makes Substance Different

Substance Cores **do not create.** They reshape, redirect, and amplify what already exists in the environment. A fire-type Substance Corebearer does not generate fire from nothing — they access the thermal potential latent in the air and accelerate it. This is why Substance-types are environment-sensitive: a fire-type in a vacuum produces nothing. A water-type in a desert operates at a fraction of normal capacity.

This limitation is also the source of their greatest strength: a Substance Corebearer who understands their environment can always find something to work with. They are never truly without resources. The question is always *what is here* — and for a Substance-type, the answer to that question is always *something.*

---

## Substance Coremark — *The Material Script*

Substance Coremarks are built from geometric precision — triangles, hexagons, lattice structures, interlocking angular forms. No curves, no organic shapes, no flowing lines. The geometry is exact, almost mechanical, as if it was calculated rather than grown.

The mark always looks like it could be a technical diagram. Something about it suggests engineering — the visual language of a blueprint or a crystalline structure diagram.

**Element-specific visual tendencies:**
- Fire-type: sharp upward-pointing forms, asymmetric spike clusters
- Water/ice-type: interlocking rings, wave-interrupted grids, radial patterns that suggest fluid geometry
- Earth/stone-type: dense heavy tessellations, layered compression forms
- Metal-type: clean geometric lattices, precision grid patterns
- Lightning-type: branching fractal splits, jagged directional lines
- Nature/biological-type: geometric forms that just barely suggest organic shapes — hexagons that almost look like cells, angles that almost look like branches

The mark feels cool to the touch regardless of the Corebearer's specific element. This has been measured. It is slightly cooler than the surrounding skin. Always.

# REACH 2 — FORCE

> *"The world doesn't just exist. It moves. Force Cores speak to the movement."*

**What it is:** The Core interfaces with kinetic energy, momentum, inertia, and the physical laws that govern how matter interacts with matter. If Substance is *what the world is made of*, Force is *how the world behaves when things meet.* It is the second most common type globally.

**The logic that makes it inevitable:** Matter existing is not enough to produce a world. Matter has to *move.* It has to push and pull and collide and flow. The layer of existence that governs movement — the physics layer — is Force. A Core that resonates with this layer doesn't touch matter directly. It touches what matter *does.*

---

## What Force Covers

**Kinetic Expression (most common):**
Telekinesis (moving objects with the mind — actually with projected Force energy), kinetic amplification (making moving things hit harder), momentum transfer (redirecting force from one object to another), impact absorption and redirection, explosive force generation, shockwave production and shaping, recoil nullification.

**Barrier & Field Expression:**
Force fields, hardlight constructs (solidified force projection), impact barriers, pressure domes, gravitational lensing (bending the path of physical force, not gravity as a phenomenon), inertia fields (areas where motion is suppressed or amplified), rebound surfaces (force returns to source).

**Vibration & Wave Expression:**
Vibration manipulation (resonant frequency attacks — the ability that can shatter stone by finding its resonant frequency), soundwave weaponization at the force level (not sound itself, but the physical wave), pressure wave generation, harmonic disruption.

**Aura & Projection Expression:**
Force aura (a body-surrounding field of kinetic pressure), Will Projection (projecting intent as a physical force — this is the mechanism behind what Ethan Harrington does, a Force-type expression where concentrated will becomes a directional pressure), energy blasts of non-elemental origin, force bolt attacks.

**Mass & Gravity-Adjacent Expression:**
Mass manipulation (changing the effective weight of objects or people), inertia control (making objects impossible to start or stop moving), gravitational anomaly generation (distinct from earth/gravity as an element — this is manipulation of force geometry rather than gravitational phenomena).

---

## What Makes Force Different

Force Cores operate in the layer between Substance and Space. They do not change *what* matter is (Substance), they do not change *where* matter is (Space) — they change how matter **moves and interacts.** A Force-type at high rank can stop a mountain-destroying attack not by enduring it but by perfectly redirecting every joule of force in the impact.

The discipline is almost entirely about precision and efficiency. A Substance-type can overwhelm through volume — more fire, more earth, more mass. A Force-type must understand exactly what is happening in the physical interaction they are trying to alter. The gap between a mediocre Force-type and an exceptional one is the gap between someone who pushes and someone who redirects.

---

## Force Coremark — *The Impact Diagram*

Force Coremarks radiate outward from a central point — lines, arcs, and layered concentric shapes expanding from a core origin. They look like the visual representation of an explosion or an impact frozen at the moment of maximum expansion.

The central point is always either empty or a single dot. Everything else originates from it. Some Force marks have a clear directionality — all lines flowing one way. Others are perfectly symmetric. The distinction correlates with personality: directional marks tend to appear on Corebearers whose approach is offensive; symmetric marks on those whose ability expression is primarily defensive or redirectional.

The mark has a faint sense of energy to it even at rest. Standing close to a high-rank Force Coremark, sensitive individuals report feeling a very faint pressure against the skin — as if the air nearest the mark is very slightly more compressed.

# REACH 3 — SPACE

> *"The world doesn't just contain things. It has a shape. Space Cores speak to the shape."*

**What it is:** The Core interfaces with the dimensional fabric of reality — the spatial relationships between positions, the structure of distance, the geometry of where things are in relation to each other. If Substance is matter and Force is movement, Space is the *stage* on which both perform.

**The logic that makes it inevitable:** For matter to exist in particular places, there have to *be* places. Space is the architecture that makes location possible. A Core that resonates with this layer doesn't touch objects or their movement — it touches the relationship between *here* and *there.*

---

## What Space Covers

**Displacement Expression (most common):**
Teleportation, short-range blink-steps (spatial shortcuts — moving from point A to point B without traversing the distance between), forced displacement (teleporting others against their will), portal creation and maintenance, dimensional shortcuts.

**Geometry Expression:**
Spatial compression (making a large area occupy a small space — folding distance), spatial expansion (making a small area occupy a large space — stretching distance), area denial (warping the space around a zone so that entering it becomes geometrically impossible), spatial cutting (cleaving through the fabric of space itself — attacks that ignore physical barriers entirely because they don't travel through physical space).

**Dimensional Expression:**
Pocket dimension creation and control, dimensional storage (personal spatial pockets used to hold equipment), phase-shifting through solid matter (existing briefly in a spatial state where matter doesn't interact — not intangibility, spatial detachment), void space creation (zones of non-space where normal physics don't apply).

**Perception Expression:**
Spatial sense (perceiving everything within a defined radius regardless of line of sight — the spatial equivalent of sonar), distance awareness, positional memory (knowing exactly where everything in an environment is at all times), boundary detection.

**Anchoring Expression:**
Spatial locks (preventing movement within a zone), positional anchoring (locking an object or person in a specific location in space), territorial binding (creating a defined spatial domain the Corebearer has absolute awareness within).

---

## What Makes Space Different

Space Cores are disproportionately powerful at high ranks compared to their low-rank expression. The gap between a C-rank and an A-rank Space-type is not *more* teleportation or *faster* blinking — it is a qualitative shift in how much of the spatial fabric they can affect simultaneously. A C-rank Space-type can move themselves across a room in an instant. An A-rank Space-type can fold the room so that the door leads to the ceiling.

The limitation at low rank is specificity: a Space Core can only manipulate the spatial relationships between points it can perceive or has mapped. This is why Space-type Corebearers are notorious for their spatial awareness habits — they are always mapping, always noting positions, always building a precise mental model of every environment they enter. The ability requires knowledge before it can function.

---

## Space Coremark — *The Impossible Geometry*

Space Coremarks are composed of shapes that overlap and intersect at impossible angles — as if the mark exists in more than two dimensions and the skin is only showing one cross-section. Circles that pass through each other, squares rotated through a third axis, lines that appear to go behind and in front of each other without clear logic.

The mark appears to shift slightly when viewed from different angles — not dramatically, but enough that a careful observer notices the design changes fractionally with perspective. Whether this is real movement or optical effect has been debated by ICA researchers for two hundred years. No instrument has resolved the question.

Looking at a Space Coremark too long produces a faint spatial vertigo — not dizziness, but a briefly altered sense of where one's body is in relation to the rest of the room. Not everyone notices. Almost everyone has noticed if they've looked long enough.

# REACH 4 — MIND

> *"The world doesn't just have shape and substance. It is perceived. Mind Cores speak to the perception."*

**What it is:** The Core interfaces with the consciousness layer — the field of awareness generated by complex nervous systems. Every being with a developed brain generates a consciousness signature. Mind Cores can read, write to, and alter that signature. If the previous three Reaches operate on the physical world, Mind Reach operates on the world *as experienced.*

**The logic that makes it inevitable:** Existence that is not perceived is a different kind of existence. The layer of reality that generates experience — the consciousness substrate — is as real as matter, force, and space. It is simply real in a different medium. A Core that resonates with this layer has access to something that physical Corebearers cannot touch: the inside of other people's experience.

---

## What Mind Covers

**Reading Expression (most common):**
Surface thought scanning, emotion sensing (reading current emotional state as a data stream), intent reading (perceiving what someone is about to do before they do it — the tactical precognition of reading intent rather than future), memory surface reading (the most recent and strongest memories without deep access), lie detection at the emotional/physiological level.

**Projection Expression:**
Illusion creation at all sensory levels (visual, auditory, tactile, olfactory, proprioceptive — the ability to create experiences that bypass the physical world entirely), fear induction (projecting specific emotional states into a target's consciousness), pain projection, false-experience overlay.

**Alteration Expression:**
Memory manipulation (modifying, suppressing, or fabricating memories), deep mind compulsion (forcing specific decisions or actions — the most regulated ability in the ICA's framework), belief alteration, personality overlay, mental fortress disruption.

**Transmission Expression:**
Telepathy (direct mind-to-mind communication), shared consciousness fields (allowing multiple minds to share information instantaneously), mental link networks (the type Allen's Tactical Link falls into — not full telepathy but sensory-information sharing across a connected group), psychic projection (creating a mental presence in a remote location).

**Defense Expression:**
Psychic shielding, mental fortress construction, perception filtering (making the Corebearer undetectable to minds rather than to physical senses), consciousness hardening (resistance to all forms of mental interference), counter-compulsion.

**Cognitive Enhancement Expression:**
Information absorption (processing observed data at superhuman speed), tactical modeling (running probability simulations on observed data in real time), perfect recall, multi-stream attention (processing multiple information sources simultaneously without degradation).

---

## What Makes Mind Different

Mind Cores operate through an entirely separate medium from physical energy. This is why mental abilities work on dungeon creatures despite their non-human cognition (all complex nervous systems generate a consciousness signature) but fail entirely on constructs, spatial phenomena, and anything without a mind to interface with.

The power ceiling for Mind-types is exceptionally high — at the upper end, the difference between affecting a single person and affecting everyone in a city is a question of bandwidth, not principle. The ICA's most extensive regulatory framework surrounds Mind-type Corebearers6666yt because the potential for undetectable abuse is the highest of any type. A fire-type attack is visible. A compulsion attack leaves no physical trace.l

---

## Mind Coremark — *The Living Script*

Mind Coremarks look like writing — but writing in no known human or foreign language. The marks are composed of fluid, cursive-like strokes forming characters or symbols that feel as though they should be readable. The sense of almost-meaning is consistent: every person who looks at a Mind Coremark for more than a few seconds reports the same thing — that the mark is saying something they can almost understand.

No linguist has decoded it in 650 years of attempts. Several have gone to unusual lengths trying. The ICA has classified three research projects into Mind Coremark linguistics as "active investigations." None have produced results.

The mark produces a mild, indefinable unease in most non-Corebearers when looked at directly. Not fear. Not threat response. Simply the feeling of being near something that is looking back.

# REACH 5 — SOUL

> *"The world doesn't just have matter and mind. It has life. Soul Cores speak to the alive."*

**What it is:** The Core interfaces with the life-force layer — the energy pattern that distinguishes a living being from dead matter. Soul Reach is the most philosophically significant type, the most heavily regulated, and the most misunderstood. In older ICA classifications, it was called Cursed-type and Heretic-type. Both terms have been retired. Neither has been forgotten.

**The logic that makes it inevitable:** There is a difference between a living human and a dead one that is not captured by Substance (same matter), Force (same physics), Space (same location), Mind (gone), or Time (same moment). That difference is the life-force signature — the animating pattern that makes something *alive.* Soul Cores interface with that layer directly. When you reach into the dimension that underlies biology, everything that lives becomes readable.

---

## What Soul Covers

**Healing Expression (most common, most socially valued):**
Healing by restoring the life-force pattern beneath physical damage — not by directly repairing tissue but by returning the vital signature to its prior configuration, which allows the body to complete the repair on its own. This distinction matters: Soul-type healing works fastest on injuries where the underlying life-force pattern is intact (cuts, fractures, organ damage) and slowest on injuries that have disrupted the pattern itself (curse damage, soul wounds, certain origin-level attacks).

**Drain Expression:**
Life force absorption — taking vitality from other beings and converting it to the Corebearer's own energy or health. The most controversial Soul ability in active use. ICA-regulated. Lethal drain is classified as murder regardless of method.

**Bonding Expression:**
Familiar bonding (creating a permanent energy connection with a creature or person — the familiar shares some of the Corebearer's strength and the Corebearer gains some of the familiar's nature), contract binding at the soul level (agreements enforced by life-force signature — breaking them has physical consequences), link abilities (permanent or semi-permanent consciousness connections between life-forces).

**Death Expression:**
Death affinity (reading, sensing, and interpreting the energy state of death — perceiving the difference between a recent death and an ancient one, sensing where death has occurred, reading cause of death from the residual signature), undead creation (reconstructing a body around a death-energy anchor), undead command, spirit communication.

**Blessing and Curse Expression:**
Blessing application (strengthening a target's life-force signature — producing effects that feel like enhanced luck, accelerated healing, improved vitality), curse application (weakening or distorting a target's life-force signature — producing persistent negative effects that resist conventional treatment because they exist beneath the physical layer).

**Aura Expression:**
Soul sight (perceiving the full spiritual and energetic signature of any being — rank, emotional state, life-force strength, the presence of curses or blessings, the rough truth of a person's nature), aura reading, presence sensing.

**Sacrifice Expression:**
Converting life force — the Corebearer's own or (heavily regulated) others' — into raw power. The most powerful short-term power multiplier available to any type, with the highest personal cost. At extreme expression, sacrifice abilities can produce output that exceeds the Corebearer's rank by several tiers for a brief window.

**Reversal Expression:**
Death reversal — returning a recently-dead being to life by restoring the life-force pattern before the signature fully dissipates. Temporary at low rank. Increasingly permanent and less costly at high rank. The most regulated ability in the ICA's entire framework. Unauthorized death reversal carries the same legal weight as unauthorized memory alteration.

---

## What Makes Soul Different

Soul Cores are the only type that can affect what exists *beneath* the physical. A sufficiently advanced Soul-type attack bypasses physical defenses entirely — not by moving through space (Space-type) or by nullifying (Void-type) but by simply operating in a layer that armor, barriers, and physical durability cannot address. There is no shield against a curse that operates at the soul level. There is no endurance training that prepares a body for life-force drain.

This is why the ICA's regulatory framework for Soul-types is the most complex of any type — not because Soul-type Corebearers are more dangerous in raw combat terms, but because their power intersects with every major ethical category the Association maintains. Healing and killing at the soul level look mechanically similar. The intent is the only difference.

---

## Soul Coremark — *The Growing Branch*

Soul Coremarks look alive. They are composed of branching organic forms — shapes that suggest roots, veins, neurons, mycelial networks, or coral growth. They are the only type whose Coremarks visibly develop over time: at initial Awakening, the mark is simple — a few clean branches. As the Corebearer advances in rank, the mark extends, branches divide, new structures appear. The growth is slow and continuous. By B-rank, a Soul Coremark that began as a simple branching form has become something closer to a full organic pattern.

The mark feels slightly warmer than the surrounding skin. This has been measured repeatedly by ICA medical research. The temperature difference is consistent: approximately 0.3–0.7°C above ambient skin temperature, always. No satisfying explanation has been produced.

# REACH 6 — TIME

> *"The world doesn't just exist, contain life, and be perceived. It happens in sequence. Time Cores speak to the sequence."*

**What it is:** The Core interfaces with the temporal flow of dimensional energy — the layer of reality that produces the experience of sequence, causality, and change over time. Time Reach is the rarest of all types. ICA estimates fewer than one in ten thousand Awakenings produce a Temporal Core. The rarity is not random: the temporal layer is the most structurally deep of the six, and a Core that resonates at that depth is one whose origin shard has been compressed and refined by extraordinary pressure.

**The logic that makes it inevitable:** The six reaches cover the fundamental layers of existence: what things are (Substance), how things move (Force), where things are (Space), how things are perceived (Mind), whether things are alive (Soul), and *when things happen* (Time). Remove time and causality collapses. Nothing happens *before* or *after* anything else. The temporal layer is the one that makes change possible — and change is what everything else depends on.

---

## What Time Covers

**Acceleration Expression (most common):**
Personal time acceleration — the Corebearer experiences time at an accelerated rate while the world slows around them. Not true time manipulation: the Corebearer's personal dimensional energy flow speeds up relative to the world's. From the outside, the Corebearer appears to move faster. From their perspective, the world has slowed.

**Localized Dilation Expression:**
Time dilation field creation — zones where time moves faster or slower than normal. An aging field (matter inside ages rapidly). A preservation field (matter inside is frozen in its current state, preventing decay). A speed zone (Corebearers inside move at accelerated subjective time).

**Reversal Expression:**
Object and wound reversal — returning something to an earlier configuration. A broken blade returned to whole. A wound returned to un-wounded. This is not healing (Soul Reach) — it is forcing matter back to a prior temporal state. The cost scales sharply with complexity: reversing a wound costs far less than reversing a death.

**Perception Expression:**
Predictive precognition — reading short windows of future probability. Not fixed vision of what will happen, but a probability distribution of the most likely next events. At low rank: useful combat awareness (seeing what an opponent is most likely to do in the next second). At high rank: strategic precognition (reading the most probable outcomes of major decisions hours or days out).
Post-cognition — reading the past of a location or object. The temporal residue of past events can be read by a Time-type Corebearer: what happened here, in sequence, in reverse order from most to least recent.

**Anchoring Expression:**
Temporal anchoring — preventing something from being affected by time-altering effects. Moment freezing (locking an object or person in a specific temporal state — frozen mid-motion). Causal threading (identifying and following the chain of causes behind a current state — working backward through events to find the origin of a specific condition).

**Paradox Expression (high rank only):**
At A-rank and above, Time-type Corebearers begin accessing something the ICA calls "causal editing" in classified documentation — the ability to retroactively alter specific past events in a limited local scope. The limitations are severe: the further back, the higher the cost; paradox resistance from the world's temporal structure makes large-scale changes progressively harder; and the Corebearer can only alter events they have already observed. No known Corebearer has demonstrated reliable retroactive alteration beyond a two-hour window.

---

## What Makes Time Different

True Time Cores are not manipulating a fourth dimension — they are manipulating the Corebearer's relationship to the *sequence of dimensional energy flow.* This is why time abilities have the costs they do: the temporal layer has resistance. Reality pushes back against changes to sequence because causality is structurally supported by the cosmos. Every temporal effect is fighting the universe's preference for its established sequence.

This is also why Time-types develop more slowly than almost any other type: the resistance that makes their abilities costly also applies to their own development. Advancing a Time Core requires more force of will against more cosmic resistance than advancing any other type. The Corebearers who do it tend to be exceptionally stubborn people. This is not coincidental.

---

## Time Coremark — *The Recursive Spiral*

Time Coremarks are built from spirals — single, double, logarithmic, recursive. The mark may feature patterns that repeat at smaller and smaller scales, or outer rings that contain smaller versions of the full design. The overall effect is a mark that appears to be in motion: the eye follows the spiral inward and does not find a resting point.

At B-rank and above, some Time Coremarks pulse at irregular intervals. Instruments confirm the mark does not physically move during these pulses. The pulse, whatever it is, occurs between observations — it has been captured exactly once on high-speed recording equipment, and the footage has been classified by the ICA's research division.

The mark produces a specific perceptual effect in sensitive observers: a brief sensation of déjà vu. Not the ordinary kind. The kind where the person is briefly uncertain whether they have been standing here before — whether this specific moment has already happened. It passes in under a second. Most people dismiss it.

# REACH 7 — VOID

> *"But what about the absence? What about nothing?"*
>
> — The question that generates the seventh type.

**What it is:** The Core interfaces with negation itself — not any of the six layers of existence, but the active manipulation of *absence.* Void Reach is the second rarest type after Time, and in many ways the most dangerous. Not because of destructive output. Because a Void Core's fundamental function is to make other things stop being real.

**The logic that makes it inevitable:** The six layers of existence can all be negated. Matter can be erased. Force can be nullified. Space can be voided. Mind can be blanked. Life can be extinguished. Time can be stopped. If there is a power that can reach into each of these layers and cancel them — that is not just a variation on any of the six. That is something else. The seventh type is the power of *nothing.* Which is, paradoxically, a power.

**This is not a normal type.** Void Reach does not fit neatly beside the other six. The other six are positive expressions — they reach *into* something. Void Reach is a negative expression — it reaches into the absence of things. This is why ICA researchers have argued for 300 years about whether Void is a distinct Reach or a high-rank expression of any of the others. The current consensus is that it is distinct. The argument continues.

---

## What Void Covers

**Nullification Expression (most common):**
Ability nullification — suppressing or canceling another Corebearer's power within range. Not just overpowering; true nullification makes the ability cease to function at the source. A fire-type in a nullification field does not have their fire suppressed — they simply cannot produce fire. The Core can still attempt to access the Substance layer. The Void field prevents the access from completing.

**Erasure Expression:**
Energy erasure — consuming and eliminating energy rather than redirecting or absorbing it. The energy does not go somewhere else. It is gone. Anti-materialization — preventing energy from taking physical form (energy blasts dissolve before manifestation, elemental attacks fail to solidify).

**Negation Field Expression:**
Silence fields — defined zones where no ability of any type functions. The most feared defensive construct in the world at high rank. A high-rank Void-type's silence field cannot be entered without ability function ceasing entirely. Physical attributes (strength, speed) remain. Everything else does not.

**Suppression Expression:**
Existence suppression — making the Corebearer or a target undetectable to any sense or instrument. Not invisibility (that is a Mind-type illusion) but literal absence from detection: the target has no energy signature, no spatial presence, no soul-resonance, no temporal trace. Negation aura — passively weakening abilities used against the Corebearer as they approach.

**Conceptual Expression (high rank only):**
At A-rank and above, Void-types begin accessing something that has no standard name in ICA documentation — the ability to remove a *concept* from a localized area. Removing the concept of *hardness* from a stone. Removing the concept of *heat* from fire. The object remains. The property is gone. This is the ability that makes high-rank Void-types genuinely alarming to the ICA at a philosophical level — because removing a concept from a space is not clearly different from editing reality.

**Counter-Force Expression:**
Resistance fields, immunity stacking, disruption of dimensional energy flow, signal cancellation, rebound negation (making attacks against the Corebearer simply cease to have effect rather than being blocked or absorbed).

---

## What Makes Void Different

Void Cores are not the absence of power. They are the *active manipulation of absence.* The distinction matters because true nullification requires more precise control than almost any other type: to erase something completely, the Corebearer must perfectly understand what it is they are erasing. A Void-type who does not understand a Fire-type's ability cannot nullify it. They can suppress it, disrupt it, create fields that limit it. They cannot negate what they do not comprehend.

An A-rank Void-type can functionally neutralize an S-rank of any other type in the right conditions. This is the most significant power-gap-bridging capability in the entire system. Which is why Void-types represent less than 1% of the Corebearer population and every single one of them is on the ICA's Tier 1 monitoring list.

---

## Void Coremark — *The Absent Mark*

Void Coremarks are the inverse of every other type. The mark is defined not by what is drawn but by what is absent. The mark appears as a region of skin that looks subtly *wrong* — where light behaves slightly differently, where shadow falls in ways that don't match ambient lighting. The "design" is the shape of the void: a precisely bounded region of apparent emptiness, often edged by a single clean line defining the boundary of the absence.

Looking at a Void Coremark produces a sensation that is difficult to describe — not vertigo, not unease, but a sense of something *missing* in the field of vision, as if a piece of what was there a moment ago is no longer there.

Some people cannot look directly at a high-rank Void mark for more than a few seconds. This is not a compulsion effect. The mark simply resists sustained attention. Instruments that try to image a Void Coremark produce files in which the mark's region appears as an artifact — a blank where the imaging data simply did not record.

# THE EIGHTH CATEGORY: ORIGIN

> This is not a Core Type. It is a classification that the ICA does not have.

**What it would be:** A Core that doesn't reach into any of the six layers of existence, or into their absence. A Core that reaches into something deeper — the *source identity* of things. Not what something is made of, not how it moves, not where it is, not how it's perceived, not whether it's alive, not when it happened — but *what it fundamentally is.* Its origin. Its nature as a specific thing rather than a generic instance.

In current ICA classification, this category would have zero documented cases. Not because it doesn't exist. Because the only person on Earth who has it doesn't have a Core Type at all by ICA standards — he has a system that makes the Core Type question irrelevant.

This is Marc.

The Devour System does not reach into the six layers. It reaches into the seventh thing: the layer beneath all of them. The origin-identity of everything that exists. This is why ICA scanners read him as Dormant — the system is looking for a resonance frequency that matches its known seven reaches. Marc's Core resonates at a frequency the scanner doesn't have a category for.

**If a researcher in-universe ever identified this type, they would call it:**
- *Primal Reach* — the most likely academic name
- *Origin Type* — the functional description
- *Sovereign Type* — the Zenith Empire's term, which exists in the System's archive and nowhere else

**The Coremark would be:** Nothing standard. Or everything at once. A mark that ICA's classification language cannot read.

This category is listed here not as a live type but as a structural gap in the system — the thing that makes Marc's nature cosmologically significant rather than just personally unusual.

# EDGE ABILITY EXPRESSION & MIXED MARKS

**Edge abilities** occur when a Corebearer's power sits at the boundary between two types. A Physical-type (now Substance) whose regeneration begins affecting nearby allies is approaching Soul territory. A Force-type whose barriers develop spatial qualities at A-rank. These are not type changes — the Core Type does not shift — but they produce abilities that ICA Classification Review flags.

**Mixed Coremarks** — marks that contain visual elements from two different type languages — are extremely rare and always significant. Fewer than thirty documented cases in 650 years. Every single one of those Corebearers was exceptional in ways that defied standard rank assessment. The ICA has no satisfying theoretical explanation for mixed marks.

This information is not publicly available. It is in a restricted classification file.

# THE IN-UNIVERSE DISCOVERY MOMENT

When the six-type classification system was first formalized — approximately 580 years ago, years after the ICA's founding — a researcher named Hel Vaur presented the framework to the Classification Council. Her argument was simple:

*"Every ability we have documented reaches into one of six things. The substance of the world. The movement of the world. The shape of the world. The experience of the world. The life of the world. The sequence of the world. Every ability. Without exception. If you find an ability that doesn't fit, bring it to me and we will discover together which of these six you have misunderstood."*

Nobody brought her an exception in her lifetime.

The Void type was formally recognized eighty years after her death. Her descendants spent years arguing it was simply a special case of the sixth type applied to itself. They eventually accepted the consensus. Void type was added as the seventh. The classification became "the seven reaches."

Nobody has proposed an eighth.

Nobody has met Marc yet.

>
> — Time covers all acceleration, dilation, reversal, precognition, post-cognition, and causal expression. Any ability about when things happen lives here.
>
> — Void covers all nullification, erasure, negation, suppression, and conceptual expression. Any ability about the absence of the above lives here.
