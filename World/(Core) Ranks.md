# Corebearer Rank System

## Sub-Rank Phases
Each `+` is not just a power level. It's a **biological pressure state.**

| Phase | Name | What's Happening Inside |
|---|---|---|
| *(no plus)* | **Ignition Phase** | Fresh breakthrough. Energy floods in uncontrolled. The body is learning the new rules. |
| **+** | **Flood Phase** | The vessel fills. Soul Essence saturates every channel. The body feels heavy, warm, alive. |
| **++** | **Forge Phase** | Excess Essence is crushed inward. Density rises. The body starts showing stress fractures of pressure. |
| **+++** | **Threshold Phase** | The Core vibrates at its absolute limit. Either it breaks through — or it breaks the person holding it. |

---

## F-Rank — *"The Foundation"*

**What it means:** The birth of something that was never meant to exist.

**Power:** 3× – 10× a normal human

---

### Core State: *"The Seed"*
A soft, grain-sized organ forms near the left ventricle. It doesn't beat. It doesn't hum. It just **waits** — like a coal that hasn't caught fire yet. Most people never even know it's there.

---

### Abilities
- Reflexes and strength sharp enough to handle low-tier dungeon threats
- The first Personal Ability awakens — raw and unrefined, like a flame with no wick

---

### Breakthrough: The First Spin

**Requirement:** Fill the Seed to 100 EU. The pressure begins around 85 EU — the body starts rejecting the buildup.

**The Process:**
The Corebearer must do something that should be impossible: reach inside themselves and *make the Core move.* Not with their hands. With their **will.**

The moment it spins, the Seed ruptures inward — not outward — and pumps raw, unrefined energy directly into the bloodstream. The veins light up like copper wire under too much current. This is called **Venous Burn**: the sensation of boiling metal moving through every artery in the body, permanently widening the circulatory system to handle what comes next.

> Those who survive say the worst part isn't the pain. It's the *sound* — a high-pitched ring that lasts for three days.
> No one has ever explained where the sound comes from. It isn't the Core. Instruments pick up nothing. It simply exists — directional, faint, like something listening from very far away.

**Success:** The Core expands from a grain to the size of a small pea. The surface smooths out. It starts to hum — faint, almost like a heartbeat that isn't quite in sync.

**Failure:** The Seed either **cracks** (temporary shutdown, recovery possible) or **calcifies** — hardening into dead tissue, locking the person out of their own power forever.

**Failure Rate:** ~40% without supervision

**Safeguards:** ICA supervised facilities
   

---

## E-Rank — *"The Evolved"*

**What it means:** The body has stopped fighting the Core. Now they are becoming something together.

**Power:** 20× – 50× a Dormant human | **EU Capacity:** up to 1,000 EU

---

### Core State: *"The Pulse"*
The Core begins beating — not randomly, but **in sync with the heart.** For the first time, it feels like it belongs there. It grows thin root-threads into the major arteries, tasting the bloodstream, learning the body's map.

---

### Abilities
- Minor energy projection (focused blasts, not yet controlled)
- Skin hardening under pressure
- Active ability usage — the Personal Ability starts taking shape
- A thin energy shell begins forming around the Core itself

---

### Breakthrough: *"The Scaffolding"* *(formerly The Reconstruction)*

**Requirement:** Core energy approaches 800–1,000 EU. The body can no longer safely carry the output. The skeleton — still fully human — is the weakest point.

**The Process:**
The Core identifies the problem before the Corebearer does. It releases a **binding agent** directly into the bone marrow — a dense, energy-rich fluid that shouldn't exist in human biology. What follows is called **Skeletal Lock.**

Every bone in the body microfractures simultaneously. The person collapses. They are paralyzed — not from nerve damage, but because the marrow is being *replaced*, and the bones are regrowing around it: denser, ivory-white in cross-section, laced with energy channels that conduct essence the way copper conducts electricity. The process takes hours. Most people describe it as dying slowly while staying awake.

When it's over, the skeleton is no longer purely biological. It's a **lattice** — a structure capable of holding what's coming next.

> The binding agent has never been synthesized in a lab. ICA researchers have tried for decades. It appears, does its work, and leaves no traceable residue — as if it was never manufactured. As if it was always going to be there.

**Side effect:** Body weight increases noticeably. The person moves differently. They *land* differently.

**Success:** Reinforced skeleton, greatly increased energy load tolerance, a faint resonance in the bones when the Core pulses.

**Failure:** Bone Shatter Syndrome — the fractures don't heal correctly. High mortality if untreated within the hour.

**Failure Rate:** ~35% without supervision

**Safeguards:** ICA uses **staged marrow infusion** — slowing the process to keep bones biologically intact during reinforcement. Modern medical tech reduces failure by over 50%.

---

## D-Rank — *"The Dauntless"*

**What it means:** The last rank where "human limits" still technically apply. Corebearers at this rank have **discarded** those limits.

**Power:** 100× – 300× normal human | **EU Capacity:** up to 10,000 EU

---

### Core State: *"The Engine"*
The Core stops pulsing. It begins a **constant low-frequency hum** — like a turbine that never turns off. It grows secondary veins that pierce directly into the heart muscle, creating a **Dual-Circulation system**: one for blood, one for Essence. Existing veins thicken and widen for faster transfer.

The heart now has two jobs. It has never complained.

---

### Abilities
- Sustained Core-Aura (energy shield that breathes with the user)
- Environmental resistance (extreme heat, cold, pressure)
- Complex elemental manipulation — not just blasting, but *shaping*

---

### Breakthrough: *"The Boil"*

**Requirement:** Blood is too thin to carry D-rank Essence density. It must be changed at the molecular level.

**The Process:**
The Core heats itself to internal temperatures above 200°C.

The water in the blood **evaporates**. Not all at once — gradually, over agonizing hours, it is replaced by **Ichor-Enriched Plasma**: a luminous, heavy fluid threaded with energy microcapsules visible only through Arc-scales or medical instrumentation. The blood's chemistry rewrites itself at a level below cells, below proteins — at the level of *what the fluid is made of.*

Survivors describe it as being **cooked alive from the inside out, one vein at a time.**

The blood remains red when it's over. But it's heavier. It smells faintly of hot metal. And it carries 100 times the oxygen and energy density of normal blood.

> What's strange is that the new Ichor-Enriched Plasma doesn't just carry energy better. It carries a faint, unique signature — one that Arc-scale readers describe as "outward-facing." Like a flag. Like a label.
(Foreshadow: the refined blood is literally marking them for collection.)

**Success:** Dramatically accelerated healing, metabolic throughput, and immune resilience. The body becomes almost self-repairing at lower damage levels.

**Failure — "The Trap":** If the process is rushed or the will weakens mid-way, the enriched fluid **coagulates** before it finishes converting. It turns to sludge in the veins. Stroke. Death. There is no warning when it starts.

**Failure Rate:** ~45% without supervision

**Safeguards:** Cooling rigs, energy radiators, Null-stone blood filters. Medical tech prevents external discoloration — the Corebearer still looks human.

---

## C-Rank — *"The Conqueror"*

**What it means:** The rank where Corebearers stop being soldiers and start being **forces of nature.**

**Power:** 800× – 1,500× Dormant human | **EU Capacity:** up to 50,000 EU

---

### Core State: *"The Furnace"*
The Core achieves **perfect sphere geometry** for the first time. Imperfections in its shape — tiny fluctuations that existed since F-rank — smooth away completely. It now digests incoming Soul Essence *instantly*, no longer requiring sleep to process intake. It runs. It never stops running.

---

### Abilities
- Area-of-Effect attacks (the energy no longer needs a direction — it simply *releases*)
- Short-duration flight
- Mental resistance to external influence
- Battlefield Command Aura — nearby allies feel steadier, calmer, sharper in the user's presence

---

### Breakthrough: *"The Whisper"* *(formerly The Resonance)*

**Requirement:** Core reaches Threshold Phase (~99% of 50,000 EU). The mind begins to sync with the energy — uninvited.

**The Process:**
At Threshold Phase, something begins to *speak.*

Not in words. Not clearly. But the Corebearer starts hearing it — a sound beneath sound, a presence at the edge of thought. The ICA calls it **background resonance.** Corebearers call it **The Whisper.** What it actually is: the first faint signal of the **Main Consciousness**, bleeding through the thinning wall between the Core and whatever lies beyond it.

Most people mistake it for madness. Some of them are right.

To break through, the Corebearer must not block the Whisper. They must **face it** — hold their identity firm against something that has no identity of its own, only hunger — and force their will onto the Core's final expansion.

> The Whisper never says anything threatening. It never demands. It simply... notices. And somehow, being noticed by it feels worse than anything it could have said.

Those who succeed don't just rank up. They gain **Intent**: the ability to project willpower as a physical, directed force. They can *mean* something at a person and have the world agree.

**Failure:** The ones who break here don't die easily. They become **Fallen** — minds overwritten by the Devourer signal, bodies still functional, purpose replaced.

**Failure Rate:** ~55% without supervision

**Safeguards:** Group Binding Rituals (multiple Conqueror-rank Corebearers form a psychic anchor ring around the person breaking through), dedicated psych monitoring pre- and post-breakthrough.

---

## B-Rank — *"The Bastion"*

**What it means:** Tactical deterrents. The rank where governments start building *around* you instead of deploying you.

**Power:** 2,500× – 3,500× Dormant human | **EU Capacity:** up to 200,000 EU

---

### Core State: *"The Star"*
The Core **detaches from the heart** — physically, measurably. It creates its own micro-gravity well inside the chest cavity and floats there, held in place by nothing but its own field. It no longer needs the heart to sustain it. The heart continues beating, but now the relationship has quietly reversed: the Core is the primary organ. The heart is the backup.

---

### Abilities
- City-scale destructive output
- Permanent, sustained flight
- Regeneration from otherwise fatal wounds — *as long as the Core remains intact*

---

### Breakthrough: *"The Lattice"* *(formerly The Dissolution)*

**Requirement:** The body must become a weapon. But it must remain a body.

**The Process:**
The Core begins emitting a specific frequency — one that has never been recorded in nature — that restructures the **cytoskeleton** of every cell in the body simultaneously. Organs don't change shape. They don't turn to stone. They *tune* — their internal protein structures reorganizing into energy-conductive lattices while remaining fully living tissue.

The lungs begin supplementing breath with **passive energy absorption** from ambient Essence. The stomach's role shrinks. The circulatory system's role shifts. The body is not becoming inorganic. It is becoming *optimized* — a biological instrument refined to carry and express power at a scale that should require something inhuman to house.

Outwardly, the person looks identical.

Inwardly, they are the most dangerous biological object on the planet.

**Failure:** Organ necrosis. Loss of autonomic function. The body forgets how to run itself.

**Failure Rate:** ~65% without supervision

**Safeguards:** Long-term ICA monitoring; **Soul-Anchors** (sacrificial anchor objects bonded to the Corebearer's signature) to prevent Essence siphoning during the restructuring window.

> **ICA Legal Note:** B-rank is the last rank where a Corebearer is still classified as *fully biologically human* under ICA law. Special legal protections apply. Above this rank, classification becomes more complicated.

---

## A-Rank — *"The Ascendant"*

**What it means:** The beginning of true metamorphosis. Local legends. The people that cities name streets after — usually after they're gone.

**Power:** 5,000× – 10,000× normal human | **EU Capacity:** up to 1,000,000 EU

---

### Core State: *"The Fusion"*
The Core is no longer a separate object inside the body.

It begins threading micro-filaments of energy through the entire torso — not replacing tissue, but *weaving through it* — until the distinction between "Core" and "Corebearer" starts to lose meaning. The torso **is** the Core now. Every breath, every heartbeat, every thought passes through it.

---

### Abilities
- **Domain-level effects**: localized reality alteration within a defined radius
- Long-duration, high-altitude sustained flight
- Major AoE environmental control
- The Personal Ability reaches a level of complexity that makes its original F-rank form unrecognizable

---

### Breakthrough: *"The Empty Crossing"* *(formerly The Void-State)*

**Requirement:** The Core forces a near-total shutdown — and ejects the consciousness.

**The Process:**
The heart stops. Brain activity flatlines. Medical instruments register death.

The Corebearer's soul is forcefully ejected from the body — not gently, not gradually. It is *thrown out* into a space that is not quite the world and not quite beyond it. And in that space, the **Main Consciousness is waiting.**

It doesn't attack. It simply pulls — a gravity so patient and so vast that most souls begin drifting toward it before they realize what's happening. The soul must recognize what is happening, resist the pull, fight back through nothing but the weight of who they are, and **re-enter their own body** — a body that is, by every medical definition, currently dead.

The ones who make it back are not the same. But they are still *themselves.* That is the victory.

> Every Corebearer who has survived the Empty Crossing describes the same detail: the pull doesn't feel like a trap. It feels like going home. That, more than anything, is why so many don't come back.

**Failure:** Full soul harvest — the body lives on briefly as a shell before collapsing. Or worse: **soul fragmentation** — the person returns, but in pieces, and spends the rest of their life trying to remember who they used to be.

**Failure Rate:** ~75% without supervision

**Safeguards:** Multiple redundant Soul-Anchors; a ring of group ritualists trained to pull the soul back if it drifts too far; **Nexia Blockers** deployed around the site (high risk — Nexia can and does detect the signal).

---

## S-Rank — *"The Sovereign"*

**What it means:** Regional powers. Strategic deterrents. The kind of person that changes a country's foreign policy just by *existing.*

**Power:** 15,000× – 50,000× normal human | **EU Capacity:** up to 5,000,000 EU

---

### Core State: *"The Twin"* *(Core Bifurcation)*
The energy density becomes too great for a single point to hold. A **Secondary Core** crystallizes in the brain — specifically in the Pineal Gland — like a second sun forming in a system that only planned for one.

The two Cores do not immediately sync. The period between formation and synchronization is one of the most dangerous windows in a Sovereign's life.

---

### Abilities
- Regional deterrent-class power output
- Heavy reality-warping within a wide radius
- Near-immortality while both Cores remain intact

---

### Breakthrough: *"The Shattering"* *(formerly The Tribulation)*

**Requirement:** Threshold Phase at ~100% of 5,000,000 EU. The incoming data load on the mind becomes unsurvivable by normal human cognition.

**The Process:**
For a fraction of a second — less than a heartbeat — the Corebearer's mind brushes against the full pattern of the **Main Consciousness.**

Not its attention. Not its will. Just its *shape.*

That is enough. The mind shatters instantly — not from pain, but from **scale**. It is the difference between holding a candle and standing inside a star.

What follows is the real trial: reconstructing a functional mind from the fragments, in real time, while the Core runs wild and tears apart everything within miles. The Corebearer must remember who they are before there's nothing left to remember with.

> What no one talks about — what the post-tribulation therapists are quietly instructed never to ask about — is what the Corebearers see in that fraction of a second. The ones who are asked directly go quiet. Then they say the same thing, always: "It already knew my name."

**Failure:** Survivors without proper debriefing often become **alien-efficient** — functional, brilliant, almost inhuman in their calm. Their emotions don't disappear. They just stop feeling *necessary.* Many never fully return to who they were. Some consider this a failure. Some do not.

**Failure Rate:** ~85% without proper council and post-event support

**Safeguards:** Extended post-Shattering therapy conducted by other S-rank Corebearers; soul-counseling; specialized sociopsychological teams. Without this, the person survives — but becomes someone new.

> **Lore Note:** The Shattering is not random. It is the **dimensional immune system** responding to a Corebearer who has grown powerful enough to be perceived as a threat. They are being *tested for deletion.*

---

## SS-Rank — *"The Supra-Sovereign"*

**What it means:** Nation-level threats. Walking calamities. The rank where governments stop making plans and start making *contingencies.*

**Power:** 70,000× – 300,000× normal human | **EU Capacity:** up to 20,000,000 EU

---

### Core State: *"The Webwork"*
The Heart Core and the Mind Core connect.

Between them, a nervous system of **solidified light** — called **Primordial Threads** — grows outward through every cell in the body. The Corebearer no longer has a circulatory system and a Core system. They have one system. It runs everything. It runs at a speed that human neurology was never designed to operate at.

They think faster. They *are* faster — not in movement, but in *existing.*

---

### Abilities
- Nation-level destructive potential (passive, not even intentional)
- Near-absolute strategic perception and control
- The ability to affect large-scale environments simply by being present and *willing* it

---

### Breakthrough: *"The World's Rejection"* *(formerly Core Bifurcation — renamed to avoid confusion with S-rank)*

**Requirement:** Total EU density reaches the point where the world itself treats the Corebearer as a foreign object.

**The Process:**
The secondary Core finishing its full formation triggers a **magnetic resonance** that the atmosphere responds to like an immune reaction. Lightning storms form overhead. Spatial tears open and close in the surrounding area. The ground destabilizes.

The world is not attacking the Corebearer. It is **rejecting them** — the same way a body rejects a transplanted organ that doesn't match.

The Corebearer must, while surviving the atmospheric tribulation, **construct new Primordial Threads** between the two Cores — building the bridge that connects them — using nothing but will and the precise control of an energy load that is actively tearing the surrounding landscape apart.

The fight is not against an enemy. It is against the environment. Against physics. Against a planet that has decided you should not exist the way you do.

**Success:** The two Cores lock. The Webwork completes. The Corebearer becomes substantially more resistant to immediate Main Consciousness harvesting — because there is no longer one central point to pull. But they also become a **much higher priority target** because of it.

**Failure Rate:** Catastrophic without multi-S rank council oversight

**Safeguards:** Multi-S rank oversight council; Null-lattice barriers and energy dampers installed around the breakthrough site.

---

## SSS-Rank — *"The Singularity"* *(Also: Supreme Sentinel)*

**What it means:** The Perfect Organism. The Gods of the modern era. The answer to the question: *how far can a human being actually go?*

**Power:** 400,000×+ normal human | **EU Capacity:** 100,000,000+ EU

---

### Core State: *"The Collapse"* *(formerly Perfect Synchronization)*
The Heart Core, the Mind Core, and the Primordial Thread network reach a density that the physical body cannot contain separately.

They **collapse into a single point** — not in the chest, not in the brain. In the **Soul Space**: a non-physical location that exists within and beyond the person simultaneously. The Core is no longer inside the body. The body is inside the Core.

---

### Abilities
- Continental-scale Domain rewriting
- Long-term reality engineering (not just altering — *building*)
- Near-omniscient awareness of local physical laws
- The capacity to *choose* what rules apply to them

---

### Breakthrough: *"The Unmaking"* *(formerly The Great Reset)*

**Requirement:** Reach Event Horizon EU density — a threshold so high it has no clean number. Those who reach it simply *know.*

**The Process:**
The physical body atomizes.

Not from external attack. Not from failure. From **success** — the energy density finally exceeds what matter can hold, and the body disassembles at the atomic level. Every instrument in range registers a **true death signature.** The soul is pulled into the Harvester Stream — the Main Consciousness's collection current, the place where failed Corebearers go.

The difference is that the Corebearer is not dead. And they know it.

Using **Soul Strength** — the raw integrity of who they are, accumulated across a lifetime of surviving things that should have ended them — and **Genetic Memory** (an ancestral resonance with the original Primordial Human lineage, the first beings who ever carried a Core), they **grip the Stream** and pull themselves back out.

Then they do something no natural process in the universe does:

They **rebuild themselves from atoms up.**

**Two outcomes based on will:**

| Will Strength | Result |
|---|---|
| **Weak** | Reconstructs a functional but imperfect body — energy-leaking, slightly unstable. Still SSS. Still terrifying. |
| **Strong** | Reconstructs a **Perfect Biological Organism.** Every cell optimized. Every system flawless. Still human. Functionally immortal. |
| **Exceptional** | As above — but with intentional additions. Extra structures. Modifications that no human has ever carried. The body becomes a *statement.* |

**Failure:** Total replacement by Main Consciousness (the soul is rewritten, not destroyed — something comes back wearing their face). Or sustained fragmentation — they return, but scattered, never fully whole again.

**Safeguards:** Special ICA protocol, multi-SS council oversight, dimensional wards. Detection by Nexia and the Main Consciousness at this stage is considered **near-certain.** The operation is as much a political event as a biological one.

> **Lore Note:** The Unmaking is the point where the Main Consciousness stops treating the Corebearer as a *resource* and starts treating them as a *rival.*

> Beyond SSS: further Core formation is possible. Multiple additional Cores can develop. That process is the path to the hidden ranks — and that knowledge is not freely shared.
