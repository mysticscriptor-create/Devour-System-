# ⚔️ COREBEARER VITALS SYSTEM
### *Complete Sub-Stat Architecture*

> **How to read this document:**
> Each Vital is a category. The sub-stats beneath it are what the system actually tracks.
> The Vital score shown on the interface is the **weighted average** of its sub-stats.
> Sub-stats can diverge — a Corebearer can have high Power but low Control, which shows up as Power E+ with an asterisk (*) flagging imbalance.
> Cross-stat influences are at the end.

# ① POWER
### *The force the body generates outward*

> Power is not just hitting hard. It is every form of force the body and Core produce — from the weight behind a punch to the pressure of an energy blast expanding outward. It is the output stat. Everything that leaves the body and acts on the world.

---

## Sub-Stats

### 1.1 — RAW FORCE
*The brute mechanical output of the body.*

The pure physical strength of muscles, bone, and the Core's structural reinforcement of both. Lifting capacity, pushing force, grip strength, carrying weight, breaking resistance. This is the most straightforward sub-stat — it answers the question: how much physical mass can this person move?

Increases with physical training and dungeon runs at any rank. Plateaus relative to Core rank — a D-rank Corebearer who focuses entirely on this sub-stat cannot exceed D-rank's maximum Raw Force ceiling no matter how hard they train. The ceiling moves at breakthrough.

### 1.2 — STRIKE FORCE
*The concentrated output of a single attack.*

Distinct from Raw Force. Strike Force is the ability to compress and accelerate the body's output into a single point of contact — how much of the Corebearer's total physical capacity lands in one blow. Two Corebearers with identical Raw Force scores can have dramatically different Strike Force values depending on technique, body mechanics, and Core circulation during an attack.

Strike Force benefits from Speed (specifically Attack Acceleration) — a faster strike delivers more kinetic energy to the target. The relationship is not linear. At higher ranks, the Speed contribution to Strike Force becomes significant.

### 1.3 — ENERGY OUTPUT FORCE
*The destructive scaling of Core-powered techniques.*

When a Corebearer fires an energy blast, launches a technique, or expresses their ability offensively — the force that technique carries is governed here. Energy Output Force determines how much damage a Corebearer's Core abilities deal on contact. It scales with both Power and Energy: Power sets the ceiling, Energy provides the fuel.

A Corebearer with high Power but low Energy can have enormous Output Force in theory but cannot sustain it. A Corebearer with high Energy but low Power produces high-volume techniques that don't hit as hard as their energy expenditure implies.

### 1.4 — IMPACT AREA
*The scale at which force expresses.*

Whether a Corebearer's power concentrates into a point (precision) or spreads across an area (scale). Impact Area governs shockwaves, area denial, environmental destruction, and the difference between punching through a wall versus collapsing a building.

High Impact Area with low Strike Force produces wide, shallow destructive output — covers ground, doesn't punch through hardened targets. Low Impact Area with high Strike Force produces narrow, deep output — punches through armor, doesn't clear rooms.

Most Corebearers naturally skew one way or the other based on their ability type. Control-types tend toward point expression. Explosive-types tend toward area expression.

### 1.5 — STRUCTURAL FORCE
*Force applied to the Corebearer's own body as a weapon.*

The capability of using the body itself as an impact weapon — shoulder checks, body slams, Core-reinforced tackles, the ability to run through obstacles rather than around them. Distinct from Strike Force (which is directional, active) — Structural Force is the passive weight and solidity the Corebearer carries.

A high Structural Force Corebearer is difficult to move. Being hit by them accidentally is dangerous. At B-rank and above, their physical presence has measurable gravitational-adjacent effects on the immediate environment.

### 1.6 — FORCE CONTROL
*Precision and restraint over outgoing force.*

The ability to calibrate output — to apply exactly as much force as intended and no more. Force Control is what separates a Corebearer who can shake a building from one who can write with a pen without destroying it. Without Force Control, power is a liability in non-combat situations. With it, the same raw output becomes surgical.

Force Control also governs the Corebearer's ability to maintain technique precision at high output levels — the point at which raw power starts to degrade accuracy and form. A high Force Control Corebearer does not lose technique quality as they push harder. A low Force Control Corebearer's abilities become increasingly uncontrolled at maximum output.

# ② SPEED
### *The body's kinetic and temporal capability*

> Speed is not just how fast a Corebearer moves. It is every dimension of quickness — how fast they think in a fight, how fast their body responds to that thought, how fast their attacks arrive, how efficiently they move through space. It is the temporal stat. Everything that relates to *when* things happen.

---

## Sub-Stats

### 2.1 — MOVEMENT SPEED
*Raw displacement velocity.*

How fast the Corebearer can cover ground — straight-line sprint speed, maintained run speed, and the Core's contribution to velocity. This is the number most people think of when they think of Speed. It is not the most combat-relevant sub-stat, but it governs positioning, pursuit, evasion by distance, and the ability to disengage from unfavorable engagements.

Movement Speed has a meaningful ceiling relative to the environment — a Corebearer moving at D-rank speeds in an enclosed space is limited by the space. Open terrain advantages high Movement Speed more than any other sub-stat.

### 2.2 — REACTION TIME
*Neural and Core processing speed in response to stimulus.*

The gap between detecting a threat and the body responding to it. Reaction Time is the latency in the Corebearer's combat system — it governs how much of their other speed stats they can actually use in real combat. A Corebearer with high Movement Speed but poor Reaction Time will react too late to use their speed when it matters.

Reaction Time is the most covertly governed sub-stat — it is influenced heavily by Perception (specifically Threat Detection and Predictive Processing) and improves passively as combat experience accumulates. Veterans with moderate raw Speed often outperform faster but less experienced Corebearers because their Reaction Time has been trained down to near-zero latency.

### 2.3 — COMBAT REFLEX
*The body's automated response system during active combat.*

Distinct from Reaction Time. Reaction Time is the response to an external stimulus. Combat Reflex is the body's trained automatic behaviors — the counters, the footwork, the guard adjustments — that activate before conscious processing even begins. It is the sub-stat that produces the impression of "reading" an opponent.

Combat Reflex develops through repetition and real combat exposure. It cannot be significantly advanced in simulation — the nervous system builds its reflex library from genuine threat exposure. This is one of the reasons Markham's dungeon running is considered non-negotiable: Combat Reflex requires actual danger to develop properly.

### 2.4 — ATTACK ACCELERATION
*The speed at which offensive output reaches its target.*

The velocity of strikes, techniques, and projected abilities. Attack Acceleration is distinct from Movement Speed — a Corebearer does not need to be moving fast to deliver a fast attack. Wrist speed, the acceleration curve of a technique, the launch velocity of a projected ability — all governed here.

High Attack Acceleration at lower-than-expected Movement Speed is the signature of specialized strikers — Corebearers who may not move impressively but whose attacks are difficult to see, block, or predict because they arrive faster than the Motion Speed implies.

Attack Acceleration contributes to Strike Force (Power sub-stat) at a scaling rate — faster delivery increases kinetic impact.

### 2.5 — EVASION
*The ability to not be where an attack lands.*

Evasion is not just speed — it is the combination of Movement Speed, Reaction Time, Combat Reflex, and body control that allows a Corebearer to not be hit. A high Evasion Corebearer makes poor choices for opponents look obvious and executes those choices with minimum body movement.

Evasion has a ceiling that is partially governed by Perception — a Corebearer cannot evade an attack they did not perceive. High Evasion without corresponding Perception sub-stats produces a fighter who can dodge what they see but gets hit by what they don't.

### 2.6 — AGILITY
*Multidirectional mobility and body control during motion.*

The ability to change direction, maintain balance in unstable conditions, operate in three-dimensional space, and use the environment actively as part of movement. Agility governs performance in close quarters, on uneven terrain, while carrying weight, and while executing techniques that require body rotation or precise positioning.

Agility and Movement Speed are frequently conflated. They diverge significantly in practice: a Corebearer with high Movement Speed but low Agility is fast in a straight line and clumsy everywhere else. A Corebearer with high Agility but moderate Movement Speed is difficult to engage because they are always exactly where they want to be.

### 2.7 — BURST VELOCITY
*Maximum instantaneous speed over a short duration.*

The Corebearer's ability to produce a sudden spike of speed — a short-range dash, an explosive first step, a maximum-output sprint that cannot be sustained. Burst Velocity is the gap-closing and gap-opening stat. It is almost always higher than sustainable Movement Speed, sometimes dramatically so.

In combat, Burst Velocity is frequently more valuable than raw Movement Speed. The ability to cover five meters in a fraction of a second is more tactically significant than being able to run at high speed for extended periods, in most dungeon environments.

# ③ VITALITY
### *Structural integrity and biological resilience*

> Vitality is the body's answer to everything the world does to it. It is every form of resistance, every recovery mechanism, every system that keeps the Corebearer functional when they should not be. It is the integrity stat. Everything that determines how much the body can take before it stops.

---

## Sub-Stats

### 3.1 — STRUCTURAL DENSITY
*The physical hardness and load-bearing capacity of the body.*

The density and reinforcement of bone, connective tissue, and Core-infused structural material. Structural Density determines how much physical force the body can absorb before structural failure (broken bones, torn joints, crushed tissue). It is the most fundamental Vitality sub-stat — everything else depends on the body maintaining its structural integrity under pressure.

Structural Density increases at every breakthrough as the Core deposits denser biological material into the skeleton and connective tissue. The Scaffolding breakthrough at E-rank is primarily a Structural Density event — the bone replacement process that causes Skeletal Lock is this sub-stat advancing rapidly.

Structural Density contributes to Raw Force (Power sub-stat) — denser structural material transmits force more efficiently.

### 3.2 — PHYSICAL RESISTANCE
*Tolerance to mechanical and kinetic damage.*

How well the body resists being physically harmed — cuts, impacts, crushing force, penetration. Physical Resistance is distinct from Structural Density: Density is the skeleton's load-bearing capacity; Physical Resistance is the skin, muscle, and outer tissue's resilience to damage. A Corebearer with high Physical Resistance feels like hitting reinforced rubber. The damage still accumulates, but it takes significantly more to produce meaningful injury.

Physical Resistance scales unevenly across ranks — it develops slowly at F-rank, accelerates at D-rank when The Boil replaces blood chemistry, and becomes dramatically more significant at B-rank when cellular restructuring occurs.

### 3.3 — ENERGY RESISTANCE
*Tolerance to Core-generated and dimensional energy attacks.*

The body's ability to withstand energy-based damage — ability strikes, technique impacts, environmental dungeon energy, and dimensional pressure. Energy Resistance is structurally separate from Physical Resistance because energy damage operates differently: it bypasses physical armor, resonates with the Core, and can cause internal damage without visible surface injury.

A Corebearer with high Energy Resistance can take ability hits that would incapacitate Corebearers of similar rank. They also have greater resistance to dungeon environmental effects — pressure zones, energy-saturated rooms, the passive output of high-tier creatures. The dungeon environments that cause progressive damage to standard teams do measurably less to high Energy Resistance Corebearers.

### 3.4 — MENTAL FORTITUDE
*Cognitive and psychological resistance at the biological level.*

The base-level resistance of the brain and nervous system to mental interference, fear effects, compulsion abilities, cognitive disruption, and psychological destabilization. Mental Fortitude is not willpower or personality — it is the physical hardness of the mind, the degree to which the neural architecture resists external manipulation.

Mental Fortitude at its base level is governed by Vitality. Full mental capability and adaptability are governed by Perception. The distinction: Vitality's Mental Fortitude is a wall — it says "this cannot get in." Perception's mental sub-stats are what happens once something is inside the wall.

Without adequate Mental Fortitude, a Corebearer can be rendered non-functional by a sufficiently powerful mental ability regardless of their physical stats. This is why some extremely physically capable Corebearers are surprisingly vulnerable in encounters with Mind-Reach types.

### 3.5 — MUSCULAR INTEGRITY
*The quality and efficiency of muscle tissue.*

Not muscle mass — muscle architecture. The efficiency with which muscle fibers contract, recover, and translate Core energy into physical output. Muscular Integrity governs how well the Corebearer's body executes what it's trying to do — the mechanical efficiency beneath every physical action.

High Muscular Integrity produces what observers describe as "clean" movement — no wasted motion, no mechanical inefficiency, every action using exactly the muscle engagement required. Low Muscular Integrity with high Raw Force produces power that is partially wasted in each action — brute force without refinement.

Muscular Integrity contributes to both Strike Force (Power) and Agility (Speed), making it one of the more cross-stat-significant sub-stats in the system.

### 3.6 — REGENERATION RATE
*The speed at which the body repairs damage.*

How quickly wounds close, bones knit, tissue regenerates, and energy damage heals. Regeneration Rate is one of the most obviously rank-scaling Vitality sub-stats — at F-rank, minor cuts close in hours; at A-rank, lost limbs regrow in hours; at S-rank, regeneration occurs in real-time during combat.

Regeneration Rate is governed not just by Core rank but by Energy availability — regeneration consumes Core energy. A Corebearer with high Regeneration Rate but depleted Energy still heals, but slowly. Adequate Energy sustains the rate. This creates a meaningful tactical consideration: Corebearers who take heavy damage and rely on regeneration are simultaneously depleting the Energy that drives further combat capability.

### 3.7 — STAMINA
*Sustained output capacity over time.*

How long the Corebearer can maintain high-intensity activity before physical performance degrades. Stamina is distinct from Energy (which governs Core output duration) — Stamina is the biological substrate, the oxygen efficiency, the metabolic management, the waste product processing. It is the body's endurance independent of the Core's fuel.

In short engagements, Stamina is irrelevant. In extended dungeon runs, multi-hour operations, or drawn-out fights against opponents who intentionally create attrition, Stamina becomes a primary determinant of outcome. Two Corebearers of identical rank can diverge dramatically in extended combat based on Stamina alone.

### 3.8 — TOXIN & PATHOGEN RESISTANCE
*Biological immunity and chemical tolerance.*

Resistance to poisons, venoms, diseases, corrupted dungeon materials, and chemical agents. At F and E-rank, this is relatively straightforward — the Core provides some general enhancement to the immune system. At D-rank and above, the restructured blood chemistry actively neutralizes most conventional toxins before they reach dangerous concentrations. At A-rank and above, a Corebearer can process materials that would be immediately lethal to a normal human.

Toxin & Pathogen Resistance also governs dimensional energy contamination — the kind of slow corruption that affects Corebearers who spend extended time in high-tier dungeon environments or near rift zones. This makes it more practically significant for high-rank Corebearers than the name suggests.

# ④ PERCEPTION
### *Sensory and cognitive processing*

> Perception is not just seeing further. It is every system by which the Corebearer gathers, processes, and acts on information — from physical senses extended by Core energy to the combat intuition that forms after thousands of hours of genuine danger. It is the intelligence stat. Everything that determines what the Corebearer knows and how fast they know it.

---

## Sub-Stats

### 4.1 — SENSORY RANGE
*The physical reach of extended senses.*

How far and precisely the Corebearer's Core-enhanced senses operate. At F-rank this means sharp vision and hearing that extends beyond normal human range. At higher ranks it includes: reading heat signatures through walls, hearing heartbeats at significant distance, detecting vibrations through surfaces, sensing electromagnetic fields. At S-rank, some Corebearers develop ambient environmental awareness that functions like three-dimensional sensing of everything within their range.

Sensory Range is the furthest-extending of the Perception sub-stats. Its limitation is processing — extending senses beyond the Corebearer's Sensory Processing capacity produces overwhelming input that becomes more liability than advantage. Range and Processing must scale together.

### 4.2 — SENSORY PROCESSING
*The mind's ability to filter, prioritize, and use incoming sensory data.*

What the brain does with everything the extended senses are sending it. Sensory Processing determines whether high Sensory Range is an advantage or a hindrance. A Corebearer who can hear a needle drop at fifty meters but cannot filter that from the ambient noise of a dungeon ecosystem will be constantly disoriented.

Sensory Processing develops with experience. New Corebearers at high ranks often go through a period of sensory overwhelm as their Range jumps ahead of their Processing capacity. Experienced high-rank Corebearers can passively filter the full output of their extended senses at all times, only attending to what crosses a threat threshold.

### 4.3 — THREAT DETECTION
*The ability to identify danger before it fully materializes.*

The pattern recognition system that identifies threat signatures — pre-attack tells, energy gathering patterns, movement intention, changes in ambient energy that precede creature attacks. Threat Detection is the sub-stat most responsible for the "sense danger" capability that high-rank Corebearers develop.

Threat Detection draws on Sensory Range and Processing to function, but is a distinct capability — it is the threat-specific application of those senses rather than general awareness. A Corebearer with high Threat Detection passively sorts incoming sensory data for danger signatures at all times. They are rarely surprised. They are almost never flanked successfully.

Threat Detection directly feeds into Reaction Time (Speed sub-stat) — better threat detection means the reaction starts earlier, effectively reducing the apparent response latency.

### 4.4 — PREDICTIVE PROCESSING
*Pattern recognition applied to opponent behavior and environmental flow.*

The capability to anticipate what will happen next. Not supernatural precognition — accumulated pattern recognition from combat experience, operating at speeds that feel instinctive but are calculated. Predictive Processing reads an opponent's weight shift, muscle tension, energy movement, and recent behavioral pattern, and generates a prediction of their next action.

At high levels, Predictive Processing allows a Corebearer to respond to an attack before it is visible — not because they are faster, but because they started moving earlier. This is what makes high-rank Corebearers appear to read minds. They are not. They are processing pattern data faster than the situation appears to provide.

Predictive Processing feeds Combat Reflex (Speed sub-stat) — predictions that arrive fast enough become automatic responses rather than conscious decisions.

### 4.5 — TARGETING PRECISION
*Accuracy and focus in applying power to a specific point.*

The ability to direct strikes, techniques, and energy output to exactly where intended. Targeting Precision is what makes the difference between hitting a target and hitting a specific point on a target. At high ranks, a Corebearer with high Targeting Precision can exploit structural weak points, joint vulnerabilities, and the narrow gaps in defensive ability fields that a lower-precision fighter cannot reliably find.

Targeting Precision feeds Force Control (Power sub-stat) — precision in directing power reduces waste and improves the calibration of output.

### 4.6 — ENERGY SENSITIVITY
*Detection of Core energy, dungeon energy, and ability signatures.*

The ability to sense energy that is invisible to normal physical senses — other Corebearers' Core output, dungeon ambient energy, ability activation signatures, rift energy, dimensional distortions. Energy Sensitivity is the sub-stat that allows Corebearers to "read the room" at the dimensional level.

At low ranks, Energy Sensitivity means a Corebearer can tell when someone nearby has activated an ability. At higher ranks, they can identify what type of ability is active, estimate its intensity, and detect whether an ability is charging before discharge. At peak development, some Corebearers can sense energy signatures through walls and across significant distances.

Energy Sensitivity is the primary Perception sub-stat for countering stealth and ambush — a Corebearer with developed Energy Sensitivity cannot be surprised by ability users regardless of physical concealment.

### 4.7 — BODY AWARENESS
*Proprioceptive feedback and internal physical monitoring.*

The Corebearer's perception of their own body — the precise feedback system that tells them where every limb is, what every muscle is doing, where damage has been taken, how much energy is remaining, and when a physical limit is approaching. Body Awareness is the internal-facing half of Perception, as distinct from all other Perception sub-stats which face outward.

High Body Awareness allows a Corebearer to perform precisely under physical impairment — to continue fighting effectively with injuries that would disorient a lower-Awareness practitioner, because they have accurate real-time data about their actual current capability rather than operating on assumption.

Body Awareness contributes to Muscular Integrity (Vitality sub-stat) — precise proprioceptive feedback allows the body to optimize muscle use and architecture over time.

### 4.8 — COMBAT INTUITION
*The synthesis of all Perception sub-stats into a unified combat intelligence.*

The highest-order Perception sub-stat. Combat Intuition is not a separate sense — it is the emergent property of all other Perception sub-stats operating together at high efficiency. A Corebearer whose Sensory Range, Processing, Threat Detection, Predictive Processing, and Targeting Precision are all highly developed generates something that functions like a sixth sense for combat: an overall tactical awareness that operates faster than deliberate thought.

Combat Intuition cannot be directly trained. It develops as a consequence of advancing the other Perception sub-stats and accumulating real combat experience that the system can use to build its pattern library. Corebearers who develop it describe it as *the fight feeling obvious* — not easy, but legible. As if the combat environment is translating itself into a language they can read at a glance.

# ⑤ ENERGY
### *The Core's output capacity and control*

> Energy is not one thing. It is the entire system by which the Core generates, stores, cycles, and spends its output. It is the resource stat. Everything that determines how much the Corebearer can do and for how long — and how precisely they can do it.

---

## Sub-Stats

### 5.1 — ENERGY POOL
*Total stored Core energy available for use.*

The maximum amount of Core energy the Corebearer can hold at any time. Energy Pool is the raw tank size — the ceiling on everything else. A Corebearer cannot sustain output beyond their Pool capacity, cannot fuel techniques above their Pool allows, and cannot regenerate faster than their Pool can supply.

Energy Pool is the most rank-correlated Energy sub-stat — it scales dramatically with each breakthrough, reflecting the Core's fundamental expansion at each tier. The EU system (Energy Units) measures Pool capacity: F-rank caps at 100 EU; by SSS-rank the measurement becomes functionally meaningless in traditional units.

### 5.2 — REGENERATION EFFICIENCY
*The rate at which the Core replenishes spent energy.*

How fast the Energy Pool refills between and during expenditure. Regeneration Efficiency is the sub-stat that determines whether a Corebearer is a sprinter (high Pool, low Regen) or a marathon runner (lower Pool, high Regen). In extended engagements, Regen Efficiency often matters more than total Pool size — a Corebearer who regenerates faster than they spend can theoretically sustain indefinitely.

Regeneration Efficiency also governs biological repair speed (the Core is the fuel source for Regeneration Rate in Vitality) and passive ability maintenance. It is one of the most quality-of-life relevant sub-stats, affecting everything from how quickly a Corebearer recovers between dungeon runs to whether their passive abilities stay active during long-form missions.

### 5.3 — OUTPUT PRESSURE
*The density and impact force of Core energy when expressed.*

Not how much energy is produced — how hard that energy hits when it makes contact. Two Corebearers with identical Energy Pools can have dramatically different Output Pressure: one releases energy broadly, the other compresses it to maximum density before release. Output Pressure is the quality stat of Core output versus quantity.

High Output Pressure means each unit of Energy spent does more damage, powers techniques more efficiently, and punches through Energy Resistance more effectively. It is the sub-stat responsible for the phenomenon where an experienced lower-ranked Corebearer's techniques hit harder than their rank implies — years of compression training have pushed Output Pressure beyond what the rank-standard for their Pool size would suggest.

Output Pressure directly scales Energy Output Force (Power sub-stat).

### 5.4 — BURST CAPACITY
*Maximum instantaneous energy output over a short duration.*

The ability to spike output dramatically above sustainable levels — releasing a concentrated surge of energy that exceeds normal operating parameters. Burst Capacity is the Corebearer's overdrive: a limited-duration window of dramatically increased output that draws down the Energy Pool rapidly.

Burst Capacity governs: the peak power of a charged technique, the maximum output of a single ability activation, the energy behind a final-resort attack. It is almost always significantly higher than Sustained Output — the body and Core can briefly tolerate output levels they cannot maintain.

Using Burst Capacity repeatedly in a short window risks Core strain — a temporary state where the Core's output regulation becomes unstable, producing unpredictable energy spikes or, at worst, a Core feedback event.

### 5.5 — SUSTAINED OUTPUT
*The rate of energy expenditure the Corebearer can maintain indefinitely.*

The operational baseline — how much Energy the Corebearer can spend per unit of time while Regeneration Efficiency keeps pace. Sustained Output governs: how long passive abilities stay active, how many techniques can be used in sequence without draining the Pool, the energy cost of simply existing at full operational capacity in a high-energy-density environment.

The ratio of Sustained Output to Regeneration Efficiency is the most important energy management figure in long-form operations. An experienced Corebearer knows their ratio precisely and adjusts their engagement style to stay within it. An inexperienced one doesn't notice the drain until they're empty.

### 5.6 — CORE CIRCULATION SPEED
*The rate at which energy moves through the Core's internal network.*

The speed of energy flow from storage to application — how quickly drawn energy reaches the ability being activated. Core Circulation Speed is what governs technique activation time, the speed of ability charging, and the latency between "decide to use an ability" and "ability is active." 

High Core Circulation Speed makes abilities feel immediate. Low Circulation Speed produces a perceptible lag between intent and expression that becomes a combat liability at higher ranks, where opponents can exploit the window between ability initiation and activation.

Core Circulation Speed also affects Attack Acceleration (Speed sub-stat) — faster circulation speed allows techniques to launch faster from initiation.

### 5.7 — CONTROL PRECISION
*The granularity of energy management and technique expression.*

The ability to control energy in fine increments — to spend exactly the amount needed, to shape technique output with precision, to maintain multiple abilities simultaneously without interference. Control Precision is what separates a Corebearer who can use their ability and a Corebearer who has fully mastered it.

High Control Precision means: zero energy waste per technique, perfect technique consistency, the ability to use an ability at 1% output as accurately as at 100% output, and the capacity to maintain several simultaneous ability expressions without them blurring into each other.

Control Precision feeds Targeting Precision (Perception sub-stat) and Force Control (Power sub-stat) — energy control and output control develop together.

### 5.8 — CORE SENSITIVITY
*The Core's perception of external energy and dimensional activity.*

The outward-sensing extension of the Core — its ability to detect other energy signatures, sense dungeon energy density, read dimensional distortions, and perceive the energy environment around the Corebearer. Core Sensitivity is Energy's contribution to Perception: where Perception's Energy Sensitivity is the brain processing sensory input, Core Sensitivity is the Core generating that sensory input.

High Core Sensitivity means the Corebearer receives detailed information about their energy environment passively. They sense when another Core is active nearby. They feel the edge of a dungeon's energy field before they can see it. They detect the dimensional static that precedes a rift formation.

Core Sensitivity directly amplifies Energy Sensitivity (Perception sub-stat) — the Perception sub-stat's ceiling is partially determined by Core Sensitivity.

# CROSS-STAT SYSTEM — COMPLETE REFERENCE

## The Logic Behind It

Vitals do not develop in isolation. The body and Core are a single system. Gains in one area structurally affect others — not as a game mechanic, but because this is how biology and physics work. Dense bones transmit force better. Faster physical movement trains faster neural response. Controlled energy use develops precise muscle expression.

The cross-stat system reflects these real relationships.

## Why These Numbers

The percentages are not arbitrary. They reflect the actual strength of the biological/physical relationship:

**5% (strongest cross-stat links):**
- Vitality Structural Density → Power Raw Force: dense bones transmit force directly
- Energy Regeneration Rate → Vitality: energy is the literal fuel source for repair
- Energy pool improvements from Vitality: structural density is the housing

**3–4% (significant links):**
- Perception Threat Detection → Speed Reaction Time: these are mechanistically the same process at different steps
- Energy Output → Power techniques: direct fuel-to-output relationship
- Energy Regen ← Vitality Stamina: endurance and energy recovery share biological substrate

**2% (real but indirect links):**
- Speed → Perception cross-stats: fast movement builds threat maps, but indirectly
- Power Control → Energy Precision: parallel skills that develop together
- Muscular Integrity cross-links: quality-of-movement affects multiple systems

## Imbalance Flags

The system flags the Vital score with (*) when sub-stat variance is high — when some sub-stats are significantly above or below the Vital average.

**What (*) means in practice:**

A Power (*) Corebearer might have E-rank Raw Force but only F-rank Force Control. Their scan reads Power E* — average is E, but one sub-stat is dramatically out of proportion. Experienced Corebearers and instructors know to look for the asterisk. It tells them more about a fighter than the letter grade does.

**Marc's situation:**

Marc's Vitals show imbalance flags on almost every stat because his development was suppressed for years and then suddenly and violently unlocked. His Vitality reads D++ because the World Core Shard has been passively hardening his structure since childhood — specifically Structural Density and Physical Resistance are dramatically above what his other sub-stats support.

His Power reads E because the heart-devouring transferred high-density essence in a single event, front-loading Energy Output Force and Strike Force without developing the foundational sub-stats underneath them.

His Speed and Perception are lower because those sub-stats require active development, training, and real combat exposure. The World Core Shard couldn't passively advance them.

The result is a scan that no ICA assessor has a template for — a Dormant Coreborn showing sub-stat profiles that don't correspond to any standard development pattern. The asterisks are everywhere.

Sub-stats are not visible on the standard interface. The Vital score is the only thing displayed. This is deliberate — full sub-stat breakdowns require an ICA assessment scan, which costs AP at academies and standard ICA fees outside them.

A Corebearer can feel the difference between their sub-stats through experience — they know their Evasion is better than their Attack Acceleration, even if the Speed score doesn't distinguish between them. But seeing the exact numbers requires external assessment.

The Devour System shows Marc more than this. His interface includes full sub-stat breakdowns by default. He does not yet know this is unusual. He does not yet know that no standard system shows this level of detail.
