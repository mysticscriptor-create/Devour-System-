### Ability Evolution System (Adaptive Manifestation)

Every ability evolves through 1–3 axes:

| Axis | Description | When |
|------|-------------|------|
| **Reinforcement** | Stronger / faster / cheaper at what it already does | Always |
| **Expansion** | Gains new applications, conditions, functions | Common mid-rank |
| **Transformation** | Becomes something fundamentally different | Rare — high rank only |

**Evolution behavior types:**

| Type | Pattern | Common In |
|------|---------|-----------|
| **Linear** | Reinforcement-heavy, predictable | Most physical abilities |
| **Modular** | Expansion-heavy, gains new tools | Utility / tactical |
| **Ascendant** | Transformation-heavy, identity-shifting | Bloodlines / rare abilities |
| **Adaptive** | Context-reactive — evolves based on style, environment, decisions | Region-shaped abilities |

> All four zones are **refugees from collapsed worlds**, not invaders. They are here because their origin realms are gone or dying. This does not make them friendly. It makes them calculated.

**ABYSSAL THRONEDOM** — *First Fracture | Pacific Ocean*
- Vast whirlpool-ringed ocean zone. Border: the Pressure Wall — their own construction, not natural. Ships implode approaching it without clearance.
- **Truth:** The Nagarae stabilized the First Fracture from inside *before* humans understood what happened. Humanity has been protected by them for 800 years while calling them an infested zone.
- **Secret treaty (500 years old):** Nagarae protect Earth's oceans from dimensional collapse. Humanity supplies dimensional crystals for origin-realm communication. Four people on Earth know the full terms.
- **Why they haven't invaded:** Their world is gone. This ocean is the only stable place they have. Destroying Earth destroys themselves.
- **Ruling factions:** Abyssal Nagarae (golden-eyed serpentine royals — law, diplomacy, ancient), Leviathan Ancients (colossal semi-sentient beings — their full activation destabilizes the fracture, which keeps everyone peaceful), Thalassar Deep Elves (bioluminescent — administration, scholarship, deep hatred for Jade Hegemony's High Sylvans)
- **Sub-species:** Bathyshell Colossi (city-sized crustaceans — living infrastructure), Siren Clades (biological sonic weapons — restricted by treaty, used as intelligence operatives)
- **4th Fracture:** Searching for the cause. Have not explained it. Still calculating.

---

**JADE HEGEMONY** — *Second Fracture | South-Center continent, larger than any human region*
- World-tree ecology — completely alien evolutionary lineage. Border: the Living Ring — migrating megaflora belt that rearranges the border daily. Cannot be mapped.
- **Truth:** The Hegemony is not the aggressive power the ICA presents it as. The majority ruling circle wants internal peace. One rogue High Sylvan noble house is causing border trouble in Vexton Nexus and Aethelgard Reach *without full Mandala authorization*. The ruling circle knows. They haven't stopped it because internal conflict violates their governing structure.
- **Attitude:** High Sylvans view planets as crops to cultivate, not consume. They're frustrated by Earth being "mismanaged." This is not benign — just a different kind of dangerous.
- **Ruling factions:** High Sylvans (tall, silver-haired "garden gods" — cultivation ideology, extreme patience), Worldroot Dwarves (fused with planetary root systems — underground logistics, bio-metal forging), Verdant Titans (living mountains — region stabilizers, killing one collapses a biome, giving every faction survival reason to protect them), Mycelial Synod (distributed intelligence across entire territory — decay, rebirth, neutral arbiters), Skybound Aviora (four-winged aerial aristocracy — weather control, mercenary loyalty, have sold info to humans 3 times)
- **4th Fracture:** Have no adequate explanation. Still searching.
- **Stays to themselves** — isolationist by structure. The rogue noble house is the exception, not the rule.

**IRON-SANDS SULTANATE** — *Third Fracture | Equatorial Scorchbelt*
- Ash-and-fire ecology. Sky: permanent particulate twilight (Ash Firmament). Border: the Cinder Cloud — intelligent particulate atmosphere that resists pressure. Aircraft cannot fly through it.

- **Ruling factions:** Rakshasa Clans (horned, rune-scarred, crimson — gain power by consuming Cores, respect only demonstrated strength), Ashen Efreet (semi-incorporeal fire entities bound to obsidian cores — cannot be killed without destroying their core, ancient and manipulative), Umbral Sun-Elves (fire-scarred Sylvan descendants — elite tacticians, ICA Intelligence considers them the most dangerous military planners in any zone, mutual hatred with Jade Hegemony's High Sylvans)
- **Sub-species:** Behemoth Carriers (mountain-scale creatures with cities on their backs — contract-bound loyalty), Scorpion Lamiar (venom-tail Lamia — primary intelligence network against human territories)
- **4th Fracture:** Searching for the cause. Hesitating from large-scale action because of it.

**GLACIAL HIVE** — *Fourth Fracture | Far north, above Argent-Palar | Status: BOUNDED*
- Arctic territory. The fracture was sealed 19 years ago — entities already inside are still there but cannot expand further and cannot leave through the now-sealed fracture.

## CREATION RULES

1. **Rank = ability scope.** Never write a character acting beyond their rank's physical limits.
2. **EU capacity** must match the rank. Never exceed it.
3. **Core Potential** is fixed at awakening. Never inflate it without strong narrative justification.
4. **Substance types** cannot generate from nothing — always reshaping what exists. Always environment-dependent.
5. **Pseudo-ranks** (E−, D−, etc.) = power exceeds rank, Core not transformed. Rare. Flag it clearly.
6. **Mixed Coremarks** = fewer than 30 documented in 650 years. Major story signal — not cosmetic flavor.
7. **Mind-types** are heavily regulated. Any compulsion ability → note ICA monitoring status.
8. **Time-types** → always under permanent ICA surveillance. Justify why this character exists freely (if they do).
9. **Void-types** → ICA Tier 1 threat. Treat accordingly.
10. **Will Projection** = C-rank+ only. Force-type expression. Concentrated intent becomes directional physical pressure.
11. **Every ability must have a limitation.** No exceptions. Limitations prevent god-moding.
12. **Region shapes ability evolution.** Use the regional tendency table — do not ignore it.
13. **Breakthrough events leave permanent marks.** Every character above F shows physical or psychological evidence of what they survived.
14. **The ICA watches everything.** Any character above D-rank has a file. B-rank and above — governments maintain personal files on them.
15. **Foreign zone characters** have fundamentally different worldviews. Less trust in human institutions. Abilities shaped by origin world's dimensional laws.
